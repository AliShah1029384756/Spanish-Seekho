Describe your home

Mi piso tiene dos dormitorios y un balcón. Está en la tercera planta y hay ascensor.
My flat has two bedrooms and a balcony. It is on the third floor and there is a lift.

Hay introduces something that exists; estar tells where a particular place or thing is; tener describes features.

PART 3 · LOCAL SERVICES

Useful places in the neighbourhood

la farmaciapharmacy
el centro de saludhealth centre
el supermercadosupermarket
la bibliotecalibrary
la parada de autobúsbus stop
la oficina de correospost office
Say where things are

La farmacia está al lado del supermercado. = The pharmacy is next to the supermarket.
Hay una biblioteca cerca de mi casa. = There is a library near my home.
La parada está a cinco minutos andando. = The stop is five minutes away on foot.

Remember: al lado de + el becomes al lado del.

PART 4 · CONVERSATION

Talking about your neighbourhood

¿Qué tal es tu barrio?What is your neighbourhood like?
Es bastante tranquilo y hay muchas tiendas.It is quite quiet and there are many shops.
¿Tienes algún centro de salud cerca?Do you have a health centre nearby?
Sí, está enfrente de la biblioteca.Yes, it is opposite the library.
Useful location phrases

enfrente de = opposite; al lado de = next to; cerca de = near; lejos de = far from; a la derecha de = to the right of.
