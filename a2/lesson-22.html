PART 2 · ACTIONS

What do you need to do?

pedir una citato request an appointment
rellenar un formularioto fill in a form
presentar documentosto submit documents
entregar una solicitudto hand in an application
empadronarseto register on the municipal population register
Useful context

El empadronamiento is registration on a municipality's population register. Local procedures and required documents can vary, so check the relevant town hall's current instructions.

PART 3 · POLITE QUESTIONS

Ask clearly and politely

¿Necesito cita previa?Do I need an appointment?
¿Qué documentos tengo que traer?Which documents do I have to bring?
¿Dónde puedo presentar este formulario?Where can I submit this form?
¿Me puede indicar dónde está la ventanilla?Could you tell me where the service counter is?
¿Podría darme un justificante, por favor?Could you give me a confirmation document, please?
Polite forms

¿Me puede…? and ¿Podría…? are useful when addressing staff formally with usted. Keep the verb form consistent and add por favor when appropriate.

PART 4 · MODEL CONVERSATION

At the town hall

—Buenos días. Quiero informarme sobre el empadronamiento.Good morning. I would like information about municipal registration.
—¿Tiene cita previa?Do you have an appointment?
—Todavía no. ¿Cómo puedo pedir una?Not yet. How can I request one?
—Puede pedirla por internet o consultar en esta oficina.You can request it online or ask at this office.
—Muchas gracias. ¿Dónde puedo ver los documentos necesarios?Thank you. Where can I see the required documents?
Important

This is a language-learning example, not legal or administrative advice. Appointment systems, eligibility and document lists differ by municipality and service. Always check the current official instructions for the place where you need the service.

PRACTICAL TASK

Ab khud practise karein

Speaking and writing

Role-play a visit to a public office. Explain what you need, ask whether an appointment is required, ask which documents to bring, and request a confirmation document. Use polite usted forms.
