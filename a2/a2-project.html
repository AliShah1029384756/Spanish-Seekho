CEFR A2 · END-OF-LEVEL MODULE 26

Plan a short trip in Spain.

Is project mein aap 2–3 din ka short trip plan karenge: transport, accommodation, activities, food, directions aur aik unexpected problem. Spanish ko real-life planning ke liye use karein.

Practical project5 connected tasksSpeaking + writing
PROJECT BRIEF

Your situation

Aap Málaga se Granada ya kisi doosray Spanish shehar ka short trip plan kar rahe hain. Dates, budget aur activities apni marzi se choose karein. Har task mein apni Spanish likhein; model phrases ko copy karne ke bajaye adapt karein.

Project goal: Aik understandable plan banana, information poochna, arrangements karna aur simple problem ka solution explain karna. Website aapki writing/speaking ko automatically grade nahi karti.
TASK 1 · DESTINATION & TRANSPORT

Choose where and when to go

Destination, travel day/time aur transport choose karein. Departure aur arrival ki basic information Spanish mein likhein.

Useful language

Quiero viajar a Granada el sábado. El tren sale a las nueve y media y llega a las once.

I want to travel to Granada on Saturday. The train leaves at 9:30 and arrives at 11.

TASK 2 · ACCOMMODATION

Book a place to stay

Hotel/hostel ya apartment ke bare mein poochhein: availability, price, room aur location.

Useful language

¿Tiene una habitación para dos noches? ¿Cuánto cuesta? ¿Está cerca del centro?

Do you have a room for two nights? How much does it cost? Is it near the centre?

TASK 3 · DAILY ITINERARY

Plan two days

Do din ke liye 3–4 activities arrange karein. Sequence connectors aur timings use karein.

Useful language

Primero visitamos el centro. Después comemos cerca de la catedral. Por la tarde vamos al museo.

First we visit the centre. Afterwards we eat near the cathedral. In the afternoon we go to the museum.

TASK 4 · FOOD & LOCAL SERVICES

Manage everyday needs

Restaurant mein order dein aur kisi local service/place ke bare mein aik question poochhein.

Useful language

Quisiera una mesa para dos, por favor. ¿Dónde está la farmacia más cercana?

I would like a table for two, please. Where is the nearest pharmacy?

TASK 5 · HANDLE A PROBLEM

Explain and solve a travel problem

Imagine karein train late hai ya reservation mein problem hai. Problem explain karein, follow-up question poochhein aur alternative suggest karein.

Useful language

Mi tren tiene retraso. ¿A qué hora sale el siguiente? Por eso voy a llamar al hotel para avisar.

My train is delayed. What time does the next one leave? That is why I am going to call the hotel to let them know.

PROJECT REFLECTION

Review your trip plan

  • My dates, transport and accommodation details are understandable.
  • My itinerary uses sequence words such as primero, después and por la tarde.
  • I can ask for information politely and explain a simple problem.

How completion works

Completion is based on your own task checklist and written drafts. Website aapki pronunciation ya speaking ko automatically grade nahi karti. Yeh practical project hai, official assessment nahi.
