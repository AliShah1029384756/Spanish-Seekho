Past-time words

Yaad rakhein

Ayer estudié. = I studied yesterday. Yeh completed past action hai. A2 mein hum past tense ko step by step seekhenge.

PART 2 · REGULAR -AR VERBS

Hablar → hablé

Regular -ar verbs ke endings: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron. Spain mein vosotros form aam hai; Latin America mein aam tor par ustedes use hota hai.

habléI spoke / talkedRoman Urdu: a-BLEاردو آواز: اَبلے
hablasteyou spoke (informal)Roman Urdu: a-BLAS-teاردو آواز: اَبلَستے
hablóhe / she spoke; you spoke (formal)Roman Urdu: a-BLOاردو آواز: اَبلو
hablamoswe spokeRoman Urdu: a-BLA-mosاردو آواز: اَبلَاموس
hablaronthey spoke / you all spokeRoman Urdu: a-BLA-ronاردو آواز: اَبلارون
Examples

Ayer hablé con mi amigo. = I spoke with my friend yesterday.
Anoche trabajamos. = We worked last night.

One form to notice

Hablamos is written the same in the present and the pretérito indefinido: it can mean “we speak” or “we spoke”. The time expression and context make the meaning clear: Hoy hablamos (today we speak) / Ayer hablamos (yesterday we spoke).

PART 3 · REGULAR -ER AND -IR VERBS

Comer → comí · Vivir → viví

Regular -er aur -ir verbs ke past endings same hote hain: -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron.

comíI ateRoman Urdu: ko-MEEاردو آواز: کومِی
comisteyou ate (informal)Roman Urdu: ko-MIS-teاردو آواز: کومِستے
comióhe / she ate; you ate (formal)Roman Urdu: ko-MYOاردو آواز: کومیو
vivíI livedRoman Urdu: bee-BEEاردو آواز: بِبی
vivieronthey livedRoman Urdu: bee-BYE-ronاردو آواز: بِبییرون
Compare

Ayer comí paella. = I ate paella yesterday.
Viví en Madrid. = I lived in Madrid.

PART 4 · BUILD A SENTENCE

Subject + past verb + extra information

Estudié español ayer.I studied Spanish yesterday.Roman Urdu: es-too-DYE es-pa-NYOL a-YERاردو آواز: اِستودیے اِسپانیول اَیَر
Comimos en casa.We ate at home.Roman Urdu: ko-MEE-mos en KA-saاردو آواز: کومیموس این کاسا
Trabajé por la mañana.I worked in the morning.Roman Urdu: tra-ba-HE por la ma-NYA-naاردو آواز: تراباخے پور لا مَنیانا
Important pronunciation note

Past-tense accent marks matter: hable and hablé are not the same form. In Spain, j in trabajé is a strong breathy sound. Roman Urdu spellings are only approximations.

PART 5 · MINI CONVERSATION

What did you do yesterday?

A:

¿Qué hiciste ayer?

What did you do yesterday?
B:

Estudié español y trabajé.

I studied Spanish and worked.
A:

¿Comiste en casa?

Did you eat at home?
B:

Sí, comí con mi familia.

Yes, I ate with my family.

Conversation mein hiciste aur comiste ka use hai. Comiste regular hai; hiciste verb hacer ka irregular past form hai. Neeche kuch common irregular forms ka short preview hai; is lesson ka main focus regular verbs hi hain.

PART 6 · A FEW COMMON IRREGULAR VERBS

Hacer, ir & tener

Har past verb regular pattern follow nahi karta. Ye teen bohat common verbs hain. In ki forms yaad karna useful hai; full irregular-verb system is starter lesson ka focus nahi.

Hacer → hice (to do / make)

hice (I did/made) · hiciste (you did) · hizo (he/she did) · hicimos (we did) · hicisteis (you all did, Spain) · hicieron (they did)

Ir / Ser → fui (to go / to be)

Dono verbs ke pretérito forms same hain; meaning sentence ke context se samajh aata hai: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron.

Tener → tuve (to have)

tuve, tuviste, tuvo, tuvimos, tuvisteis, tuvieron.

Ayer hice los deberes.Yesterday I did my homework.Roman Urdu: a-YER EE-the los de-BE-resاردو آواز: اییر اِیثے لوس دےبیرےس
Fui al centro.I went to the town centre.Roman Urdu: FWEE al THEN-troاردو آواز: فوی آل ثینترو
Tuve una reunión.I had a meeting.Roman Urdu: TOO-be OO-na re-oo-NYONاردو آواز: تووے اونا ریونیون
Spain-specific form

Hicisteis, fuisteis aur tuvisteis Spain mein informal plural vosotros ke liye hain. Latin America mein aam tor par ustedes use hota hai: hicieron, fueron, tuvieron.
