A2 grammar & communication review

Review past tenses, present perfect, plans, advice and comparisons

PART 1 · PAST EVENT OR PAST HABIT

Past event or past habit

Ayer fui al médico.Yesterday I went to the doctor.
Cuando era niño, vivía en Lahore.When I was a child, I lived in Lahore.
Estudiaba cuando me llamaste.I was studying when you called me.
El sábado pasado trabajé.I worked last Saturday.
PART 2 · PRESENT PERFECT

Present perfect

Hoy he estudiado mucho.Today I have studied a lot.
¿Has visto esta película?Have you seen this film?
Todavía no hemos comido.We have not eaten yet.
PART 3 · PLANS AND OBLIGATIONS

Plans and obligations

Voy a buscar trabajo.I am going to look for work.
Tienes que pedir cita.You have to make an appointment.
Deberías practicar cada día.You should practise every day.
Hay que respetar las normas.Rules must be respected.
PART 4 · COMPARISONS AND REASONS

Comparisons and reasons

Este barrio es más tranquilo que aquel.This neighbourhood is quieter than that one.
Prefiero el tren porque es cómodo.I prefer the train because it is comfortable.
No es tan caro como parece.It is not as expensive as it seems.
PART 5 · COMMUNICATION

Communication

¿Podría ayudarme?Could you help me?
No lo he entendido. ¿Puede repetirlo?I have not understood it. Can you repeat it?
En mi opinión, es una buena idea.In my opinion, it is a good idea.
