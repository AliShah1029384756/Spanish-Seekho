PART 1 · THE FORMULA

haber + past participle

Present perfect = conjugated haber + past participle. Spain mein ye form recent past aur life experiences dono ke liye use hota hai.

heI haveRoman Urdu: eاردو آواز: اے
hasyou have (informal)Roman Urdu: as
hahe / she has; you have (formal)Roman Urdu: a
hemoswe haveRoman Urdu: E-mos
habéisyou all have (Spain)Roman Urdu: a-BEYS
hanthey have / you all haveRoman Urdu: an
Past participle (regular)

-AR verbs → -ado (hablar → hablado)
-ER / -IR verbs → -ido (comer → comido, vivir → vivido)

PART 3 · TIME EXPRESSIONS

When we use present perfect in Spain

In Spain, the present perfect is common with time periods that are still open, such as hoy and esta semana, and when talking about life experiences. Usage varies by region and speaker: many Latin American varieties favour the pretérito indefinido in some of these contexts, but neither Spain nor Latin America follows one single rule.

hoytodayRoman Urdu: oy
esta semana / este mesthis week / this monthRoman Urdu: ES-ta se-MA-na / ES-te mes
alguna vezever / at some timeRoman Urdu: al-GOO-na beth
nunca / jamásneverRoman Urdu: NOON-ka / kha-MAS
ya / todavía noalready / not yetRoman Urdu: ya / to-da-BEE-a no
Spain tip

¿Has comido ya? = Have you eaten yet? (very natural in Spain)
Todavía no he terminado. = I haven’t finished yet.

Examples with irregulars

He visto esa serie. = I have seen that series.
¿Has hecho los deberes? = Have you done the homework?
Todavía no he escrito el email. = I still haven’t written the email.

PART 5 · MINI CONVERSATION

Have you ever been to Madrid?

PERSONA A

¿Has estado alguna vez en Madrid?

Have you ever been to Madrid?
PERSONA B

Sí, he estado dos veces. ¿Y tú?

Yes, I have been there twice. And you?
PERSONA A

Todavía no. Pero he visto muchas fotos.

Not yet. But I have seen many photos.
PERSONA B

¿Has comido ya?

Have you eaten yet?
PERSONA A

Sí, he comido en casa.

Yes, I have eaten at home.

Tip: alguna vez = ever. Todavía no = not yet. These pairs are very natural with the present perfect.
