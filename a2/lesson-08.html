¿Te apetece tomar un café mañana?Would you fancy having a coffee tomorrow?Roman Urdu: te a-pe-TE-the to-MAR oon ka-FE ma-NYA-naاردو آواز: تے اپےتےثے توما‌ر اون کافی مانیانا
Me duele la cabeza desde ayer.I've had a headache since yesterday.Roman Urdu: me DWE-le la ka-BE-tha DES-de a-YERاردو آواز: مے دوئےلے لا کابےثا دیسدے اییر
Past tense reminder

Ayer trabajé presents a completed event in a finished past time. Cuando era pequeño/a, jugaba… describes a past habit or background. Words such as ayer often point to a completed event, while cuando era… often introduces background; these are useful clues, not automatic rules for every sentence.

Roman Urdu aur Urdu-script pronunciation sirf qareebi guide hain, exact phonetic transcription nahi. Spain ke standard Castilian pronunciation ko target kiya gaya hai; regional accents mein farq ho sakta hai.

PART 2 · CAN-DO CHECKLIST

Can you do these things?

Har statement par sochain. Jo cheez abhi mushkil ho us lesson ko dobara revise karein.

  • ☐ Main kal ya pichlay haftay ke completed actions ke bare mein simple jumlay bana sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main past habits aur background describe karne ke liye imperfecto ke basic patterns pehchan sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main apni studies, job aur basic responsibilities explain kar sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main hotel reservation, price aur basic accommodation requests kar sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main simple opinions, likes/dislikes aur near-future plans express kar sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main invitation accept ya politely decline karke doosra plan suggest kar sakta/sakti hun.
  • ☐ Main appointment, basic symptoms aur pharmacy mein madad ke liye simple sawal kar sakta/sakti hun.
