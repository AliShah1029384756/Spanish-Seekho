Connectors & sequencing events

Link ideas, explain reasons and put everyday events in order

A2 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · ORDER OF EVENTS

Put events in sequence

primerofirst
despuésafterwards
luegothen
más tardelater
al final / por últimoin the end / lastly
Example

Primero fui al supermercado. Después compré fruta y, más tarde, volví a casa. Al final preparé la cena.
First I went to the supermarket. Afterwards I bought fruit and later I returned home. In the end I prepared dinner.

PART 2 · GIVE REASONS AND RESULTS

Because, so and then

porquebecause (gives a reason)
por esothat is why / therefore (gives a result)
entoncesthen / in that case
ademásalso / in addition
perobut
Compare reason and result

Llegué tarde porque había tráfico. = I arrived late because there was traffic.
Había mucho tráfico; por eso llegué tarde. = There was a lot of traffic; that is why I arrived late.

Porque introduces a reason; por eso introduces the result. They are not interchangeable in the same sentence pattern.

PART 3 · BUILD A CONNECTED STORY

From separate sentences to a short account

Short story

Ayer tenía una cita en el centro. Primero salí de casa temprano, pero el autobús no llegó. Entonces llamé a la oficina para avisar. Después fui en metro y llegué veinte minutos tarde. Por eso tuve que cambiar la hora de mi cita.
Yesterday I had an appointment in the centre. First I left home early, but the bus did not arrive. Then I called the office to let them know. Afterwards I went by metro and arrived twenty minutes late. That is why I had to change my appointment time.

How the links work

Primero / después / entonces organise events. Pero adds contrast. Por eso shows a result. Además adds another point.

PART 4 · CONVERSATION

Explain a day in order

¿Qué hiciste ayer?What did you do yesterday?
Primero fui a clase y después estudié en la biblioteca.First I went to class and then I studied in the library.
¿Y por qué volviste temprano?And why did you return early?
Porque tenía que trabajar. Por eso no me quedé.Because I had to work. That is why I did not stay.
PRACTICAL TASK

Ab khud practise karein

Speaking and writing

Write a short account of yesterday or a recent trip in 6–8 sentences. Use at least three sequence connectors, one reason with porque, one result with por eso, and one contrast with pero.
