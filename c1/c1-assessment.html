Integrated C1 assessment

A structured, self-administered assessment across reading, listening, writing, speaking and mediation.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Interpret demanding written and spoken material and evaluate the speaker's or author's position.
  • Produce a coherent, well-supported and appropriately qualified written response.
  • Demonstrate fluent spoken argument, interaction and mediation with audience awareness.
PART 1 · C1 CAPSTONE

Assessment conditions and integrity

Choose a quiet setting, complete tasks without translation tools during the attempt, and record time and conditions. This is a learning assessment, not an accredited exam.

Voy a realizar la tarea sin consultar traducciones ni modelos de respuesta.I am going to complete the task without consulting translations or model answers.
Anotaré las condiciones para poder comparar los resultados en otra ocasión.I will record the conditions so that I can compare the results on another occasion.
PART 2 · C1 CAPSTONE

Reading and source analysis

Read two contrasting texts on one issue. Identify each central claim, supporting evidence, assumptions, tone and one limitation.

El primer texto atribuye la mejora a una serie de cambios organizativos.The first text attributes the improvement to a series of organisational changes.
El segundo cuestiona que exista una relación causal demostrada.The second questions whether a causal relationship has been demonstrated.
PART 3 · C1 CAPSTONE

Listening and inference

Listen to an extended interview, lecture or discussion. Note the main position, supporting reasons, shifts in stance and implied meaning; replay only according to your chosen assessment conditions.

La ponente introduce una reserva antes de presentar su conclusión.The speaker introduces a reservation before presenting her conclusion.
El cambio de tono sugiere que la cuestión sigue abierta.The change in tone suggests that the issue remains open.
PART 4 · C1 CAPSTONE

Extended writing

Write a 250–300-word synthesis or proposal. Attribute sources, organise paragraphs, use precise connectors and qualify conclusions proportionately.

En conjunto, los datos respaldan una interpretación prudente de los resultados.Overall, the data support a cautious interpretation of the results.
No obstante, la ausencia de seguimiento a largo plazo limita el alcance de la conclusión.Nevertheless, the absence of long-term follow-up limits the scope of the conclusion.
PART 5 · C1 CAPSTONE

Speaking and interaction

Give a 3–4-minute structured response, then answer follow-up questions. Assess clarity, fluency, precision, repair strategies and ability to respond rather than recite.

Permíteme matizar ese punto antes de responder a la pregunta.Allow me to qualify that point before answering the question.
Es una observación pertinente; añadiría, sin embargo, una segunda consideración.That is a relevant observation; I would, however, add a second consideration.
PART 6 · C1 CAPSTONE

Mediation and self-scoring

Explain the key points of a complex source to a different audience, then score each skill separately. Record evidence and uncertainty; do not convert this into an official CEFR result.

La idea principal, expresada de forma sencilla, es que los beneficios dependen del contexto.The main idea, expressed simply, is that the benefits depend on the context.
Mi valoración es provisional y necesitaría contrastarla con una evaluación externa.My assessment is provisional and I would need to compare it with an external evaluation.
CAPSTONE TASK

Apply your learning

Complete a timed self-assessment: read two contrasting texts (30 minutes), listen to a 5–8-minute Spanish interview or lecture (twice at most), write a 250–300-word synthesis (40 minutes), record a 3–4-minute spoken response, and mediate the main findings for a non-specialist. Save the source links and your own outputs for review.
