CEFR C1 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 21

Chairing and facilitating complex discussion

Lead demanding discussions, balance contributions, clarify positions and move a group towards reasoned decisions.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Open, structure and close a complex discussion with clear signposting.
  • Invite quieter participants, manage interruption and summarise competing positions fairly.
  • Move disagreement towards options, decisions and agreed next steps.
PART 1 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Set a shared purpose

A discussion leader states the question, desired outcome and boundaries. Hoy nos centraremos en… and El objetivo de esta reunión es… keep participants oriented.

El objetivo de esta reunión es acordar prioridades para el próximo trimestre.The aim of this meeting is to agree priorities for the next quarter.
Hoy nos centraremos en las medidas que pueden aplicarse a corto plazo.Today we will focus on measures that can be implemented in the short term.
PART 2 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Distribute the floor

Invite contributions without putting people under pressure. ¿Qué opinión te merece…? and Me gustaría escuchar también… make participation more balanced.

Me gustaría escuchar también la perspectiva del equipo de atención al cliente.I would also like to hear the customer support team's perspective.
¿Qué opinión te merece la propuesta desde el punto de vista técnico?What is your view of the proposal from a technical perspective?
PART 3 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Interrupt and redirect tactfully

Use brief, respectful interventions to protect time and relevance. Si te parece, retomamos… and Permíteme volver al punto… redirect without dismissing a speaker.

Si te parece, retomamos esa cuestión al final para no perder el hilo.If you agree, we will return to that issue at the end so we do not lose the thread.
Permíteme volver al punto que planteó Marta sobre los plazos.Let me return to the point Marta raised about the deadlines.
PART 4 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Clarify and test understanding

Paraphrase a contribution and check whether the interpretation is accurate. Si no te he entendido mal… invites correction.

Si no te he entendido mal, la objeción principal no es el coste, sino el calendario.If I have understood you correctly, the main objection is not the cost but the timetable.
¿Podrías concretar qué entiendes por una implantación gradual?Could you clarify what you mean by a gradual implementation?
PART 5 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Summarise disagreement fairly

Represent each position in terms its supporters would recognise. Por una parte…; por otra… avoids caricature.

Por una parte, se valora la rapidez; por otra, preocupa la falta de pruebas.On the one hand, speed is valued; on the other, the lack of evidence is a concern.
Ambas posturas coinciden en la necesidad de reducir los riesgos.Both positions agree on the need to reduce risks.
PART 6 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Turn discussion into decisions

Separate agreement, unresolved questions, owners and deadlines. Para cerrar, dejamos constancia de… makes outcomes actionable.

Para cerrar, dejamos constancia de tres acuerdos y dos cuestiones pendientes.To close, we will record three agreements and two outstanding questions.
La próxima revisión tendrá lugar el jueves y cada equipo presentará sus avances.The next review will take place on Thursday and each team will present its progress.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Prepare a 5-minute chair's script for a meeting with three competing priorities. Include an opening, two invitations to contribute, one tactful interruption, a fair summary of disagreement and a closing record of decisions.
