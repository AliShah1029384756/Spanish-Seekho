CEFR C1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 19

Impersonal, passive and distancing strategies

Control agency, responsibility and evidential distance through passive, impersonal and reporting constructions.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Choose active, passive and impersonal structures according to communicative purpose.
  • Use se constructions and reporting frames accurately.
  • Distinguish neutral distance from deliberate evasion of responsibility.
PART 1 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Agency and information focus

Active sentence agent ko foreground karti hai; passive ya impersonal construction process/result ko focus kar sakti hai. Choice ka effect reader par assess karein.

El comité aprobó la propuesta por unanimidad.The committee approved the proposal unanimously.
La propuesta fue aprobada por unanimidad.The proposal was approved unanimously.
PART 2 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

The passive with ser and participle

Ser + participle formal reports mein common hai. Participle grammatical subject ke gender/number se agree karta hai; agent ko por phrase se include kar sakte hain.

Las conclusiones fueron revisadas por un equipo independiente.The conclusions were reviewed by an independent team.
El documento será publicado la próxima semana.The document will be published next week.
PART 3 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Impersonal se and passive se

Se vive bien aquí impersonal use hai; se publicaron los resultados passive se hai aur verb subject ke saath agree karta hai. Meaning context se distinguish karein.

Se trabaja con equipos internacionales.People work with international teams here.
Se publicaron los resultados ayer.The results were published yesterday.
PART 4 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Reporting and evidential distance

Según el informe, al parecer aur se ha informado de que source ya evidence ka distance mark karte hain. Attribution vague ho to claim ki strength bhi limited rakhein.

Según el informe, la demanda ha aumentado.According to the report, demand has increased.
Al parecer, las negociaciones continúan.Apparently, negotiations are continuing.
PART 5 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Responsibility should remain visible

Passive wording kabhi focus ke liye suitable hai, lekin accountability ke liye agent ko name karna zaroori ho sakta hai. Neutrality aur responsibility concealment ko confuse na karein.

La empresa comunicó el cambio a sus empleados.The company communicated the change to its employees.
El cambio fue comunicado a los empleados por la empresa.The change was communicated to employees by the company.
PART 6 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Select the construction for the purpose

Scientific method, institutional update aur incident report mein different focus useful hota hai. Sentence ko audience, evidence aur accountability ke mutabiq frame karein.

Se analizaron 240 respuestas durante el estudio.Two hundred and forty responses were analysed during the study.
El equipo analizó 240 respuestas durante el estudio.The team analysed 240 responses during the study.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek short incident/report paragraph ke 3 versions likhein: active, passive aur impersonal/se. Har version mein identify karein ke agent, evidence aur responsibility kitni visible hai. Jahan source maloom ho, attribution explicitly dein.
