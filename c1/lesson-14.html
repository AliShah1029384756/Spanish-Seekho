CEFR C1 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 14

Idioms, collocations, connotation and lexical precision

Use natural word partnerships, interpret idioms in context and select vocabulary for precise meaning and tone.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Recognise common collocations and avoid literal word combinations.
  • Interpret idioms and figurative expressions from context.
  • Choose words by connotation, register and degree of precision.
PART 1 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Why words travel in groups

Collocations woh natural word partnerships hain jo fluent Spanish mein aksar saath aati hain. Har grammatically possible combination idiomatic nahi hota.

Tomar una decisión requiere tiempo y reflexión.Making a decision requires time and reflection.
Prestar atención a los detalles evita errores.Paying attention to details prevents errors.
PART 2 · STYLE AND REGISTER

High-value verb–noun collocations

Verb ke saath natural noun yaad karein: plantear una cuestión, alcanzar un acuerdo, asumir una responsabilidad. Direct translation se unnatural combinations ban sakti hain.

El equipo alcanzó un acuerdo después de varias reuniones.The team reached an agreement after several meetings.
La investigadora planteó una cuestión fundamental.The researcher raised a fundamental question.
PART 3 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Idioms and figurative meaning

Idioms ko word-by-word translate na karein. Context aur communicative purpose se meaning samjhein; zaroorat par neutral paraphrase use karein.

Al final, dieron en el clavo con la solución.In the end, they hit the nail on the head with the solution.
No quiero andarme por las ramas: necesitamos más tiempo.I don’t want to beat around the bush: we need more time.
PART 4 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Connotation: more than dictionary meaning

Synonyms ka emotional ya evaluative effect different ho sakta hai. Describir, criticar, cuestionar aur denunciar same action ko same intensity se frame nahi karte.

El equipo cuestionó la viabilidad del proyecto.The team questioned the project’s feasibility.
La prensa denunció las condiciones abusivas.The press denounced the abusive conditions.
PART 5 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Choose precise rather than vague vocabulary

Cosas, hacer aur bueno jaise broad words kabhi useful hain, magar advanced communication mein exact noun/verb information ko sharper banata hai.

La medida redujo el consumo energético un doce por ciento.The measure reduced energy consumption by twelve per cent.
El estudio detectó una relación, pero no demostró causalidad.The study detected a relationship but did not prove causation.
PART 6 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Check collocation, context and register

New phrase ko dictionary examples aur reliable context mein check karein. Spain Spanish mein natural usage seekhein aur regional expressions ko universal label na dein.

La propuesta suscita dudas entre los especialistas.The proposal raises doubts among specialists.
Conviene valorar las consecuencias a largo plazo.It is advisable to assess the long-term consequences.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Apne field ya interest par 10 collocations collect karein. Har ek ke saath original sentence likhein, phir 3 idioms ko context mein explain karein aur 3 vague words ko precise alternatives se replace karein.
