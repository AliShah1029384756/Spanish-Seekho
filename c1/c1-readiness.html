CEFR C1 · CLOSING MODULE 28 · READINESS

C2 readiness and next-level learning plan

Reflect on C1 performance, identify the capabilities needed for further development and build a realistic transition plan.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Evaluate C1 strengths and gaps across all communicative activities.
  • Understand the broader demands of C2 without treating readiness as automatic progression.
  • Create a personalised, evidence-based next-stage learning plan.
PART 1 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review C1 evidence

Gather your review audit, practical project, integrated assessment and examples of revised work. Look for consistency across tasks, not a single strong performance.

A lo largo de varias tareas he demostrado mayor precisión al matizar mis argumentos.Across several tasks I have demonstrated greater precision in qualifying my arguments.
Todavía necesito mejorar la rapidez con la que sintetizo información compleja.I still need to improve the speed with which I synthesise complex information.
PART 2 · C1 CAPSTONE

Check breadth and flexibility

Consider whether you can adapt to unfamiliar subjects, varied registers, implicit meaning and demanding interaction without relying on memorised scripts.

Puedo adaptar mi explicación cuando el interlocutor no conoce el tema.I can adapt my explanation when the interlocutor is unfamiliar with the topic.
Me cuesta mantener la precisión cuando el debate cambia de rumbo inesperadamente.I find it difficult to maintain precision when the debate changes direction unexpectedly.
PART 3 · C1 CAPSTONE

Understand the C2 challenge

C2 development involves very high flexibility, precision, nuance and effective handling of demanding material. It is not simply a longer vocabulary list or a guaranteed next step.

El objetivo no es utilizar palabras más rebuscadas, sino expresarme con mayor precisión y naturalidad.The aim is not to use more elaborate words, but to express myself with greater precision and naturalness.
La flexibilidad comunicativa exige ajustar el discurso a situaciones poco previsibles.Communicative flexibility requires adapting discourse to unpredictable situations.
PART 4 · C1 CAPSTONE

Choose focused development priorities

Select two or three priorities such as stylistic control, rapid synthesis, idiomatic precision, complex listening or spontaneous interaction.

Durante el próximo mes me centraré en la comprensión de debates especializados.During the next month I will focus on understanding specialised debates.
Registraré los errores recurrentes y comprobaré si disminuyen con la práctica.I will record recurring errors and check whether they decrease with practice.
PART 5 · C1 CAPSTONE

Build an advanced practice routine

Combine extensive reading/listening, output, feedback and spaced review. Use authentic material and note unfamiliar expressions in context.

Cada semana resumiré dos textos y compararé sus perspectivas.Each week I will summarise two texts and compare their perspectives.
Después de cada presentación, revisaré la grabación y anotaré tres mejoras concretas.After each presentation, I will review the recording and note three concrete improvements.
PART 6 · C1 CAPSTONE

Set a readiness checkpoint

Choose a future date and repeat comparable tasks. Seek qualified feedback before making a formal proficiency claim or exam decision.

Volveré a evaluar estas destrezas dentro de ocho semanas.I will reassess these skills in eight weeks.
Antes de presentarme a un examen oficial, contrastaré mi nivel con un docente cualificado.Before taking an official exam, I will compare my level with a qualified teacher.
CAPSTONE TASK

Apply your learning

Write an 8-week learning plan with two priority skills, weekly authentic input, one speaking and one writing output each week, a feedback method, and a repeat assessment date. State what evidence would convince you that each priority has improved.
