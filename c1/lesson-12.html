CEFR C1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

Synthesising long and contrasting sources

Bring multiple demanding sources into one coherent account: group ideas by theme, preserve attribution, explain disagreement and produce a balanced synthesis.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Group ideas across sources by theme rather than summarising one source at a time.
  • Preserve attribution, agreement, disagreement and differences in evidence.
  • Produce a coherent synthesis that distinguishes shared findings from unresolved questions.
PART 1 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Build a source matrix

Har source ko alag paragraph mein summarize karne ke bajaye themes ke mutabiq matrix banayein. Rows themes, columns sources; har cell mein claim aur evidence ka short note.

Ambas fuentes coinciden en que el acceso ha mejorado, aunque discrepan sobre el alcance del cambio.Both sources agree that access has improved, although they disagree about the extent of the change.
La primera destaca los resultados locales; la segunda examina las diferencias entre regiones.The first highlights local results; the second examines differences between regions.
PART 2 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Attribute ideas precisely

Reader ko clear hona chahiye kis source ne kya kaha. Attribution verbs stance dikhate hain: sostiene, advierte, cuestiona, documenta, matiza.

El informe sostiene que la inversión ha sido insuficiente para cubrir la demanda.The report argues that investment has been insufficient to meet demand.
La entrevista matiza esa conclusión al señalar diferencias entre municipios.The interview qualifies that conclusion by pointing to differences between municipalities.
PART 3 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Synthesis by theme, not source order

Synthesis ka structure aapke analytical themes se banta hai. Sources ko har theme ke andar compare karein; source A phir B phir C ki list na banayein.

En cuanto a la financiación, las tres fuentes identifican obstáculos distintos pero relacionados.Regarding funding, all three sources identify different but related obstacles.
Desde la perspectiva del usuario, los testimonios añaden matices que no aparecen en las estadísticas.From the user's perspective, the testimonies add nuances that do not appear in the statistics.
PART 4 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Represent disagreement without flattening it

Disagreement ko hide na karein. Dekhein sources asal mein contradict karte hain, different populations study karte hain ya different definitions use karte hain.

La aparente contradicción se debe, en parte, a que los estudios analizan periodos diferentes.The apparent contradiction is partly due to the studies examining different periods.
Mientras una fuente prioriza la eficiencia, la otra pone el acento en la equidad.While one source prioritises efficiency, the other emphasises equity.
PART 5 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Integrate evidence and limitations

Synthesis mein evidence ki quality aur limits bhi include karein. Statistics, interviews aur expert interpretation ka role alag ho sakta hai.

Los datos cuantitativos muestran la tendencia, pero no explican por sí solos sus causas.Quantitative data show the trend but do not by themselves explain its causes.
Los testimonios ilustran experiencias concretas, aunque no representan necesariamente a toda la población.Testimonies illustrate specific experiences, although they do not necessarily represent the whole population.
PART 6 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Write a coherent synthesis

Opening mein shared question aur overall pattern batayein; body themes aur contrasts ko develop kare; ending agreement, disagreement aur evidence gaps ko sum up kare.

En conjunto, las fuentes apuntan a una mejora desigual y condicionada por el contexto.Taken together, the sources point to uneven improvement shaped by context.
Queda por determinar si estas tendencias se mantendrán a medio plazo.It remains to be determined whether these trends will continue in the medium term.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Teen short source cards (apne selected topic par) use karein. 220–260 word synthesis likhein: shared theme, two areas of agreement, one disagreement, source attribution, evidence limitation aur balanced conclusion. Direct copying ke bajaye apne alfaaz use karein.
