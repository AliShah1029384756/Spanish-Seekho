CEFR C1 · CLOSING MODULE 26 · PROJECT

Practical C1 communication project

Plan, research, present and defend a realistic proposal for a complex audience and stakeholder group.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Define a real-world communication problem and its stakeholders.
  • Synthesize evidence into a nuanced proposal and adapt it for different audiences.
  • Present, negotiate and document a justified decision with clear next steps.
PART 1 · C1 CAPSTONE

Choose a meaningful challenge

Select a topic with competing priorities, such as access, sustainability, digital services or community learning. Define the audience and the decision needed.

El reto consiste en mejorar el acceso sin aumentar la brecha digital.The challenge is to improve access without widening the digital divide.
Antes de formular una propuesta, conviene delimitar el problema.Before formulating a proposal, it is worth defining the problem.
PART 2 · C1 CAPSTONE

Gather and evaluate information

Use at least two contrasting sources. Record authorship, date, method, purpose and limitations; do not invent evidence.

Según la primera fuente, la medida reduciría los tiempos de espera.According to the first source, the measure would reduce waiting times.
La segunda fuente advierte de posibles costes que no se habían tenido en cuenta.The second source warns of possible costs that had not been taken into account.
PART 3 · C1 CAPSTONE

Build a balanced proposal

Present the problem, options, evidence, trade-offs, recommendation and conditions. Attribute information clearly.

A la luz de ambas perspectivas, proponemos una aplicación gradual.In light of both perspectives, we propose a gradual implementation.
Esta recomendación dependería de una evaluación independiente al cabo de seis meses.This recommendation would depend on an independent evaluation after six months.
PART 4 · C1 CAPSTONE

Adapt the message for two audiences

Prepare a concise executive summary and a more detailed technical explanation. Keep facts consistent while changing depth and terminology.

Para la dirección, la prioridad es valorar el impacto y los recursos necesarios.For management, the priority is to assess the impact and the resources required.
Desde el punto de vista técnico, habría que comprobar la seguridad y la interoperabilidad.From a technical point of view, security and interoperability would need to be checked.
PART 5 · C1 CAPSTONE

Present and negotiate

Deliver a structured presentation, invite challenge, acknowledge a valid objection and explain what could be adjusted.

Entiendo la preocupación por el coste; por eso planteamos una fase piloto.I understand the concern about cost; that is why we propose a pilot phase.
Podríamos incorporar esa salvaguarda sin renunciar al objetivo principal.We could incorporate that safeguard without giving up the main objective.
PART 6 · C1 CAPSTONE

Record outcomes and reflect

Write minutes or a decision note with agreements, unresolved questions, owners, deadlines and review indicators.

Queda pendiente confirmar quién coordinará la evaluación externa.It remains to be confirmed who will coordinate the external evaluation.
La decisión se revisará cuando dispongamos de los primeros resultados.The decision will be reviewed when we have the first results.
CAPSTONE TASK

Apply your learning

Project deliverables: (1) a 250–350-word evidence-based proposal using at least two contrasting sources; (2) a 120-word executive summary for non-specialists; (3) a 3–5-minute oral presentation; (4) a short response to two objections; and (5) decision notes with owners, deadlines and review criteria. Use real sources if making factual claims and cite them in your own document.
