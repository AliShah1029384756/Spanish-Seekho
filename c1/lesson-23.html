CEFR C1 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 23

Resolving intercultural misunderstandings

Recognise pragmatic differences, ask non-judgemental questions, repair misunderstandings and agree on a shared interpretation.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Distinguish linguistic difficulty from differences in expectations or interactional norms.
  • Use clarification, perspective-taking and face-saving language to repair misunderstandings.
  • Confirm a shared understanding and agree practical next steps.
PART 1 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Pause before interpreting intent

A short reply, silence or direct statement may have different meanings across contexts. No quisiera sacar conclusiones precipitadas… avoids assumptions.

No quisiera sacar conclusiones precipitadas; quizá he interpretado mal el tono.I would not like to jump to conclusions; perhaps I have misread the tone.
Puede que estemos entendiendo de manera distinta lo que significa urgente.We may be understanding differently what urgent means.
PART 2 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Ask neutral clarification questions

Ask what a phrase or expectation means rather than accusing someone of being unclear. ¿Podrías aclarar a qué te refieres con…? is neutral.

¿Podrías aclarar a qué te refieres con una respuesta inmediata?Could you clarify what you mean by an immediate response?
Cuando dices que lo revisarás, ¿significa hoy o durante esta semana?When you say you will review it, do you mean today or during this week?
PART 3 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Name impact without assigning blame

Describe what happened and its practical effect using first-person framing. Mi impresión fue… and El efecto que tuvo… reduce defensiveness.

Mi impresión fue que la decisión ya estaba cerrada, aunque quizá no era esa la intención.My impression was that the decision had already been made, although perhaps that was not the intention.
El efecto que tuvo el mensaje fue generar cierta confusión sobre las responsabilidades.The effect of the message was to create some confusion about responsibilities.
PART 4 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Explore different expectations

Invite each person to explain their preferred way of giving feedback, making decisions or handling time. ¿Cómo soléis…? opens perspective.

¿Cómo soléis comunicar los cambios de plazo en vuestro equipo?How do you usually communicate deadline changes in your team?
Para nosotros, confirmar por escrito evita interpretaciones distintas.For us, confirming in writing prevents different interpretations.
PART 5 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Repair and confirm shared meaning

Offer a revised interpretation and invite correction. Entonces, si lo he entendido bien… checks alignment.

Entonces, si lo he entendido bien, no es una negativa, sino una petición de más información.So, if I have understood correctly, it is not a refusal but a request for more information.
¿Es correcto decir que acordamos revisar la propuesta el lunes?Is it correct to say that we agreed to review the proposal on Monday?
PART 6 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Agree a practical convention

Turn learning into a shared working agreement about channels, response times and decision records. Para evitar que vuelva a ocurrir… is constructive.

Para evitar que vuelva a ocurrir, confirmaremos los cambios importantes por correo.To prevent it happening again, we will confirm important changes by email.
Si surge una duda, la plantearemos antes de dar por cerrada la decisión.If a question arises, we will raise it before treating the decision as final.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Write a short mediation dialogue in which two colleagues misunderstand the phrase 'lo revisaremos cuanto antes'. Use a neutral clarification, explain impact without blame, confirm the intended meaning and agree a communication convention.
