CEFR C1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

Emphasis, focus, word order and rhetorical effect

Spanish word order aur emphasis ko rhetorical purpose ke liye control karein. Contrastive focus aur fronting ko natural context mein use karein.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Use word order to foreground contrastive or corrective information.
  • Use lo que… es… and framing structures to guide the reader.
  • Apply parallelism and emphasis without making the text theatrical.
PART 1 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Word order and information

Spanish word order flexible hai, lekin marked order information focus change karta hai. Given context ke baad new focus rakhna aksar natural hota hai.

A la reunión asistieron representantes de tres universidades.Representatives of three universities attended the meeting.
De los resultados se desprende una conclusión distinta.A different conclusion emerges from the results.
PART 2 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Corrective focus

Sino preceding element ko correct ya contrast karta hai. No…, sino… structure exact claim clarify karta hai.

No cuestionamos el objetivo, sino el procedimiento elegido.We do not question the objective, but rather the procedure chosen.
La cuestión no es cuánto cuesta, sino qué valor aporta.The question is not how much it costs, but what value it adds.
PART 3 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Lo que… es…

Lo que… es… structure main point ko focus karta hai, especially jab assumption correct karni ho.

Lo que falta no es información, sino una estrategia para interpretarla.What is missing is not information, but a strategy for interpreting it.
Lo que conviene revisar primero es la distribución de los recursos.What should be reviewed first is the distribution of resources.
PART 4 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Framing a topic

En cuanto a…, desde una perspectiva… aur en lo relativo a… discussion ka angle set karte hain. Baqi sentence ko usi frame se connect rakhein.

Desde una perspectiva económica, la medida ofrece ventajas claras.From an economic perspective, the measure offers clear advantages.
En lo relativo a la privacidad, persisten algunas dudas importantes.As far as privacy is concerned, some important doubts remain.
PART 5 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Parallelism

Parallel structures matching grammatical patterns se comparison clear banate hain. Inka purpose meaning clarify karna hai, decoration nahi.

No basta con recopilar datos; hay que interpretarlos y comunicarlos con rigor.It is not enough to collect data; they must be interpreted and communicated rigorously.
Más que aumentar la cantidad de informes, necesitamos mejorar su utilidad.Rather than increasing the number of reports, we need to improve their usefulness.
PART 6 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Rhetorical restraint

Precisamente aur lo que importa emphasis add karte hain. Har line mein emphasis text ko theatrical bana sakta hai; use purposeful rakhein.

Precisamente por esa razón, conviene evaluar los efectos a largo plazo.Precisely for that reason, long-term effects should be evaluated.
Lo esencial no es acelerar el proceso, sino garantizar su calidad.The essential point is not to speed up the process, but to guarantee its quality.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

180–220 alfaaz ki recommendation likhein jismein teen priorities hon. Lo que… es… ya no…, sino… structure, topic framing aur parallel construction purposeful use karein.
