CEFR C1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

Interpreting interviews, lectures and specialised talks

Listen strategically to extended spoken material: follow structure, distinguish stance from content, interpret emphasis and manage unfamiliar terminology.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Use signposting and discourse cues to follow long spoken explanations.
  • Distinguish a speaker's main position, supporting detail and degree of certainty.
  • Infer stance from emphasis, reformulation, hesitation and qualification.
PART 1 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Predict the structure and listen for signposts

Sunne se pehle topic aur speaker ka role dekhein. Listening ke dauran signposting phrases note karein jo sequence, contrast, example ya conclusion batati hain.

En primer lugar, conviene distinguir los efectos inmediatos de las consecuencias a largo plazo.First, it is useful to distinguish immediate effects from long-term consequences.
Llegados a este punto, quisiera volver a la cuestión inicial.At this point, I would like to return to the initial question.
PART 2 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Main point versus supporting detail

Har example central claim nahi hota. Speaker ki main position, reasons aur illustrative details alag notes mein record karein.

La idea fundamental no es sustituir el sistema, sino hacerlo más accesible.The fundamental idea is not to replace the system but to make it more accessible.
Pongo este caso como ejemplo, no como una regla general.I offer this case as an example, not as a general rule.
PART 3 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Stance, certainty and qualification

Speaker apni certainty ko modal verbs, adverbs aur conditional structures se adjust karta hai. In signals ko note karna stance samajhne mein madad deta hai.

Los resultados podrían indicar una tendencia, aunque todavía es pronto para afirmarlo.The results could indicate a trend, although it is still too early to say so.
Hasta donde sabemos, no se ha observado un efecto significativo.As far as we know, no significant effect has been observed.
PART 4 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Emphasis, reformulation and correction

Spoken language mein speaker kabhi important point repeat, rephrase ya correct karta hai. Reformulation ko extra noise na samjhein; aksar yahi key message hota hai.

Dicho de otro modo, el problema no es la falta de datos, sino su interpretación.In other words, the problem is not the lack of data but its interpretation.
Mejor dicho, deberíamos hablar de una reducción del riesgo, no de su eliminación.More precisely, we should speak of a reduction in risk, not its elimination.
PART 5 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Specialised vocabulary from context

Unknown term par ruk na jayein. Definition, contrast, example aur word family se approximate meaning infer karein; baad mein verify karein.

Por 'resiliencia urbana' entendemos la capacidad de una ciudad para recuperarse de una crisis.By 'urban resilience' we mean a city's ability to recover from a crisis.
El término se utiliza aquí en un sentido técnico, no en su uso cotidiano.The term is used here in a technical sense, not its everyday meaning.
PART 6 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Notes and post-listening synthesis

Notes ko full transcript banane ki koshish na karein. Keywords, links, speaker stance aur unresolved questions capture karke concise synthesis likhein.

En resumen, la ponente defiende una transición gradual y evaluable.In summary, the speaker supports a gradual and assessable transition.
La principal reserva se refiere al coste y a la falta de datos comparables.The main reservation concerns cost and the lack of comparable data.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Kisi 3–5 minute lecture/interview ko sun kar 8–10 bullet notes banayein: thesis, three supporting points, one qualification, one example, speaker stance aur one unresolved question. Phir 120-word neutral summary likhein.
