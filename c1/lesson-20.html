CEFR C1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 20

Reformulation, qualification and rhetorical subtlety

Refine claims, mark limitations, paraphrase accurately and use rhetorical framing without overstating evidence.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Qualify claims with precise scope, probability and limitation markers.
  • Reformulate complex information while preserving its original meaning.
  • Use rhetorical emphasis and contrast responsibly in advanced communication.
PART 1 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Qualification makes claims more accurate

En general, en cierta medida, probablemente aur en determinadas condiciones claim ki scope define karte hain. Qualification weakness nahi; evidence ke saath precision hai.

Los resultados sugieren una mejora en determinadas condiciones.The results suggest an improvement under certain conditions.
En términos generales, la medida parece viable.In general terms, the measure appears viable.
PART 2 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Probability and degree of commitment

Es probable que + subjunctive, puede que + subjunctive aur seguramente + indicative speaker ka confidence different tarah se frame karte hain. Context mein certainty calibrate karein.

Es probable que la demanda aumente durante el verano.Demand is likely to increase during the summer.
Puede que necesitemos revisar el calendario.We may need to review the timetable.
PART 3 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Limitations, exceptions and scope

Salvo que, excepto en casos en los que aur hasta cierto punto claims ki boundary show karte hain. Limitation ko clearly state karein, footnote mein hide na karein.

La estrategia funciona, salvo que cambien las condiciones iniciales.The strategy works unless the initial conditions change.
La relación se observa hasta cierto punto, pero no en todos los grupos.The relationship is observed to some extent, but not in every group.
PART 4 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Reformulate without distorting

Es decir, en otras palabras aur dicho de otro modo complex idea ko accessible banate hain. Paraphrase original source ka claim stronger ya weaker na bana de.

El estudio no demuestra causalidad; es decir, no prueba que una variable provoque la otra.The study does not demonstrate causation; that is, it does not prove that one variable causes the other.
Dicho de otro modo, los datos muestran una asociación, no una causa.In other words, the data show an association, not a cause.
PART 5 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Rhetorical contrast and emphasis

No se trata tanto de X como de Y, más que... aur lo fundamental es que... audience ko central point tak guide karte hain. Rhetoric evidence ko replace nahi karti.

No se trata tanto de reducir costes como de mejorar la calidad del servicio.It is not so much about reducing costs as improving service quality.
Lo fundamental no es la rapidez, sino la fiabilidad del resultado.What matters is not speed but the reliability of the result.
PART 6 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Edit for precision and proportion

Final edit mein vague quantifiers, absolute claims aur unsupported intensifiers identify karein. Evidence jitna support kare, claim utna hi strong hona chahiye.

La mayoría de las respuestas apunta a una tendencia, aunque la muestra es limitada.Most responses point to a trend, although the sample is limited.
Estos datos permiten plantear una hipótesis, no establecer una conclusión definitiva.These data allow us to propose a hypothesis, not establish a definitive conclusion.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 200-word claim-based paragraph likhein. Kam az kam 4 qualification markers, 2 reformulation phrases aur 1 rhetorical contrast use karein. Apne strongest claim ko evidence ke mutabiq revise karein aur explain karein ke kya limitation add ki.
