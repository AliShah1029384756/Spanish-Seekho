CEFR C1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 18

Complex clause structures and stylistic compression

Build precise subordinate structures, balance information density and compress repeated ideas without obscuring meaning.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Use relative, concessive, causal and conditional clauses accurately.
  • Reduce repetition through non-finite and participial constructions where natural.
  • Balance syntactic complexity with clarity and reader processing.
PART 1 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Build relationships between propositions

Complex clauses show cause, contrast, condition, purpose aur concession. Connector choose karte waqt logical relation pehle identify karein.

Aunque el plazo era breve, el equipo completó el análisis.Although the deadline was short, the team completed the analysis.
El equipo completó el análisis porque distribuyó bien las tareas.The team completed the analysis because it distributed tasks well.
PART 2 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Relative clauses: identify or add information

Relative clauses noun ko identify ya describe karti hain. Antecedent aur clause ka role clear rakhein; prepositions ke saath quien, el que ya el cual context ke mutabiq aate hain.

La propuesta que presentó el comité recibió varias observaciones.The proposal that the committee presented received several comments.
La investigadora con quien hablamos publicó los resultados.The researcher with whom we spoke published the results.
PART 3 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Concession and contrast with precision

A pesar de que, por más que aur si bien different rhetorical weight rakhte hain. Clause mood aur focus ko meaning ke saath align karein.

Si bien los resultados son prometedores, falta replicar el estudio.Although the results are promising, the study still needs replication.
Por más que revisemos el documento, no encontraremos ese dato.No matter how much we review the document, we will not find that detail.
PART 4 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Purpose and result structures

Purpose clauses para que + subjunctive se use karti hain jab subject change ya purpose frame ho. Result clauses tan... que / de modo que se build kar sakti hain.

Reorganizamos los datos para que el equipo pudiera compararlos.We reorganised the data so that the team could compare them.
La muestra era tan pequeña que no permitía extraer conclusiones firmes.The sample was so small that it did not allow firm conclusions.
PART 5 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Compress repeated information carefully

Nominalisation, infinitive clauses aur participial phrases repetition reduce karte hain. Compression tabhi useful hai jab agent, time ya logical relation lose na ho.

Después de revisar los datos, el equipo publicó el informe.After reviewing the data, the team published the report.
Una vez aprobada la propuesta, comenzó la fase de ejecución.Once the proposal had been approved, the implementation phase began.
PART 6 · COMPLEX GRAMMAR

Choose clarity over complexity

Long sentence automatically advanced nahi hota. Reader ko subject, main verb aur logical steps identify karne mein asani honi chahiye; zaroorat par sentence split karein.

El informe, que recoge datos de tres regiones y compara dos periodos, identifica una tendencia común.The report, which gathers data from three regions and compares two periods, identifies a common trend.
El informe compara los periodos. Después identifica una tendencia común en las tres regiones.The report compares the periods. It then identifies a common trend across the three regions.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

120–160 words ka short report likhein. Kam az kam 1 relative, 1 concessive, 1 purpose aur 1 result clause use karein. Phir 2 sentences ko compress karein aur explain karein ke meaning aur clarity kaise preserve ki.
