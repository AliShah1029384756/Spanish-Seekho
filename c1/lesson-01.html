CEFR C1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

Expressing stance, certainty, distance and nuance

Apni baat ka confidence aur evaluation precisely show karein; claim ko fact, possibility, inference ya personal judgement ke taur par frame karein.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Calibrate certainty and uncertainty without sounding evasive.
  • Distinguish evidence, source attribution, inference and personal evaluation.
  • Express distance, reservation and nuanced agreement in formal and everyday contexts.
PART 1 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Stance and commitment

C1 speaker clear karta hai ke statement fact, source-based claim, inference ya personal evaluation hai. Creo que personal view hai; parece que impression; según el informe attribution.

Según el informe, la participación aumentó, aunque no se explica el motivo.According to the report, participation increased, although the reason is not explained.
A mi juicio, la medida resulta razonable, aunque sus efectos todavía no están claros.In my view, the measure is reasonable, although its effects are not yet clear.
PART 2 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Degrees of certainty

Seguro que aur sin duda strong commitment show karte hain. Probablemente, es posible que aur todo apunta a que likelihood calibrate karte hain.

Todo apunta a que la demanda seguirá creciendo.Everything suggests that demand will continue to grow.
Es posible que la diferencia se deba a un cambio metodológico.It is possible that the difference is due to a methodological change.
PART 3 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Evidence and inference

Correlation ko causation na banayein. Los datos confirman una tendencia zyada careful hai jab evidence cause establish nahi karta.

Los datos confirman una tendencia, pero no permiten establecer una relación causal.The data confirm a trend but do not establish a causal relationship.
De estos resultados se desprende que conviene revisar el procedimiento.These results suggest that the procedure should be reviewed.
PART 4 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Distance and reservation

Parece ser, hasta donde sabemos aur no necesariamente measured distance dete hain. Inhein sirf tab use karein jab claim ki limits hon.

La propuesta parece viable, siempre que se asegure la financiación.The proposal appears viable, provided that funding is secured.
Hasta donde sabemos, no se ha publicado una evaluación independiente.As far as we know, no independent evaluation has been published.
PART 5 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Nuanced agreement

C1 disagreement binary nahi. Compartiría parte del diagnóstico, si bien… aur No cuestiono el objetivo; lo que pongo en duda es… respectful challenge dete hain.

Comparto el diagnóstico en líneas generales, si bien discrepo de la solución planteada.I broadly share the diagnosis, although I disagree with the proposed solution.
No cuestiono la necesidad de actuar; pongo en duda que esta medida sea suficiente.I do not question the need to act; I question whether this measure is sufficient.
PART 6 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Choose commitment carefully

Decide karein ke fact report kar rahe hain, source attribute, inference ya evaluation. Overstatement aur unnecessary hedging dono avoid karein.

Es posible que la reforma tenga efectos desiguales según el sector.The reform may have unequal effects depending on the sector.
La documentación disponible no permite confirmar esa interpretación.The available documentation does not allow that interpretation to be confirmed.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

180–220 alfaaz ka analytical note likhein: ek issue par position dein, do evidence-based points discuss karein, uncertainty qualify karein aur ek counterpoint acknowledge karein.
