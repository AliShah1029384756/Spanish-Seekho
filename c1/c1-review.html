Comprehensive C1 review and gap repair

Consolidate the six C1 units, diagnose recurring gaps and build a targeted revision plan.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Review precision, cohesion, register, grammar, source evaluation and mediation as connected skills.
  • Identify recurring strengths and gaps using evidence from practice tasks.
  • Create a prioritised repair plan with measurable follow-up.
PART 1 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review precision and discourse

Revisit stance, certainty, emphasis, cohesion and complex spoken explanations. Check whether your language communicates the exact strength of each claim.

Conviene matizar esta afirmación a la luz de los datos disponibles.This statement should be qualified in light of the available data.
No se trata de rechazar la propuesta, sino de precisar sus condiciones.It is not a matter of rejecting the proposal, but of specifying its conditions.
PART 2 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review critical reading and listening

Separate explicit information, inference, assumption and evaluation. Track the speaker's purpose and the evidence behind a claim.

El autor da por sentado que ambas medidas producirán el mismo efecto.The author takes for granted that both measures will produce the same effect.
La entrevistada cuestiona la interpretación de los resultados, no los datos en sí.The interviewee questions the interpretation of the results, not the data themselves.
PART 3 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review register and lexical control

Choose expressions according to audience and purpose; check collocations, connotation and regional context.

La decisión requiere una valoración más detallada de sus posibles consecuencias.The decision requires a more detailed assessment of its possible consequences.
No me queda claro si la propuesta se aplicaría de inmediato o de forma gradual.It is not clear to me whether the proposal would be applied immediately or gradually.
PART 4 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review complex grammar

Check mood, tense, clause structure, passive/impersonal choices and accurate reformulation in context.

Aunque se hayan reducido los costes, persisten algunas dudas sobre su viabilidad.Although costs may have been reduced, some doubts remain about its viability.
De haberse previsto ese escenario, se habrían adoptado otras medidas.Had that scenario been anticipated, other measures would have been adopted.
PART 5 · C1 CAPSTONE

Review interaction and mediation

Practise turn management, clarification, tactful disagreement, audience-aware explanation and shared decisions.

Si no te he entendido mal, la principal preocupación tiene que ver con los plazos.If I have understood you correctly, the main concern has to do with the deadlines.
¿Podríamos reformular el acuerdo para que resulte claro para todos los equipos?Could we reformulate the agreement so that it is clear to all teams?
PART 6 · C1 CAPSTONE

Diagnose and repair gaps

Use a learning log: note the task, evidence, recurring error, repair strategy and a date to retest. Do not treat one good score as proof of mastery.

He detectado que necesito justificar mejor las conclusiones que extraigo de varias fuentes.I have noticed that I need to justify the conclusions I draw from several sources more effectively.
Volveré a realizar la tarea dentro de una semana para comprobar si he progresado.I will do the task again in a week to check whether I have improved.
CAPSTONE TASK

Apply your learning

Create a C1 learning audit. Choose one reading/listening task, one extended writing task and one speaking/mediation task. For each, record what worked, one evidence-based gap, a repair action and a date to repeat the task. Avoid vague goals such as “learn more vocabulary”.
