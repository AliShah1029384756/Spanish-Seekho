Academic argument, critical response and discussion

Evaluate claims and evidence, identify assumptions and develop fair, nuanced counterarguments.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Distinguish a thesis, supporting reasons, evidence and assumptions.
  • Evaluate counterarguments without misrepresenting opposing positions.
  • Build a qualified academic response and participate constructively in discussion.
PART 1 · C1 PROFESSIONAL SKILL

Thesis and argument structure

Evaluate claims and evidence, identify assumptions and develop fair, nuanced counterarguments. Focus on the communicative purpose, the evidence available and the needs of the audience.

La tesis que defenderé es que la medida resulta eficaz solo en determinadas condiciones.The thesis I will defend is that the measure works only in certain conditions.
Este argumento se apoya en dos razones principales y en los datos del estudio.This argument rests on two reasons and the study data.
