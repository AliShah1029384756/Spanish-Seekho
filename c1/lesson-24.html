CEFR C1 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 24

Integrated C1 communication: analyse, synthesise and persuade

Combine critical source evaluation, nuanced argument, audience adaptation and constructive interaction in one integrated C1 task.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Evaluate and compare claims from contrasting sources with careful attribution.
  • Synthesize evidence into a coherent, qualified position.
  • Present and defend a recommendation while responding constructively to challenge.
PART 1 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Frame the question and scope

Define the issue, audience and decision required. La cuestión que nos ocupa… narrows the focus and prevents drift.

La cuestión que nos ocupa es cómo mejorar el acceso sin comprometer la privacidad.The issue before us is how to improve access without compromising privacy.
Conviene distinguir entre los beneficios inmediatos y las consecuencias a largo plazo.It is worth distinguishing immediate benefits from long-term consequences.
PART 2 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Evaluate sources and evidence

Check source expertise, date, method, sample and possible interests. Según… and los datos disponibles… support transparent attribution.

Según el informe independiente, la medida redujo los tiempos de espera.According to the independent report, the measure reduced waiting times.
La muestra es limitada, por lo que los resultados deben interpretarse con cautela.The sample is limited, so the results should be interpreted with caution.
PART 3 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Synthesize agreement and contrast

Combine shared findings while preserving differences. Ambas fuentes coinciden en…; sin embargo… maps convergence and divergence.

Ambas fuentes coinciden en la necesidad de mejorar la accesibilidad; sin embargo, discrepan sobre el coste.Both sources agree on the need to improve accessibility; however, they disagree about the cost.
En conjunto, la evidencia apunta a una mejora moderada, no a un cambio radical.Overall, the evidence points to a moderate improvement, not a radical change.
PART 4 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Build a qualified position

State a recommendation and calibrate its certainty, conditions and trade-offs. A la luz de… and siempre que… connect evidence to action.

A la luz de los datos, recomendaría una implantación gradual, siempre que se evalúen sus efectos.In light of the data, I would recommend gradual implementation, provided its effects are evaluated.
Esta opción parece viable, aunque dependerá de la financiación y del seguimiento.This option seems viable, although it will depend on funding and monitoring.
PART 5 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Respond to challenge and negotiate

Acknowledge a valid objection, answer it with evidence and identify a possible adjustment. Es una objeción pertinente… demonstrates engagement.

Es una objeción pertinente; por eso proponemos una fase piloto antes de ampliarlo.That is a valid objection; for that reason, we propose a pilot phase before expanding it.
Podríamos incorporar esa salvaguarda sin alterar el objetivo principal.We could incorporate that safeguard without changing the main objective.
PART 6 · HIGH-LEVEL INTERACTION

Conclude with action and accountability

Summarise the recommendation, conditions, indicators and review point. La propuesta quedaría condicionada a… makes responsibility explicit.

La propuesta quedaría condicionada a una revisión independiente a los seis meses.The proposal would be conditional on an independent review after six months.
El equipo publicará los resultados y revisará la decisión si aparecen efectos adversos.The team will publish the results and review the decision if adverse effects emerge.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Integrated C1 capstone: prepare a 250–300-word recommendation based on two contrasting sources (you may invent a neutral classroom scenario, not factual claims). Attribute both sources, identify one limitation, synthesize agreement and disagreement, state a qualified recommendation, answer one objection and finish with measurable next steps.
