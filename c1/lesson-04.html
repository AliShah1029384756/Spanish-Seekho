CEFR C1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

Complex spoken discourse and extended explanations

Long spoken answers ko clear, coherent aur audience-aware banayein. Signposting, reformulation aur listener feedback se complex ideas accessible banayein.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Structure extended spoken explanations with clear stages and signposting.
  • Reformulate specialised ideas in response to listener needs.
  • Use examples, transitions and synthesis to maintain coherence in longer turns.
PART 1 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Plan a long turn

Extended speaking uninterrupted speech nahi. Purpose batayein, two or three stages preview karein, phir develop aur concise synthesis dein.

Voy a dividir la explicación en tres partes: el contexto, los resultados y las posibles medidas.I will divide the explanation into three parts: context, results and possible measures.
Para empezar, conviene aclarar qué entendemos por eficacia en este caso.To begin, it is worth clarifying what we mean by effectiveness in this case.
PART 2 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Signposting

En primer lugar, a continuación, dicho esto aur llegados a este punto listener ko orientation dete hain. Pauses aur intonation se support karein.

Una vez establecido el contexto, pasemos a las consecuencias prácticas.Once the context has been established, let us move on to the practical consequences.
Llegados a este punto, hay dos cuestiones que merecen especial atención.At this point, there are two issues that deserve particular attention.
PART 3 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Explain specialised ideas

Technical term ke saath plain-language definition, example aur implication dein. Unexplained jargon se listener overload hota hai.

Por interoperabilidad me refiero a la capacidad de distintos sistemas para intercambiar información.By interoperability I mean the ability of different systems to exchange information.
En la práctica, esto significa que el usuario no tendrá que introducir los mismos datos varias veces.In practice, this means the user will not have to enter the same data several times.
PART 4 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Reformulate for clarity

Listener confused ho to same sentence louder repeat na karein. Shorter wording, example ya sequence se explain karein.

Dicho de forma más sencilla, el sistema permite que las plataformas se entiendan entre sí.Put more simply, the system allows the platforms to understand one another.
Pongamos un ejemplo concreto para ver cómo funciona en una situación real.Let us use a concrete example to see how it works in a real situation.
PART 5 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Manage questions

Question acknowledge karein, ambiguity clarify karein, answer dein aur main point se link back karein. Unavailable information honestly state karein.

Es una pregunta pertinente; antes de responder, precisaría a qué periodo se refiere.That is a relevant question; before answering, I would clarify which period you mean.
No dispongo de ese dato ahora mismo, pero puedo indicar dónde habría que comprobarlo.I do not have that figure right now, but I can indicate where it should be checked.
PART 6 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Synthesis and close

Conclusion main finding, limitation aur next step connect karti hai. Har detail repeat ya naya argument introduce na karein.

En resumen, la propuesta es viable, aunque requiere una evaluación independiente antes de aplicarse.In summary, the proposal is viable, although it requires an independent evaluation before implementation.
La idea principal, por tanto, es avanzar por fases y revisar los resultados en cada etapa.The main idea, therefore, is to proceed in stages and review the results at each stage.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

3–4 minute ki spoken presentation rehearse karein: complex topic explain karein, three stages signpost karein, specialist term define karein, example dein aur limitation + next step summarise karein.
