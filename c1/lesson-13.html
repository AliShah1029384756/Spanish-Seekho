Formal, neutral, informal and specialised registers

Choose language that fits the relationship, setting and professional field; shift register without changing the core message.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Identify the audience, purpose and degree of formality in a situation.
  • Rephrase requests and statements across formal, neutral and informal registers.
  • Recognise specialised terminology and explain it clearly when needed.
PART 1 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Read the situation before choosing words

Register sirf formal ya informal words ka naam nahi. Audience, relationship, setting aur purpose mil kar choice decide karte hain. Spain mein tú aur usted ka use context aur personal preference ke mutabiq badal sakta hai.

¿Podría indicarme dónde se encuentra el departamento de admisiones?Could you tell me where the admissions department is?
¿Me puedes decir dónde está admisiones?Can you tell me where admissions is?
PART 2 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Formal requests and professional distance

Formal request mein conditional forms aur polite framing useful hain. Usted ke saath verb third-person singular hota hai; lekin har professional situation mein usted lazmi nahi.

Le agradecería que me enviara la documentación cuando le sea posible.I would appreciate it if you sent me the documents when possible.
¿Sería tan amable de confirmar la recepción?Would you be so kind as to confirm receipt?
PART 3 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Neutral register for clear everyday communication

Neutral Spanish clear, respectful aur broadly suitable hoti hai. Overly formal wording ko har daily interaction mein use karna unnatural lag sakta hai.

Quería consultar el estado de mi solicitud.I wanted to ask about the status of my application.
¿Puedes revisar este dato antes de enviarlo?Can you check this detail before sending it?
PART 4 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Informal register and relationship

Informal register friends, peers aur familiar relations mein natural hoti hai. Slang ko audience ke mutabiq use karein; unfamiliar person ke saath assumptions na banayein.

¿Te viene bien quedar después de clase?Does meeting after class work for you?
Ahora te paso el enlace.I’ll send you the link in a moment.
PART 5 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Specialised language and plain-language reformulation

Specialised terms precision dete hain, magar audience ko samajh na aaye to brief explanation dein. Technical accuracy ko simple wording ke saath combine karein.

El informe analiza la interoperabilidad de los sistemas.The report analyses the interoperability of the systems.
En otras palabras, estudia cómo pueden trabajar juntos los sistemas.In other words, it studies how the systems can work together.
PART 6 · STYLE AND REGISTER

Shift register while preserving meaning

Register shift karte waqt facts aur intention same rakhein. Sirf vocabulary nahi, greeting, request, sentence length aur closing bhi adjust hotay hain.

Le escribo para solicitar una ampliación del plazo.I am writing to request an extension of the deadline.
¿Podríamos ampliar un poco el plazo?Could we extend the deadline a little?
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek hi real request (meeting reschedule, document mangwana ya issue report karna) ko teen versions mein likhein: formal email, neutral message aur informal peer chat. Har version mein purpose same rakhein aur register choices explain karein.
