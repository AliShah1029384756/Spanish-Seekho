CEFR C1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

Cohesion, information structure and sophisticated connectors

Long arguments ko readable aur logically connected banayein. Theme, focus aur progression ke mutabiq information organise karein.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Organise information through theme, focus and controlled progression.
  • Choose connectors for contrast, concession, consequence and reformulation precisely.
  • Build cohesive paragraphs without repetitive or mechanical linkers.
PART 1 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Cohesion beyond connectors

Cohesion sirf linkers ki list nahi. Reference, lexical variation, substitution aur logical progression sentences ko connect karte hain.

La medida reduce los costes iniciales; esta ventaja no garantiza su sostenibilidad.The measure reduces initial costs; this advantage does not guarantee its sustainability.
El equipo presentó una propuesta. La iniciativa recibió después varias modificaciones.The team presented a proposal. The initiative later received several modifications.
PART 2 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Theme and focus

Theme starting point hai; focus nayi ya contrastive information. En cuanto a… topic frame karta hai; lo verdaderamente importante es… point highlight karta hai.

En cuanto a la financiación, todavía quedan dos cuestiones por resolver.As for funding, two issues still remain to be resolved.
Lo verdaderamente preocupante no es el coste, sino la falta de seguimiento.What is truly concerning is not the cost, but the lack of follow-up.
PART 3 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Contrast and concession

Sin embargo/no obstante contrast mark karte hain. Aun así/pese a ello preceding point ke bawajood outcome introduce karte hain.

El plazo era muy ajustado; aun así, el equipo entregó el trabajo a tiempo.The deadline was very tight; even so, the team delivered on time.
La propuesta es ambiciosa. No obstante, cuenta con un plan realista.The proposal is ambitious. Nevertheless, it has a realistic plan.
PART 4 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Cause and consequence

Por consiguiente consequence mark karta hai. De ahí que ke baad subjunctive aata hai. Cause aur result logically align rakhein.

La muestra era reducida; por consiguiente, los resultados deben interpretarse con cautela.The sample was small; consequently, the results should be interpreted cautiously.
No se definieron las responsabilidades, de ahí que surgieran duplicidades.Responsibilities were not defined, which is why duplication arose.
PART 5 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Reformulation

Es decir, dicho de otro modo aur más concretamente dense idea ko clarify karte hain. Reformulate tab karein jab clarity improve ho.

La medida es provisional; dicho de otro modo, podrá revisarse con nuevos datos.The measure is provisional; in other words, it may be reviewed with new data.
El problema es estructural; más concretamente, afecta al reparto de responsabilidades.The problem is structural; more specifically, it affects the distribution of responsibilities.
PART 6 · C1 DISCOURSE SKILL

Paragraph progression

Paragraph claim se development, evidence/contrast aur implication ki taraf move kare. Har sentence ko connector ki zaroorat nahi.

Por un lado, la digitalización agiliza los trámites; por otro, exige garantías de privacidad.On the one hand, digitalisation speeds up procedures; on the other, it requires privacy safeguards.
En última instancia, la eficacia dependerá de cómo se apliquen las medidas.Ultimately, effectiveness will depend on how the measures are implemented.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

200 alfaaz ka paragraph likhein jo digital services ke benefits aur risks compare kare. Theme/focus consciously organise karein aur contrast, concession, consequence aur reformulation ko natural use karein.
