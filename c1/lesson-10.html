CEFR C1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 10

Evaluating complex arguments and evidence

Assess how a complex argument is built: trace its conclusion, test evidence quality, recognise limitations and distinguish correlation from causation.

C1 advancedRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Map claims, reasons, evidence and intermediate conclusions.
  • Evaluate source relevance, reliability, scope and limitations.
  • Recognise weak causal reasoning, overgeneralisation and misleading comparisons.
PART 1 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Map the argument before judging it

Argument ko parts mein divide karein: central claim, reasons, evidence aur conclusion. Kabhi author intermediate conclusion deta hai jo aglay point ka support banti hai.

El estudio aporta indicios relevantes, pero no permite establecer una relación causal.The study provides relevant indications but does not allow a causal relationship to be established.
A partir de estos datos, el equipo concluye que conviene ampliar la muestra.On the basis of these data, the team concludes that the sample should be expanded.
PART 2 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Evidence quality and relevance

Evidence sirf impressive lagne se strong nahi hota. Dekhein data claim ke relevant hain? Sample kaisa hai? Source ka method transparent hai?

La muestra incluye participantes de distintas edades y procedencias.The sample includes participants of different ages and backgrounds.
No se especifica el método utilizado para recopilar las respuestas.The method used to collect the responses is not specified.
PART 3 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Correlation is not causation

Do trends saath move kar sakte hain magar iska matlab yeh nahi ke aik doosray ka sabab hain. Alternative explanations aur confounding factors check karein.

Aunque ambas variables aumentan a la vez, eso no demuestra que una cause la otra.Although both variables rise at the same time, that does not prove that one causes the other.
Habría que descartar otros factores antes de atribuir el cambio a la medida.Other factors would need to be ruled out before attributing the change to the measure.
PART 4 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Scope, generalisation and comparison

Limited sample se universal conclusion nahi nikalna chahiye. Comparisons mein baseline, time period aur measurement same honay chahiye.

Los resultados son prometedores, si bien no pueden extrapolarse a toda la población.The results are promising, although they cannot be extrapolated to the entire population.
La comparación solo es válida si se mantienen los mismos criterios de medición.The comparison is valid only if the same measurement criteria are maintained.
PART 5 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Counterarguments and rebuttals

Strong evaluation opposing evidence ko fairly represent karti hai. Phir dekhein response waqai objection ka jawab deta hai ya sirf usay dismiss karta hai.

Es cierto que el coste inicial es elevado; sin embargo, el análisis no contempla el ahorro a largo plazo.It is true that the initial cost is high; however, the analysis does not consider long-term savings.
La objeción es pertinente, aunque no invalida por sí sola la conclusión principal.The objection is relevant, although it does not by itself invalidate the main conclusion.
PART 6 · C1 CRITICAL COMPREHENSION

Calibrated judgement

C1 academic style mein conclusion evidence ki strength ke mutabiq hota hai. 'Demuestra', 'apunta a', 'es compatible con' aur 'no permite concluir' ka farq samjhein.

Los datos apuntan a una mejora moderada, pendiente de confirmación independiente.The data point to a moderate improvement, pending independent confirmation.
La evidencia disponible no basta para sostener una conclusión definitiva.The available evidence is insufficient to support a definitive conclusion.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Do short claims ka 180–220 word critical response banayein. Har claim ka evidence map karein, aik limitation identify karein, alternative explanation dein aur calibrated conclusion likhein.
