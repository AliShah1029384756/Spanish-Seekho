CEFR B1 · UNIT 15 · LESSON 15

Applications and professional self-presentation

Introduce yourself professionally, describe relevant experience and respond to common interview questions.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · APPLY FOR A ROLE

Use clear professional language

Me gustaría presentar mi candidatura para...I would like to apply for...
Adjunto mi currículum.I am attaching my CV.
Tengo experiencia en...I have experience in...
Estoy disponible para una entrevista.I am available for an interview.
PART 2 · PRESENT YOUR STRENGTHS

Describe relevant skills with evidence

Me considero una persona organizada.I consider myself an organised person.
Tengo capacidad para resolver problemas.I have the ability to solve problems.
Un ejemplo de ello es...An example of this is...

Strength ke saath short example dena self-presentation ko credible banata hai. Sirf adjectives ki list na banayein.

PART 3 · COMMON INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

Answer clearly and stay relevant

¿Podría hablarme de su experiencia?Could you tell me about your experience?
¿Cuáles son sus principales puntos fuertes?What are your main strengths?
¿Por qué le interesa este puesto?Why are you interested in this position?
Formal register

Interview questions use usted: su experiencia, sus puntos fuertes, le interesa. Answers should stay polite and professional.

PART 4 · MODEL INTERVIEW

Introduce your profile

—¿Podría hablarme de su experiencia?
—Claro. Tengo experiencia en atención al cliente y en tareas administrativas. En mi último puesto colaboré con un equipo de seis personas.
—¿Cuáles son sus principales puntos fuertes?
—Me considero una persona organizada y responsable. Un ejemplo de ello es que coordinaba las tareas semanales y cumplíamos los plazos.
—¿Por qué le interesa este puesto?
—Me interesa porque puedo aportar mi experiencia y seguir desarrollando mis habilidades.

—Could you tell me about your experience? —Of course. I have experience in customer service and administrative tasks. In my last position I worked with a team of six people. —What are your main strengths? —I consider myself organised and responsible. An example is that I coordinated weekly tasks and we met deadlines. —Why are you interested in this position? —I am interested because I can contribute my experience and continue developing my skills.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a short application email (100–130 words) for a role you genuinely qualify for or a clearly fictional practice role. Include the position, relevant experience, two skills supported by examples, why you are interested and interview availability. Do not invent qualifications or work history.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
