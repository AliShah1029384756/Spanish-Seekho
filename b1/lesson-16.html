CEFR B1 · UNIT 16 · LESSON 16

Social issues and everyday change

Discuss changes in society, explain their effects and present a balanced viewpoint.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · TALK ABOUT CHANGE

Describe trends over time

En los últimos años...In recent years...
Cada vez más personas...More and more people...
Cada vez menos gente...Fewer and fewer people...
Se ha convertido en...It has become...
PART 2 · DISCUSS CAUSES AND EFFECTS

Explain what may be changing

Esto puede deberse a...This may be due to...
Como consecuencia...As a consequence...
Tiene un efecto en...It has an effect on...
Afecta especialmente a...It especially affects...
PART 3 · PRESENT A BALANCED VIEW

Avoid absolute statements

Es un tema complejo.It is a complex issue.
No afecta a todo el mundo de la misma manera.It does not affect everyone in the same way.
Hay ventajas, aunque también existen dificultades.There are advantages, although difficulties also exist.

Social topic par balanced language use karein. Trend ko universal fact na banayein; examples, context aur different experiences mention karein.

PART 4 · MODEL DISCUSSION

How has remote work changed daily life?

En los últimos años, el trabajo a distancia se ha convertido en una opción más habitual en algunos sectores. Cada vez más empresas permiten trabajar desde casa algunos días. Esto puede reducir el tiempo de desplazamiento y facilitar la organización familiar. Sin embargo, también puede ser difícil separar el trabajo y la vida personal. Además, no todos los empleos permiten trabajar a distancia. Por eso, es un tema complejo y las soluciones dependen del tipo de trabajo y de las necesidades de cada persona.

In recent years, remote work has become a more common option in some sectors. More companies allow people to work from home on some days. This can reduce commuting time and make family organisation easier. However, it can also be difficult to separate work and personal life. Furthermore, not all jobs allow remote work. That is why it is a complex issue and solutions depend on the type of work and each person's needs.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Choose a familiar social change (remote work, online education, public transport, digital services or environmental habits). Write 120–150 words describing the trend, one possible cause, two effects and a balanced conclusion. Avoid presenting personal impressions as universal facts.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
