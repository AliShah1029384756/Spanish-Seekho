CEFR B1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 03

Experiences and achievements

Talk about life experiences and completed events with the present perfect and indefinido in context.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · PRESENT PERFECT

Talk about experiences connected to now

Present perfect ka structure: haber present tense + past participle. Isay recent/relevant experiences aur open time periods ke saath use karte hain.

He trabajado en varios proyectos. = I have worked on several projects.
¿Has visitado Sevilla? = Have you visited Seville?
Esta semana hemos tenido mucho trabajo. = We have had a lot of work this week.

he / has / ha / hemos / habéis / han + participiopresent forms of haber + participle
PART 2 · INDEFINIDO

Place an event in a finished past time

Ayer terminé el curso. = Yesterday I finished the course.
En 2022 viajé a España. = In 2022 I travelled to Spain.
La semana pasada conocí a mi nuevo jefe. = Last week I met my new boss.

Finished time expressions such as ayer, el año pasado, en 2022 often go with indefinido because the event is placed in a completed period.

PART 3 · CHOOSE THE FOCUS

Open time, experience or finished event?

Hoy he hablado con mi profesora.Today I have spoken to my teacher. — today is treated as an open period.
Ayer hablé con mi profesora.Yesterday I spoke to my teacher. — finished time.
¿Alguna vez has trabajado en equipo?Have you ever worked in a team? — experience.
Regional note

In much of Spain, present perfect is common with open periods such as hoy or esta semana. Other varieties of Spanish may use indefinido more widely. Actual usage varies by speaker and region; context and meaning matter more than a rigid formula.

PART 4 · IRREGULAR PARTICIPLES

Useful forms to remember

hacer → hechoto do/make → done/made
ver → vistoto see → seen
escribir → escritoto write → written
abrir → abiertoto open → opened
poner → puestoto put → put
volver → vueltoto return → returned
PART 5 · INTERVIEW

Ask about someone's experience

—¿Has participado alguna vez en un proyecto internacional?
—Sí, he trabajado con un equipo de Portugal.
—¿Cuándo fue?
—Fue el año pasado. Colaboramos durante tres meses y aprendí mucho.

—Have you ever taken part in an international project? —Yes, I have worked with a team from Portugal. —When was it? —It was last year. We collaborated for three months and I learned a lot.

Experience question = present perfect. When the time is specified as a finished period = indefinido.

PART 6 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Prepare a short interview answer about three experiences or achievements. Say what you have done, then give one example with a finished date/time. Write 100–130 words and include at least two present-perfect forms and three indefinido forms.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
