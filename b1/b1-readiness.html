PART 1 · HONEST SELF-ASSESSMENT

How confident do you feel?

Har statement ke liye apni current ability choose karein. Apne aap ko overrate ya underrate na karein. Agar possible ho to real example ya apni writing/speaking sample se decision support karein.

1. I can understand the main points of clear speech on familiar study, work and everyday topics.Listening
2. I can understand the main ideas in clear texts and identify important details.Reading
3. I can narrate a past experience and distinguish background from completed events.Narration
4. I can describe plans, intentions, predictions and hypothetical possibilities.Time and possibilities
5. I can explain causes, consequences and give reasons for an opinion.Explaining
6. I can compare options, disagree politely and negotiate a practical agreement.Interaction
7. I can give recommendations and use common subjunctive patterns in context.Grammar in use
8. I can add detail with relative clauses and report information I have heard.Flexible language
9. I can write connected messages or short texts with a clear purpose and structure.Writing
10. I can speak for about two minutes on a familiar issue and make myself understood.Speaking
11. I can summarise key information for another person without translating every word.Mediation
12. I can manage familiar travel, study, work and service situations with increasing independence.Real-life communication
