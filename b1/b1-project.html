PROJECT BRIEF

Your situation in Spain

Aap ko Spain mein aik naya study programme, work placement ya relocation arrangement manage karna hai. Aap ko information leni hai, apni situation explain karni hai, aik problem ka hal propose karna hai aur clear next steps agree karne hain. Neeche scenario choose karein; phir Spanish mein apna complete plan tayyar karein.

A language centre has offered you a place. You need to confirm the timetable, ask about documents and explain that your arrival date may change.
TASK 1 · FIRST CONTACT

Write a clear enquiry

60–90 words. Introduce the situation, explain what you need to confirm and ask two relevant questions. Register: polite and neutral.

TASK 2 · CLARIFY INFORMATION

Ask follow-up questions

Write four questions you would ask in a call or meeting. Include at least one question about timing, one about documents and one polite clarification request.

TASK 3 · EXPLAIN A PROBLEM

Describe what happened and its effect

60–80 words. Explain a realistic obstacle, its cause and consequence. Use past narration and connectors such as porque, debido a, así que or por eso.

TASK 4 · NEGOTIATE A SOLUTION

Propose an alternative

Write a short exchange (at least six turns) between you and a coordinator, employer or landlord. Include a polite proposal, a disagreement or concern, a compromise and an agreed next step.

TASK 5 · INTEGRATED ACTION PLAN

Write your final plan

130–160 words. Summarise your goal, confirmed details, remaining issue, solution, deadlines and who will do what. Include one recommendation using a suitable subjunctive structure.

TASK 6 · MEDIATION

Explain the plan to another person

Imagine a family member or classmate does not understand the original message. Write a 50–70 word summary in clear Spanish, selecting only the information they need.

SAVE YOUR PROJECT

Review and mark your work

Drafts autosave on this device. A project is marked complete only when all six written tasks contain a meaningful draft and all six self-checks are ticked. This checks task completion, not Spanish accuracy.

Final self-review
  • Read each text aloud and check verb forms and accents.
  • Use a consistent tú/usted relationship and register.
  • Check agreement, connectors and punctuation.
  • Ask a proficient Spanish speaker to review your work when possible.
