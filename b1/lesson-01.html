CEFR B1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

Narrating past events naturally

Ab past tense sirf alag alag jumlon mein nahi — aik mukammal waqia story ki tarah bayan karna seekhein. Background, actions, interruptions aur sequence ko naturally connect karein.

Indefinido vs imperfectoStorytellingSpain Spanish
PART 1 · THE BIG IDEA

Do past tenses, do different jobs

Indefinido aksar batata hai ke past mein kya hua — completed event, action ya story ka next step.

Imperfecto aksar batata hai ke us waqt situation kaisi thi, kya chal raha tha, ya pehle kya regularly hota tha.

Yaad rakhne ka asaan tareeqa

Indefinido = story ke events. Imperfecto = story ka background. Yeh useful rule hai, lekin har sentence ka faisla context aur meaning se hota hai.

PART 2 · COMPARE THE FORMS

Same past, different focus

Ayer fui al centro.Yesterday I went to the city centre. — completed event
Antes iba al centro en autobús.I used to go to the city centre by bus. — past habit
La tienda estaba cerrada.The shop was closed. — background/state
A las ocho, abrió la tienda.At eight, the shop opened. — event

Roman Urdu: “dukaan band thi” = estado/background (estaba). “Dukaan khul gayi” = completed event (abrió).

PART 3 · BUILD THE STORY

Background + interruption

Ayer llovía mucho.Yesterday it was raining heavily.
Yo caminaba hacia casa.I was walking home.
De repente, vi a un amigo.Suddenly, I saw a friend.
Nos paramos y hablamos un rato.We stopped and talked for a while.
Grammar pattern

Ongoing background: imperfecto (llovía, caminaba). Sudden/completed events: indefinido (vi, nos paramos, hablamos). “Mientras” often introduces an ongoing action; “de repente” often introduces a sudden event.

PART 5 · REAL-LIFE STORY

Un día complicado en Málaga

El sábado pasado salí de casa a las nueve.
Last Saturday I left home at nine.

Hacía frío y había mucha gente en la calle.
It was cold and there were many people in the street.

Mientras esperaba el autobús, recibí una llamada.
While I was waiting for the bus, I received a call.

Era mi compañero; necesitaba ayuda.
It was my colleague; he needed help.

Así que cambié mis planes y fui a su casa.
So I changed my plans and went to his house.

Notice: salí, recibí, cambié, fui = events. Hacía, había, esperaba, era, necesitaba = scene/background or ongoing action.

PART 6 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni story banayein

Speaking challenge

Apne pichlay weekend ka 60–90 second ka waqia Spanish mein sunayein. Kam az kam 4 indefinido verbs, 3 imperfecto verbs aur 3 connectors use karein.

Writing challenge

“Un día que nunca olvidaré” (A day I will never forget) par 100–120 words likhein. Pehle background set karein, phir events ko order mein batayein aur ending explain karein.

Self-check

Kya listener ko samajh aata hai ke kya chal raha tha, phir kya hua, aur aakhir mein kya result nikla? Agar haan, aapki story clear hai.
