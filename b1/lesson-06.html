CEFR B1 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

Agreeing and disagreeing diplomatically

Respond to different viewpoints, disagree respectfully and keep a discussion moving.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · AGREE

Show full or partial agreement

Estoy totalmente de acuerdo.I completely agree.
Estoy de acuerdo contigo.I agree with you.
En parte, sí.Partly, yes.
Tienes razón en eso.You are right about that.
PART 2 · DISAGREE POLITELY

Challenge an idea without attacking the person

No estoy del todo de acuerdo.I do not entirely agree.
Entiendo lo que dices, pero...I understand what you are saying, but...
No lo veo exactamente así.I do not see it exactly that way.
Puede que tengas razón, aunque...You may be right, although...
Conversation tip

Pehle doosray person ki baat acknowledge karein, phir apna different view reason ke saath explain karein. “You are wrong” kehne ke bajaye “No estoy del todo de acuerdo porque…” zyada diplomatic hai.

PART 3 · KEEP THE DISCUSSION MOVING

Ask for clarification and suggest alternatives

¿A qué te refieres exactamente?What exactly do you mean?
¿Puedes explicar un poco más?Can you explain a little more?
¿Y si buscamos otra solución?What if we look for another solution?
Quizá podríamos...Perhaps we could...
PART 4 · MODEL DISCUSSION

Should students study online?

—Creo que las clases online son más cómodas.
—Estoy de acuerdo en parte. Ahorran tiempo, pero a veces es difícil concentrarse en casa.
—Entiendo lo que dices. Sin embargo, podemos organizar pausas y participar en grupos.
—Es verdad. Quizá podríamos combinar clases online y presenciales.
—Me parece una buena solución.

—I think online classes are more convenient. —I partly agree. They save time, but sometimes it is difficult to concentrate at home. —I understand what you mean. However, we can organise breaks and take part in groups. —That's true. Perhaps we could combine online and in-person classes. —That seems like a good solution.

Register

Estoy de acuerdo contigo uses informal tú. In a formal conversation, you can say Estoy de acuerdo con usted or Entiendo su punto de vista.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Have a short discussion about online learning, public transport or working from home. Write both sides of the conversation (6–8 turns). Include one full agreement, one partial disagreement, one clarification question and one compromise proposal.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
