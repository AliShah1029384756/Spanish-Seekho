CEFR B1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 20

Complaints, claims and resolving service problems

Explain a service problem clearly, make a polite complaint and ask for a practical resolution. Roman Urdu explanations ke saath Spain Spanish mein practical communication develop karein.

Customer serviceFormal complaintsPractical Spain Spanish
LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of this lesson

By the end, you can describe a service problem, make a polite formal complaint, provide relevant evidence and request a specific resolution.

PART 1 · EXPLAIN WHAT WENT WRONG

Describe the issue clearly

El producto no funciona correctamente.The product does not work properly.
Me han cobrado dos veces.I have been charged twice.
La reserva no aparece en el sistema.The reservation does not appear in the system.
El pedido llegó con retraso y faltaba un artículo.The order arrived late and one item was missing.
Remember

State facts first: what happened, when it happened and what evidence you have. Avoid insults or assumptions about intent.

PART 2 · MAKE A POLITE COMPLAINT

Use formal, neutral language

Quisiera presentar una reclamación.I would like to make a formal complaint.
Hay un problema con mi factura.There is a problem with my invoice.
Me gustaría que revisaran el cargo.I would like you to review the charge.
¿Podría comprobarlo, por favor?Could you check it, please?
Remember

Quisiera and podría are polite conditional forms. In Spain, reclamación is common for a formal complaint or claim about a service.

PART 3 · REQUEST A SOLUTION

Say what outcome you need

Solicito la devolución del importe.I request a refund of the amount.
¿Sería posible cambiar el producto?Would it be possible to exchange the product?
Le agradecería que me enviara una copia de la factura.I would appreciate it if you sent me a copy of the invoice.
¿Cuándo recibiré una respuesta?When will I receive a response?
Remember

Solicito is direct and formal. Le agradecería que + imperfect subjunctive (enviara) is a polite request. State the solution you are asking for.

PART 4 · A PRACTICAL SPAIN CONTEXT

Ask about the complaint process

¿Podría indicarme cómo presentar una reclamación?Could you tell me how to make a complaint?
¿Hay una hoja de reclamaciones disponible?Is a complaint form available?
¿Me puede dar un justificante de la solicitud?Can you give me proof of the request?
Guardaré la factura y el número de referencia.I will keep the invoice and reference number.
Remember

In Spain, hoja de reclamaciones is a commonly used complaint form in consumer-service contexts. The exact process can vary by service and autonomous community; this lesson teaches language, not legal advice.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Write a clear complaint

Explain the problem with dates, order/reference details and evidence.Explain the problem with dates, order/reference details and evidence.
Ask politely for one reasonable solution.Ask politely for one reasonable solution.
Keep a copy of your message and any reference number.Keep a copy of your message and any reference number.
Remember

Write a 120–150 word formal message. Be factual, polite and specific about the outcome you are requesting.

PART 6 · MODEL DIALOGUE

Use it in a real conversation

—Buenos días. Quisiera presentar una reclamación porque me han cobrado dos veces. —Lo siento. ¿Tiene el justificante del pago? —Sí, aquí está. Le agradecería que revisaran el cargo y me confirmaran cuándo recibiré una respuesta. —De acuerdo. Le daremos un número de referencia.

Dialogue ko pehle read karein, phir roles change karke aloud practise karein. Context ke mutabiq formal ya informal register maintain karein.

PART 7 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a formal complaint email about a delayed delivery, incorrect charge or faulty product. Include the facts, evidence, the solution you request and a polite closing.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur grammar review karein.
