CEFR B1 · UNIT 09 · LESSON 09

Future predictions and promises

Use the future simple to make predictions, promises and assumptions about what may happen.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · FORM THE FUTURE

Infinitive + future ending

Regular future simple mein infinitive ke saath endings add hoti hain. Yeh endings -ar, -er aur -ir verbs ke liye same hoti hain.

hablar: hablaré, hablarás, hablará, hablaremos, hablaréis, hablarán.
comer: comeré, comerás, comerá, comeremos, comeréis, comerán.
vivir: viviré, vivirás, vivirá, viviremos, viviréis, vivirán.

Mañana trabajaré desde casa.Tomorrow I will work from home.
PART 2 · IRREGULAR STEMS

Common future forms

tener → tendréto have → I will have
poder → podréto be able to → I will be able to
hacer → haréto do/make → I will do/make
decir → diréto say/tell → I will say/tell
salir → saldréto leave/go out → I will leave/go out
venir → vendréto come → I will come
PART 3 · PREDICTIONS AND ASSUMPTIONS

Say what you think will happen

Creo que mañana hará buen tiempo.I think the weather will be good tomorrow.
Probablemente llegarán tarde.They will probably arrive late.
Estará en casa ahora.He/she is probably at home now.

Future simple kabhi present ke bare mein assumption bhi show karta hai: Estará en casa = He/she is probably at home. Context se meaning clear hota hai.

PART 4 · PROMISES AND OFFERS

Commit to an action

Te llamaré cuando llegue.I will call you when I arrive.
No te preocupes, lo haré mañana.Don't worry, I will do it tomorrow.
¿Me ayudarás con las cajas?Will you help me with the boxes?
PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Give five predictions about the next year, two promises and one assumption about what someone is probably doing now. Use at least four future forms, two irregular stems and one probability adverb.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
