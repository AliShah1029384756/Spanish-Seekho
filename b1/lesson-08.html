CEFR B1 · UNIT 08 · LESSON 08

Clarifying and reformulating

Ask what someone means, check your understanding and keep a conversation moving.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · ASK FOR CLARIFICATION

Make sure you understand

¿A qué te refieres exactamente?What exactly do you mean?
¿Podrías explicarlo un poco más?Could you explain it a little more?
¿Puedes darme un ejemplo?Can you give me an example?
¿Qué quieres decir con...?What do you mean by...?
PART 2 · CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Repeat the meaning in your own words

Si te he entendido bien...If I have understood you correctly...
Entonces, lo que dices es que...So, what you are saying is that...
En otras palabras...In other words...
¿Es correcto?Is that right?
PART 3 · REFORMULATE YOURSELF

Say it another way

Lo que intento decir es que...What I am trying to say is that...
Mejor dicho...Rather / more precisely...
No me he explicado bien.I have not explained myself well.
Déjame ponerlo de otra manera.Let me put it another way.
PART 4 · KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING

Use short thinking phrases

A ver...Let's see / well...
Buena pregunta.Good question.
Déjame pensarlo un momento.Let me think about it for a moment.
Mini-dialogue

—Deberíamos cambiar el sistema.
—¿A qué te refieres exactamente?
—A que necesitamos una forma más sencilla de reservar.
—Si te he entendido bien, quieres reducir los pasos.
—Exactamente. Mejor dicho, quiero que sea más fácil para todos.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a 6–8 turn conversation where one person explains a problem and the other asks for clarification. Include a check-understanding phrase, one example request and one reformulation. Then practise it aloud.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
