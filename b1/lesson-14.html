PART 4 · MODEL PROFILE

A learner describing their development

Estudié informática y tengo formación en desarrollo web. Se me da bien resolver problemas y trabajar en equipo. Sin embargo, me cuesta hablar en público, así que estoy practicando para ganar confianza. Mi objetivo a corto plazo es mejorar mi español y participar en reuniones con más seguridad. A largo plazo me gustaría trabajar en un entorno internacional. He aprendido que la práctica constante es más útil que estudiar muchas horas de vez en cuando.

I studied computer science and have training in web development. I am good at solving problems and working in a team. However, I find public speaking difficult, so I am practising to gain confidence. My short-term goal is to improve my Spanish and participate in meetings more confidently. In the long term I would like to work in an international environment. I have learned that regular practice is more useful than studying for many hours occasionally.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Prepare a short professional/education profile (120–150 words). Mention your education or training, two strengths, one skill you are improving, a short-term goal and a long-term goal. Keep it honest and specific.
