CEFR B1 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 04

Life changes and milestones

Describe how your life, routines and priorities have changed over time.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · THEN AND NOW

Compare different stages of your life

Antes vivía con mi familia; ahora comparto piso.
I used to live with my family; now I share a flat.
De pequeño jugaba al fútbol todos los días.
As a child I played football every day.
Hoy en día trabajo y estudio por la tarde.
Nowadays I work and study in the afternoon.

Antes, de pequeño and hoy en día help organise comparisons across time.

PART 2 · SOLER + INFINITIVE

Talk about former habits

Soler + infinitive is useful for describing what someone used to do regularly. In the past, use solía / solías / solía / solíamos / solíais / solían.

Antes solía caminar al trabajo.I used to walk to work.
Solíamos pasar los veranos en el pueblo.We used to spend summers in the village.
Ahora suelo ir en metro.Now I usually go by metro.
PART 3 · MILESTONES AND CHANGES

Describe a change and its effect

Me mudé a otra ciudad hace dos años.I moved to another city two years ago.
Desde que vivo aquí, conozco mejor la ciudad.Since I have lived here, I know the city better.
Mi forma de pensar ha cambiado mucho.The way I think has changed a lot.
Fue una experiencia importante para mí.It was an important experience for me.
PART 4 · MODEL STORY

A new stage

Hace tres años vivía en un pueblo pequeño. Solía ir andando al instituto y conocía a casi todo el mundo. Después terminé mis estudios y me mudé a Málaga para trabajar. Al principio me costaba adaptarme porque la ciudad era mucho más grande. Sin embargo, poco a poco hice nuevos amigos y empecé a disfrutar de la vida allí. Desde que vivo en Málaga, soy más independiente y he aprendido a organizar mejor mi tiempo.

Three years ago I lived in a small village. I used to walk to school and knew almost everyone. Afterwards I finished my studies and moved to Málaga to work. At first it was difficult to adapt because the city was much bigger. However, little by little I made new friends and began to enjoy life there. Since living in Málaga, I am more independent and have learned to organise my time better.

Story mein past habits (vivía, solía, conocía), completed milestones (terminé, me mudé, hice) aur present relevance (he aprendido) alag roles play karte hain.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Describe three stages or changes in your life: before, a turning point and now. Use solía + infinitive for a past habit, at least two indefinido events and one present-perfect sentence about a result that matters today.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
