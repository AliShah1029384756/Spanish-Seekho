CEFR B1 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

Building coherent stories

Connect events, contrast ideas and add background detail so your story flows naturally.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · STORY STRUCTURE

Give your story a clear beginning, middle and ending

B1 par listener ko sirf events nahi, unka order aur relation bhi samajhna chahiye. Story ko stages mein organise karein.

Al principioAt first / in the beginning
Poco despuésShortly afterwards
Mientras tantoMeanwhile
Finalmente / Al finalFinally / in the end
PART 2 · CONTRAST

Show a change or unexpected result

Sin embargoHowever / nevertheless
Aunque estaba cansado, seguí caminando.Although I was tired, I kept walking.
A pesar de la lluvia, salimos.Despite the rain, we went out.
En cambioBy contrast / on the other hand
Grammar note

Aunque + indicative can introduce a fact the speaker accepts: Aunque llovía, salimos. “Although it was raining, we went out.” A pesar de + noun is another concise way to show contrast.

PART 3 · CONNECT CAUSE AND RESULT

Explain why an event happened

Como había mucho tráfico, llegamos tarde.As there was a lot of traffic, we arrived late.
Así queSo / therefore (introduces a result)
Por ese motivoFor that reason
Debido aDue to / because of
Compare

No había autobuses, así que fuimos andando. = There were no buses, so we walked.
Debido al retraso, perdimos la conexión. = Due to the delay, we missed the connection.

Debido a + el contracts to debido al.

PART 4 · MODEL STORY

A delayed journey

Al principio, todo iba bien. Salimos de Málaga temprano y el tiempo era estupendo. Mientras esperábamos el tren, anunciaron un retraso. Sin embargo, nadie explicó cuánto tiempo teníamos que esperar. Poco después, una empleada nos informó de que había una avería. Así que buscamos otra conexión. Aunque llegamos más tarde de lo previsto, finalmente pudimos continuar el viaje.

At first, everything was going well. We left Málaga early and the weather was great. While we were waiting for the train, they announced a delay. However, nobody explained how long we had to wait. Shortly afterwards, an employee told us there was a fault. So we looked for another connection. Although we arrived later than expected, we were finally able to continue the journey.

Notice how the story moves from background (iba, era, esperábamos) to events (salimos, anunciaron, informó, buscamos, llegamos) and uses connectors to show contrast and result.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Tell a 90–120 second story about a journey or unexpected event. Use at least four connectors: al principio, mientras tanto, sin embargo, así que, aunque, finalmente. Then write a 100–130 word version.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
