CEFR B1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 19

Advice, recommendations and expressing necessity

Give useful advice, recommend actions and explain what is necessary or optional. Roman Urdu explanations ke saath Spain Spanish mein practical communication develop karein.

Advice & recommendationsNecessitySubjunctive patterns
LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of this lesson

By the end, you can give advice, recommend an action with que + subjunctive, and distinguish what is necessary from what is optional.

PART 1 · GIVE DIRECT BUT POLITE ADVICE

Recommend an action

Deberías descansar un poco.You should rest a little.
Te aconsejo que hables con la profesora.I advise you to speak with the teacher.
Te recomiendo que pidas una cita.I recommend that you make an appointment.
Sería buena idea comparar los precios.It would be a good idea to compare prices.
Remember

Deberías + infinitive gives direct advice. Recomendar/aconsejar que is followed by subjunctive when recommending an action for someone else: que hables, que pidas.

PART 2 · SAY WHAT IS NECESSARY

Express obligation and need

Es necesario presentar el documento.It is necessary to present the document.
Es importante que llegues a tiempo.It is important that you arrive on time.
Hace falta más información.More information is needed.
Necesitamos que nos envíen el formulario.We need them to send us the form.
Remember

Es necesario + infinitive can describe a general action. Es importante que / necesitamos que + subjunctive refer to an action or person in a clause.

PART 3 · DISTINGUISH NEED FROM CHOICE

Say what is optional

No hace falta que vengas tan temprano.You do not need to come so early.
No es necesario reservar con semanas de antelación.It is not necessary to book weeks in advance.
Puedes hacerlo mañana si te viene mejor.You can do it tomorrow if that suits you better.
Conviene revisar los datos antes de enviarlos.It is advisable to check the details before sending them.
Remember

No hace falta que means something is not necessary; it does not mean it is forbidden. Conviene + infinitive is a useful impersonal recommendation.

PART 4 · MODEL ADVICE

A student has too many deadlines

—Estoy agobiado porque tengo tres trabajos esta semana.—I feel overwhelmed because I have three assignments this week.
—Te recomiendo que prepares un horario realista.—I recommend that you prepare a realistic timetable.
—¿Es necesario que termine los tres hoy?—Is it necessary for me to finish all three today?
—No, no hace falta que los termines hoy. Conviene que empieces por el más urgente.—No, you do not need to finish them all today. It is advisable to start with the most urgent one.
Remember

The advice is practical and does not promise a guaranteed result. Ask about the person's situation before recommending.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Give advice for a realistic situation

Choose a situation: study pressure, travel planning, a work task or an appointment.Choose a situation: study pressure, travel planning, a work task or an appointment.
Give two recommendations and explain why.Give two recommendations and explain why.
Include one thing that is necessary and one thing that is optional.Include one thing that is necessary and one thing that is optional.
Remember

Write 100–130 words. Use deberías, te recomiendo que, es importante que and no hace falta que.

PART 6 · MODEL DIALOGUE

Use it in a real conversation

—No sé cómo organizar el proyecto. —Te aconsejo que dividas el trabajo en tareas pequeñas. También conviene que hables con tu equipo. —¿Tenemos que reunirnos todos los días? —No hace falta que os reunáis a diario; podéis acordar dos reuniones por semana.

Dialogue ko pehle read karein, phir roles change karke aloud practise karein. Context ke mutabiq formal ya informal register maintain karein.

PART 7 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a short advice message to someone who is preparing for an exam or a job interview. Give recommendations, explain necessity and distinguish what is optional.
