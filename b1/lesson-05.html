CEFR B1 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 05

Expressing opinions with reasons

Give a clear viewpoint, support it with reasons and develop your ideas with examples.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · INTRODUCE YOUR VIEWPOINT

Say what you think

Desde mi punto de vistaFrom my point of view
En mi opiniónIn my opinion
Considero que...I consider that...
Me parece que...It seems to me that...

Opinion ko clear introduce karein, phir reason aur example dein. B1 par sirf “me gusta” kehna kaafi nahi; apna viewpoint develop karein.

PART 2 · GIVE REASONS

Explain why you think that

La razón principal es que...The main reason is that...
Ya que...Since / because...
Por ejemplo...For example...
Además...Furthermore / in addition
Develop the idea

En mi opinión, aprender idiomas es importante, ya que permite conocer otras culturas. Por ejemplo, cuando viajé a España, pude hablar con la gente local. Además, aprender un idioma puede ayudar en el trabajo.

PART 3 · BALANCE YOUR ARGUMENT

Show more than one side

Por un lado..., por otro lado...On the one hand..., on the other hand...
Sin dudaWithout a doubt
En cambioBy contrast
Aun asíEven so / nevertheless

Balanced opinion ka matlab yeh nahi ke aapka viewpoint weak hai. Aap benefits aur limitations dono mention kar ke apni conclusion de sakte hain.

PART 4 · MODEL OPINION

Is online learning useful?

Desde mi punto de vista, aprender por internet es una buena opción para muchas personas. La razón principal es que ofrece flexibilidad: cada estudiante puede organizar su horario. Por ejemplo, alguien que trabaja puede estudiar por la noche. Por otro lado, estudiar solo puede ser difícil porque no siempre hay alguien que corrija tus errores. Aun así, considero que las clases online son útiles si se combinan con práctica y conversación.

From my point of view, learning online is a good option for many people. The main reason is that it offers flexibility: each student can organise their schedule. For example, someone who works can study at night. On the other hand, studying alone can be difficult because there is not always someone to correct your mistakes. Even so, I consider online classes useful if they are combined with practice and conversation.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Choose a topic (online learning, public transport, learning languages or working from home). Give your opinion in 100–130 words. Include a viewpoint phrase, two reasons, one example, one balanced/contrasting point and a short conclusion.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
