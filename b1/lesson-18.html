CEFR B1 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 18

Proposing solutions, negotiating and reaching agreement

Suggest practical options, respond to other viewpoints and work towards a shared agreement. Roman Urdu explanations ke saath Spain Spanish mein practical communication develop karein.

Making proposalsNegotiationReaching agreement
PART 1 · MAKE A SUGGESTION

Offer possible solutions

Podríamos cambiar el horario.We could change the timetable.
¿Qué te parece si buscamos otra opción?What do you think if we look for another option?
Propongo organizar una reunión breve.I propose organising a short meeting.
Una posibilidad sería compartir los gastos.One possibility would be to share the costs.
Remember

Podríamos + infinitive makes a soft suggestion. ¿Qué te parece si + present indicative is a natural way to ask for an opinion.

PART 2 · RESPOND TO AN IDEA

Accept, qualify or disagree politely

Me parece una buena idea.That seems like a good idea.
Estoy de acuerdo, siempre que tengamos tiempo.I agree, provided that we have time.
Entiendo tu punto, pero me preocupa el coste.I understand your point, but the cost concerns me.
No estoy del todo convencido de esa opción.I am not entirely convinced by that option.
Remember

Acknowledge the other person's idea before giving a different view. Siempre que can introduce a condition; at this level, learn it as a useful fixed pattern.

PART 3 · FIND A MIDDLE GROUND

Negotiate practical details

¿Y si dividimos el trabajo en dos partes?What if we divide the work into two parts?
Podemos llegar a un acuerdo sobre el presupuesto.We can reach an agreement about the budget.
Estoy dispuesto a ceder en ese punto.I am willing to compromise on that point.
Busquemos una solución que nos convenga a todos.Let's look for a solution that works for everyone.
Remember

Llegar a un acuerdo = reach an agreement. Ceder = give way/compromise. Conviene distinguish a proposal from a final decision.

PART 4 · MODEL NEGOTIATION

Organise a group project

—No tenemos tiempo para preparar dos presentaciones.—We do not have time to prepare two presentations.
—Podríamos combinar las dos ideas y repartir las tareas.—We could combine the two ideas and divide the tasks.
—Me parece bien, siempre que acordemos quién hace cada parte.—That sounds good, provided we agree who does each part.
—De acuerdo. Entonces, entregamos un único proyecto el viernes.—Agreed. Then we submit one project on Friday.
Remember

A successful negotiation ends with a clear next step: who will do what and by when.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Negotiate a shared plan

Choose a situation: group work, a shared flat, travel or a timetable.Choose a situation: group work, a shared flat, travel or a timetable.
Make two proposals and respond to another person's concern.Make two proposals and respond to another person's concern.
Finish with a specific agreement and action.Finish with a specific agreement and action.
Remember

Write a 10–12 line dialogue. Use podríamos, ¿qué te parece si…?, entiendo tu punto and llegamos a un acuerdo.

PART 6 · MODEL DIALOGUE

Use it in a real conversation

—Creo que deberíamos empezar a las ocho. —Entiendo tu punto, pero algunas personas salen tarde del trabajo. ¿Qué te parece si empezamos a las ocho y media? —Me parece bien, siempre que terminemos antes de las diez. —De acuerdo. Entonces, esa será la hora.

Dialogue ko pehle read karein, phir roles change karke aloud practise karein. Context ke mutabiq formal ya informal register maintain karein.

PART 7 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a short negotiation dialogue about a shared decision. Include two proposals, one polite disagreement, one compromise and a clear final agreement.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur grammar review karein.
