CEFR B1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

Plans, intentions and possible outcomes

Compare firm arrangements, intentions and predictions, then explain what you may do if plans change.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · PLANS AND INTENTIONS

Choose the right expression

Tengo pensado cambiar de trabajo.I am thinking of changing jobs / I plan to change jobs.
Estoy pensando en estudiar un máster.I am thinking about studying for a master's degree.
Me planteo mudarme el año que viene.I am considering moving next year.
Tenemos previsto salir temprano.We plan / expect to leave early.
PART 2 · PLAN OR PREDICTION?

Separate intention from expectation

Voy a solicitar el puesto. = I am going to apply for the position. (intention/plan)
Solicitaré el puesto si se publica la oferta. = I will apply if the vacancy is advertised. (future decision/prediction)
Probablemente recibiré una respuesta la próxima semana. = I will probably receive a reply next week. (prediction)

Ir a + infinitive often presents a plan or intention. Future simple can express a prediction, promise or decision about a future time. Context may allow both, with a different focus.

PART 3 · DISCUSS POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

Prepare an alternative

Si todo va bien...If everything goes well...
En caso de que cambien los planes...If the plans change...
Si no, buscaremos otra opción.If not, we will look for another option.
De todas formas...In any case...

Notice: en caso de que is followed by subjunctive when it introduces a possible or uncertain condition. Here, cambien is the present subjunctive of cambiar.

PART 4 · MODEL PLAN

Organising a move

Tengo pensado mudarme a Valencia el próximo año. Primero voy a buscar un piso y después hablaré con varias empresas. Si todo va bien, empezaré a trabajar en septiembre. En caso de que no encuentre alojamiento a tiempo, me quedaré unas semanas con un amigo. Probablemente ahorraré algo de dinero antes de mudarme. De todas formas, todavía tengo que comparar los gastos y organizar los documentos.

I plan to move to Valencia next year. First I am going to look for a flat and then I will speak to several companies. If everything goes well, I will start work in September. If I do not find accommodation in time, I will stay with a friend for a few weeks. I will probably save some money before moving. In any case, I still have to compare the costs and organise the documents.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Describe a plan for the next year (study, work, travel or moving). Explain what you intend to do, what you predict will happen, and one alternative if the plan changes. Use tengo pensado, voy a, future simple and si todo va bien / si no.

Pehle ideas organise karein, phir aloud bol kar record karein. Apni recording khud sun kar clarity, connectors aur verb forms review karein.
