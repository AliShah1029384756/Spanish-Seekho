CEFR B1 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

Making decisions and weighing options

Compare alternatives, explain advantages and disadvantages, and justify a decision.

B1 communicationRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
PART 1 · COMPARE OPTIONS

Talk about advantages and disadvantages

La principal ventaja es que...The main advantage is that...
El principal inconveniente es que...The main drawback is that...
Por una parte..., por otra...On the one hand..., on the other...
Depende de las circunstancias.It depends on the circumstances.
PART 2 · EVALUATE ALTERNATIVES

Explain what matters most

Lo que más valoro es...What I value most is...
Teniendo en cuenta el precio...Taking the price into account...
La opción más práctica sería...The most practical option would be...
A largo plazo...In the long term...
PART 3 · REACH A DECISION

State your choice and explain it

Me inclino por la segunda opción.I am leaning towards the second option.
Después de comparar las dos opciones...After comparing the two options...
He decidido elegir...I have decided to choose...
Decision framework

1. Name the alternatives. 2. Give one advantage and one drawback for each. 3. Explain which factor matters most. 4. State your decision and reason.

PART 4 · MODEL DECISION

Choose a way to study

Tengo dos opciones para mejorar mi español: asistir a clases presenciales o estudiar por internet. La principal ventaja de las clases presenciales es que puedo practicar con otras personas. Sin embargo, el horario es menos flexible. Por otra parte, estudiar por internet suele ser más económico y puedo organizar mi tiempo. Teniendo en cuenta mi trabajo, la opción más práctica sería combinar las dos. Después de comparar las ventajas y los inconvenientes, me inclino por esa alternativa.

I have two options for improving my Spanish: attending in-person classes or studying online. The main advantage of in-person classes is that I can practise with other people. However, the schedule is less flexible. On the other hand, studying online is usually cheaper and I can organise my time. Taking my work into account, the most practical option would be to combine the two. After comparing the advantages and drawbacks, I am leaning towards that alternative.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Compare two realistic options (study methods, transport, accommodation or work schedules). Write 120–150 words with at least two advantages, two drawbacks, your most important criterion and a justified final decision.
