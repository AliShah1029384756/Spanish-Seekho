Common subjunctive: recommendations, wishes and reactions

Recognise and use the present subjunctive in common expressions of wishes, recommendations and emotional reactions. Roman Urdu explanations ke saath Spain Spanish mein practical communication develop karein.

Present subjunctiveWishes & reactionsB1 grammar in context
PART 1 · WHEN DO WE USE IT?

Talk about wishes and actions that are not stated as facts

Quiero que vengas conmigo.I want you to come with me.
Espero que todo salga bien.I hope everything goes well.
Me alegra que estés aquí.I am glad that you are here.
Es importante que descanses.It is important that you rest.
Remember

A common B1 pattern is an expression of wish, recommendation, emotion or importance + que + subjunctive. It presents an action as desired, recommended or reacted to, not simply as a fact.

PART 2 · BUILD THE PRESENT SUBJUNCTIVE

Start from the yo form of the present indicative

hablo → que hable / hables / hableI speak → that I / you / he or she speak(s).
como → que coma / comas / comaI eat → that I / you / he or she eat(s).
vivo → que viva / vivas / vivaI live → that I / you / he or she live(s).
tener → tenga; hacer → haga; ir → vaya; ser → seaCommon irregular forms: have, do/make, go, be.
Remember

Remove -o from the yo form and add opposite endings: -ar verbs take e, es, e, emos, éis, en; -er/-ir verbs take a, as, a, amos, áis, an. Some verbs are irregular: tenga, haga, vaya, sea, esté, pueda.

PART 3 · WISHES AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Use common trigger expressions

Ojalá que haga buen tiempo.I hope the weather is good.
Te recomiendo que pruebes este método.I recommend that you try this method.
Prefiero que lleguemos un poco antes.I prefer that we arrive a little earlier.
Es mejor que lo hables con ella.It is better that you discuss it with her.
Remember

Ojalá (que) expresses a wish and takes subjunctive. Recomendar, preferir and expressions such as es mejor que commonly introduce a subjunctive clause.

PART 4 · REACTIONS AND FACTS

Compare subjunctive with indicative

Me sorprende que no venga.It surprises me that he/she is not coming.
No creo que sea difícil.I do not think it is difficult.
Creo que es una buena idea.I think it is a good idea.
Sé que tienes razón.I know that you are right.
Remember

Emotions and negative opinions such as no creo que commonly take subjunctive. Creo que and sé que normally present the following clause as information the speaker accepts, so they take indicative. Meaning and context matter.

PART 5 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Give a recommendation and express a wish

Tell a friend what you recommend they do before an interview.Tell a friend what you recommend they do before an interview.
Express one wish for a trip, project or event.Express one wish for a trip, project or event.
React to a situation using me alegra que or me sorprende que.React to a situation using me alegra que or me sorprende que.
Remember

Write 100–130 words. Use at least four subjunctive forms and underline them when reviewing your own draft.

PART 6 · MODEL DIALOGUE

Use it in a real conversation

—Mañana tengo una entrevista y estoy nervioso. —Te recomiendo que prepares dos ejemplos de tu experiencia. También es importante que descanses bien. —Espero que todo salga bien. —Seguro que sí. Me alegra que estés tomando tiempo para prepararte.

Dialogue ko pehle read karein, phir roles change karke aloud practise karein. Context ke mutabiq formal ya informal register maintain karein.

PART 7 · SPEAKING & WRITING

Ab apni baat develop karein

Production challenge

Write a short message giving advice to a friend. Include a wish, two recommendations and one emotional reaction. Identify the subjunctive verbs you used.
