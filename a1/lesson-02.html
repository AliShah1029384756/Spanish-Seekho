Practice tip

In words try to keep every vowel clear. Spanish does not reduce vowels the way English does in unstressed syllables.

Spain vs Latin America note

In much of central and northern Spain, c (before e/i) and z are pronounced like English “th” in “think”. In many parts of southern Spain and the Canary Islands, and across most of Latin America, they sound like “s”. Both patterns are standard. This course introduces the Castilian pattern used in much of central/northern Spain; real regional speech varies.

PART 3 · SIMPLE STRESS RULE

Where to put the emphasis

Spanish stress is regular. Yeh simple rule yaad rakhne se reading improve hoti hai.

  • If a word ends in a vowel, n or s → stress is usually on the second-last syllable.
    casa, hablan, lunes
  • If a word ends in any other consonant → stress is usually on the last syllable.
    hotel, español, ciudad
  • Written accent mark (á é í ó ú) always shows the stressed syllable: café, teléfono.
