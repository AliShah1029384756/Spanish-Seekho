Formal vs Informal (bahut zaroori)

Spanish mein do tareeqe hain naam poochhne ke:

  • ¿Cómo te llamas? → dost, umar mein barabar, ya informal situation
  • ¿Cómo se llama? → bade log, strangers, office, formal situation (usted form)

Jawab dono cases mein same rehta hai: Me llamo + naam.

Spain pronunciation tip

In much of central and northern Spain, the letter z and c before e/i are pronounced like the English “th” in “think”. In many other Spanish-speaking regions they sound like “s”. Both are recognised varieties. The Urdu and Roman Urdu guides above are only approximations.

PART 2 · SEE IT IN A CONVERSATION

A short introduction (informal)

A:

¡Hola! Buenos días.

Hello! Good morning.
B:

¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?

Hi! What is your name?
A:

Me llamo Ali. ¿Y tú?

My name is Ali. And you?
B:

Me llamo Sara. ¡Mucho gusto!

My name is Sara. Nice to meet you!

“¿Y tú?” means “And you?” and is a friendly, informal way to ask the same question back.

Formal version (office / older person)

A:

Buenos días. ¿Cómo se llama?

Good morning. What is your name? (formal)
B:

Me llamo Sara. Mucho gusto.

My name is Sara. Nice to meet you.
A:

Mucho gusto. Me llamo Ali.

Nice to meet you. My name is Ali.
