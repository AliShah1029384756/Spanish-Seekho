Adjectives & descriptions.

Ab aap seekhenge ke Spanish mein kisi shakhs, jagah ya cheez ko kaise describe karte hain. Adjective ko noun ke gender aur number ke saath match karna bhi seekhein.

PART 1 · WHAT IS AN ADJECTIVE?

Adjective kya hota hai?

Adjective woh word hota hai jo noun ki quality, size, colour ya condition batata hai. Jaise: bara ghar, achhi kitab, lamba larka. Spanish mein adjective aksar noun ke baad aata hai.

una casa grandea big houseRoman Urdu: OO-na KA-sa GRAN-de
un libro interesantean interesting bookRoman Urdu: oon LEE-bro een-te-re-SAN-te
un chico altoa tall boyRoman Urdu: oon CHEE-ko AL-to
una chica altaa tall girlRoman Urdu: OO-na CHEE-ka AL-ta
Basic word order

Aksar simple descriptions mein noun pehle aur adjective baad mein aata hai: una mesa pequeña (a small table). Kuch common adjectives noun se pehle bhi aa sakte hain; unhein baad mein alag se seekhenge.

PART 2 · ADJECTIVES ENDING IN -O

Gender aur number ke saath badlein

Jo adjective -o par end hota hai, uski aam tor par chaar forms hoti hain. Noun masculine/feminine aur singular/plural ho to adjective bhi us se match karta hai.

Masculine singular

un chico alto

a tall boy
Feminine singular

una chica alta

a tall girl
Masculine plural

unos chicos altos

some tall boys
Feminine plural

unas chicas altas

some tall girls
Pattern

alto → alta → altos → altas. Feminine ke liye -o aksar -a banta hai; plural ke liye -s add hota hai.

PART 3 · ADJECTIVES ENDING IN -E OR A CONSONANT

Har adjective ka gender nahi badalta

Kuch adjectives masculine aur feminine dono nouns ke saath same rehte hain. Lekin singular/plural ka farq phir bhi ho sakta hai.

un chico inteligentean intelligent boy
una chica inteligentean intelligent girl
un hombre jovena young man
una mujer jovena young woman
chicos inteligentes / mujeres jóvenesintelligent boys / young women
Yaad rakhein

inteligente aur joven ka masculine/feminine form same hai. Plural mein inteligentes aur jóvenes hota hai.

PART 5 · DESCRIBE WITH SER

Ser + adjective

Kisi ki relatively stable quality ya identity describe karne ke liye ser use hota hai. Is lesson mein es (he/she/it is) aur son (they are) par focus karein.

Person

Mi hermano es alto.

My brother is tall.
Personality

Ana es simpática.

Ana is friendly.
Plural

Mis amigos son amables.

My friends are kind.
Things

La casa es grande.

The house is big.
Colour

El coche es rojo.

The car is red.
Ser yaad rakhein

es = is (singular) · son = are (plural). Adjective noun ke gender aur number ke mutabiq match karega: La casa es blanca. Los coches son rojos.

PART 6 · SHORT CONVERSATION

Describe your family

A:

¿Cómo es tu hermano?

What is your brother like?
B:

Es alto y simpático.

He is tall and friendly.
A:

¿Y tu hermana?

And your sister?
B:

Es inteligente y trabajadora.

She is intelligent and hard-working.
A:

¿Cómo son tus padres?

What are your parents like?
B:

Son amables y trabajadores.

They are kind and hard-working.
