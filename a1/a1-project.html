A1 · END-OF-LEVEL MODULE 26

Your first day in Spain.

Imagine karein aap Málaga mein apna pehla din guzar rahe hain. Aap ko introduce hona, café mein order dena, rasta poochna, shopping aur basic help leni hai. Har task khud Spanish mein complete karne ki koshish karein.

Practical project5 real-life tasksSpeaking + writing
PROJECT BRIEF

Your situation

Aap Spain mein naye hain. Aap ke paas phone, thora cash aur apna address hai. Aaj aap ko shehar samajhna aur zaroori kaam karne hain. Neeche ke prompts ko apni information se complete karein.

Project goal: Har task mein understandable, simple Spanish use karein. Perfect grammar zaroori nahi; clear meaning aur polite communication important hai.
TASK 1 · INTRODUCE YOURSELF

Meet a neighbour

Apna naam, country, language aur ek simple personal detail batayein.

Hola, me llamo ____. Soy de Pakistán y hablo urdu e inglés.Hello, my name is ____. I am from Pakistan and I speak Urdu and English.
TASK 2 · ORDER AT A CAFÉ

Ask for food and drink

Ek drink aur ek food item order karein. Price poochhein ya total samjhein.

Useful language

Quisiera un café y un bocadillo, por favor. ¿Cuánto cuesta?

I would like a coffee and a sandwich, please. How much does it cost?

TASK 3 · FIND A PLACE

Ask for directions

Pharmacy ya bus stop tak pohanchne ke liye politely rasta poochhein aur direction samjhein.

Useful language

Perdone, ¿dónde está la farmacia? ¿Está lejos?

Excuse me, where is the pharmacy? Is it far?

Siga todo recto y gire a la derecha.

Go straight ahead and turn right.

TASK 4 · BUY SOMETHING

Shop for an everyday item

Ek item, quantity aur payment method batayein. Zaroorat ho to receipt maangein.

Useful language

Quisiera un litro de agua. ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta? ¿Me da el recibo, por favor?

I would like a litre of water. Can I pay by card? Can I have the receipt, please?

TASK 5 · ASK FOR BASIC HELP

Explain a simple need

Pharmacy ya public place mein apni basic need clear karein. Medical decision ya medicine ki dosage khud specify na karein.

Useful language

Perdone, no me siento bien. ¿Puede ayudarme? ¿Dónde está el centro de salud?

Excuse me, I do not feel well. Can you help me? Where is the health centre?

PROJECT REFLECTION

Review your performance

  • I can say the key phrases without reading every word.
  • I can adapt the examples with my own name, food, place and needs.
  • I can ask someone to repeat or speak more slowly: ¿Puede repetir, por favor? ¿Más despacio, por favor?

Apne answers dobara parhein, phir aloud bol kar practice karein. Project ka model answer copy karna zaroori nahi.
