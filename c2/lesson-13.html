CEFR C2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 13

Managing spontaneous, high-stakes discussion

Time pressure aur high-stakes discussion mein complex ideas ko organise, clarify aur respond karein without losing precision or composure.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Spontaneous contributions ko coherent structure dein.
  • Pressure mein clarification, turn management aur repair strategies use karein.
  • Strong disagreement aur uncertainty ko calm, precise language se handle karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Prepare a flexible mental structure

Spontaneous ka matlab unstructured nahi. Claim, reason, evidence aur implication ka mental sequence ready rakhein; notes ko script na banayein.

Permítanme ordenar la cuestión en dos puntos antes de responder.Allow me to organise the issue into two points before responding.
La respuesta breve es afirmativa, aunque conviene introducir una precisión.The short answer is yes, although one qualification is worth adding.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Manage turns and interruptions

Turn ko enter karne ke liye si me permite, quisiera añadir aur antes de pasar a otro punto use karein. Interruptions ko politely reclaim karein.

Si me permite, quisiera matizar algo que se ha dicho hace un momento.If you allow me, I would like to qualify something that was said a moment ago.
Antes de pasar a otro punto, convendría aclarar esta distinción.Before moving to another point, it would be worth clarifying this distinction.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Clarify under pressure

Question ambiguous ho to clarify karein, lekin evasive na lagen. Scope confirm karein aur phir direct answer dein.

¿Se refiere al coste inicial o al coste total a lo largo del proyecto?Are you referring to the initial cost or the total cost over the project?
Si he entendido bien, la pregunta se centra en la viabilidad a corto plazo.If I have understood correctly, the question focuses on short-term feasibility.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Respond to challenge without escalation

Challenge ko personal attack na samjhein. Evidence, premise ya scope par respond karein; tone measured rakhein.

Entiendo la objeción, pero creo que parte de una premisa discutible.I understand the objection, but I think it starts from a debatable premise.
No cuestiono la intención, sino la solidez de la inferencia.I am not questioning the intention, but the strength of the inference.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Repair and reformulate

Agar wording unclear ho to self-correct karein: mejor dicho, lo que quiero decir es, para ser más preciso. Repair confidence ka sign hai.

Mejor dicho, no se trata de eliminar el riesgo, sino de reducirlo a un nivel aceptable.More precisely, the aim is not to eliminate the risk but to reduce it to an acceptable level.
Déjeme reformularlo para evitar una posible confusión.Let me rephrase that to avoid a possible misunderstanding.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Close with a clear position

Discussion close karte waqt agreed points, unresolved issue aur next step summarise karein. Consensus fabricate na karein.

Creo que hemos aclarado el alcance, aunque sigue pendiente acordar el calendario.I think we have clarified the scope, although agreeing on the timetable remains outstanding.
Propongo que dejemos constancia de los dos puntos de acuerdo y de la cuestión pendiente.I suggest we record the two points of agreement and the outstanding issue.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

5-minute spoken briefing ka script likhein jo ek challenging question, interruption, clarification request aur disagreement handle kare. End par agreed points aur unresolved decision recap karein.
