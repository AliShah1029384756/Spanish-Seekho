CEFR C2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 21

Independent research and rigorous source evaluation

Independent C2-level research mein research question refine karein, sources triangulate karein aur uncertainty ko transparent rakhein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Focused research questions aur defensible scope formulate karein.
  • Primary and secondary sources ki authority, methods aur relevance evaluate karein.
  • Evidence ko triangulate karke transparent conclusions develop karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Formulate a researchable question

Question specific, answerable aur bounded ho. Scope, timeframe, population aur key concepts define karein.

La pregunta debe delimitar el periodo analizado y los criterios de inclusión.The question should define the period analysed and the inclusion criteria.
Conviene distinguir el problema general de la cuestión concreta que puede investigarse.It is useful to distinguish the general problem from the specific question that can be researched.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Build a source strategy

Primary sources direct evidence deti hain; secondary sources interpret karti hain. Source choice research question aur discipline ke mutabiq karein.

Las fuentes primarias permiten examinar los datos originales, mientras que las secundarias ofrecen interpretaciones.Primary sources allow examination of original data, whereas secondary sources offer interpretations.
La selección bibliográfica responde a la pregunta de investigación, no a la facilidad de acceso.The bibliography is selected in response to the research question, not simply because sources are easy to access.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Evaluate authority and method

Author expertise, publication process, method transparency, sample, conflicts of interest aur date assess karein. Reputation alone enough nahi.

La reputación de la revista no exime de examinar el diseño del estudio.The journal's reputation does not remove the need to examine the study design.
El informe describe el procedimiento, pero no explica cómo se seleccionaron los participantes.The report describes the procedure but does not explain how participants were selected.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Triangulate evidence

Independent sources, methods aur datasets compare karein. Agreement confidence barha sakta hai, lekin shared bias bhi possible hai.

La convergencia de fuentes independientes refuerza la interpretación, aunque no elimina todas las limitaciones.Convergence across independent sources strengthens the interpretation, although it does not remove every limitation.
Los testimonios y los datos cuantitativos ofrecen perspectivas complementarias, no intercambiables.Testimonies and quantitative data offer complementary, not interchangeable, perspectives.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Manage uncertainty and reproducibility

Uncertainty ko quantify ya qualify karein. Definitions, decisions, exclusions aur source trail document karein so another researcher can follow.

Los resultados deben interpretarse dentro del margen de incertidumbre indicado.The results should be interpreted within the stated margin of uncertainty.
Se documentaron los criterios de selección para facilitar la revisión del procedimiento.The selection criteria were documented to facilitate review of the procedure.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Synthesize a defensible conclusion

Conclusion question ka answer de, evidence strength bataye, alternative explanations acknowledge kare aur future research need identify kare.

La evidencia disponible respalda esta interpretación, aunque sería necesario replicar el análisis con otra muestra.The available evidence supports this interpretation, although the analysis would need to be replicated with another sample.
La conclusión es provisional y queda abierta a revisión si aparecen nuevos datos.The conclusion is provisional and remains open to revision if new data emerge.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek focused research question choose karein. 350-word mini research brief banayein: scope, 3 source types, evaluation criteria, triangulated finding, one limitation, alternative explanation aur provisional conclusion.
