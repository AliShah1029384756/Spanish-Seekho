CEFR C2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

Precision, ambiguity and subtle distinctions

C2 par exact wording, claim ki strength aur context ki bareek boundaries ko control karein.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Near-synonyms ko register, implication aur strength ke mutabiq distinguish karein.
  • Ambiguity ko zaroorat par resolve ya deliberately preserve karein.
  • Evidence se zyada strong conclusion express kiye baghair precise likhein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Meaning beyond dictionary definitions

Afirmar, sostener, alegar aur reconocer related hain, magar speaker ka stance aur evidence relation alag hai.

El portavoz afirmó que no se habían ocultado datos.The spokesperson stated that no data had been concealed.
La autora sostiene que el cambio fue gradual, no inevitable.The author maintains that the change was gradual, not inevitable.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Degree and scope

Prácticamente, en buena medida, apenas aur no del todo claim ka scope badalte hain; ye interchangeable intensifiers nahi.

La reforma apenas modificó el procedimiento inicial.The reform barely changed the initial procedure.
La propuesta es viable, al menos en principio.The proposal is viable, at least in principle.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Semantic boundaries

Eficaz outcome achieve karta hai; eficiente resources ko well use karta hai. Relevante significance, pertinente contextual suitability batata hai.

El tratamiento resultó eficaz, aunque no especialmente eficiente.The treatment proved effective, although not especially efficient.
Su observación era pertinente, pero no decisiva.Her observation was relevant to the matter, but not decisive.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Controlled ambiguity

Ambiguity kabhi defect aur kabhi diplomatic, legal ya literary restraint hoti hai. Reader ko enough context dein, unsupported certainty nahi.

El comité revisará el informe con los especialistas externos.The committee will review the report with the external specialists.
La medida podría afectar a los centros que aún no han abierto.The measure could affect the centres that have not yet opened.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Evidence-calibrated wording

Demostrar, confirmar, sugerir aur apuntar a evidence ki different strengths hain. Conclusion ko evidence se aage na le jayein.

Los indicios apuntan a una revisión de los criterios, pero no la demuestran.The indications point to a review of the criteria, but do not prove it.
La muestra permite observar una tendencia, no establecer una regla universal.The sample allows us to observe a trend, not establish a universal rule.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Editing for exactness

Audience, genre aur purpose check karein. Academic text mein precision, public communication mein clarity aur negotiation mein tact important hai.

Conviene distinguir entre una limitación del método y un fallo del equipo.It is important to distinguish a limitation of the method from a failure by the team.
La conclusión es provisional y deberá contrastarse con nuevos datos.The conclusion is provisional and will need to be checked against new data.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

250–300 alfaaz ka analytical commentary likhein. Ek contested claim choose karein, chaar near-synonyms ko context ke saath distinguish karein, uncertainty calibrate karein aur apni wording choices justify karein.
