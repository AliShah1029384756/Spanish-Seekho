CEFR C2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 23

Integrated listening, reading, mediation and interaction

Multiple C2 skills ko integrated tasks mein combine karein: receive information, evaluate it, mediate it and respond spontaneously.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Listening aur reading se relevant evidence extract aur compare karein.
  • Complex information ko audience ke liye mediate karein.
  • Interaction mein clarification, challenge aur synthesis fluently manage karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Receive information across modes

Lecture, interview, report aur discussion different cues provide karte hain. Notes mein claims, evidence, stance and uncertainty separate rakhein.

La ponencia aporta el marco conceptual, mientras que el informe ofrece datos comparables.The presentation provides the conceptual framework, while the report offers comparable data.
En la entrevista, la persona entrevistada cuestiona una de las premisas del estudio.In the interview, the interviewee questions one of the study's premises.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Compare and integrate sources

Sources ko shared question ke around compare karein. Contradiction, complementary detail and missing evidence identify karein.

Las dos fuentes coinciden en la tendencia, pero difieren en la explicación.The two sources agree on the trend but differ in the explanation.
El testimonio complementa los datos, aunque no permite generalizar por sí solo.The testimony complements the data, although it does not allow generalisation on its own.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Mediate for a specific audience

Complex source ko concise explanation mein reframe karein, audience need aur key caveats retain karein.

Para resumirlo sin perder el matiz, el informe identifica una mejora, pero no demuestra su causa.To summarise without losing the nuance, the report identifies an improvement but does not demonstrate its cause.
La cuestión principal para el equipo es qué decisión permite tomar esta evidencia.The main question for the team is what decision this evidence allows them to make.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Respond and clarify in real time

Interaction mein listener stance check karein, clarify request karein aur response ko evidence ke mutabiq qualify karein.

¿Podrías precisar si esa cifra corresponde al conjunto de la muestra o solo a un subgrupo?Could you clarify whether that figure refers to the whole sample or only to a subgroup?
Si he entendido bien, tu objeción se refiere a la validez externa del estudio.If I have understood correctly, your objection concerns the study's external validity.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Challenge without derailing

Challenge specific assumption or evidence, not person. Offer alternative interpretation and invite response.

No cuestiono el dato en sí, sino la interpretación que se extrae de él.I am not questioning the figure itself, but the interpretation drawn from it.
¿Qué evidencia permitiría distinguir entre esas dos explicaciones?What evidence would allow us to distinguish between those two explanations?
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Synthesize and act

Integrated task ko actionable synthesis se close karein: what is known, uncertain, disputed and decision-relevant.

La evidencia respalda una intervención piloto, siempre que se evalúen sus efectos antes de ampliarla.The evidence supports a pilot intervention, provided that its effects are evaluated before expansion.
Quedan dos cuestiones abiertas que deberán resolverse antes de adoptar una decisión definitiva.Two questions remain open and must be resolved before a final decision is adopted.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Integrated simulation: 5-minute Spanish audio/presentation aur 2 short texts se notes banayein; 250-word synthesis likhein; non-specialist audience ko 120-word briefing dein; phir 3-minute discussion mein one claim challenge aur clarify karein.
