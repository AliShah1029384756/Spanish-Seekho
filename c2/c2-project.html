Practical C2 communication project

Plan and deliver a substantial real-world communication project, from research and stakeholder analysis to public explanation, negotiation and revision.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Apply advanced skills to an authentic communication task.
  • Evaluate evidence, audience needs and the limits of your own performance.
  • Plan next steps using observable evidence.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Choose a meaningful brief

Select a complex issue such as a research finding, policy proposal or organisational change. Define the decision and audience.

El proyecto abordará un problema concreto y formulará una propuesta viable.The project will address a specific problem and formulate a viable proposal.
El proyecto abordará un problema concreto y formulará una propuesta viable.The project will address a specific problem and formulate a viable proposal.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Map stakeholder needs

Identify specialists, decision-makers, affected communities and the wider public. Adapt the level of detail to each audience.

La comunidad afectada requiere una explicación clara de las consecuencias prácticas.The affected community needs a clear explanation of the practical consequences.
La comunidad afectada requiere una explicación clara de las consecuencias prácticas.The affected community needs a clear explanation of the practical consequences.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Research and substantiate

Use attributable sources, compare methods and disclose uncertainty. Link each major claim to evidence.

Cada afirmación sustantiva debe poder vincularse con una fuente identificable.Each substantive claim must be traceable to an identifiable source.
Cada afirmación sustantiva debe poder vincularse con una fuente identificable.Each substantive claim must be traceable to an identifiable source.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Produce multiple outputs

Create a detailed report and a plain-language brief. Adapt terminology, not the underlying facts.

Ambas versiones mantienen las mismas reservas sobre la calidad de los datos.Both versions retain the same reservations about data quality.
Ambas versiones mantienen las mismas reservas sobre la calidad de los datos.Both versions retain the same reservations about data quality.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Present and negotiate

Deliver an oral briefing, invite challenge, clarify objections and negotiate a practical next step.

La propuesta podría ponerse a prueba mediante una fase piloto.The proposal could be tested through a pilot phase.
La propuesta podría ponerse a prueba mediante una fase piloto.The proposal could be tested through a pilot phase.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Evaluate and revise

Gather feedback, document changes and explain remaining risks.

El informe final identifica también las cuestiones que requieren seguimiento.The final report also identifies the issues that require follow-up.
El informe final identifica también las cuestiones que requieren seguimiento.The final report also identifies the issues that require follow-up.
PART 7 · CAPSTONE TASK

Put the learning together

Project pack: 1,000-word specialist report, 300-word public brief, five-minute oral presentation, stakeholder Q&A record, source log and 250-word reflection. Use a real topic and label assumptions.
