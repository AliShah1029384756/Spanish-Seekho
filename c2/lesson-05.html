CEFR C2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 05

Synthesising multiple long spoken and written sources

Mukhtalif long sources ko combine karte hue authorship, evidence, disagreement aur shared themes preserve karein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Multiple sources se central themes aur complementary evidence identify karein.
  • Contradictions aur differences ko flatten kiye baghair integrate karein.
  • A coherent synthesis banayein jo attribution aur uncertainty preserve kare.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Synthesis is not a sequence of summaries

Har source ko alag retell karna synthesis nahi. Themes organise karein, sources ke beech relations show karein aur repeated information ko compress karein.

Ambos informes coinciden en que el acceso mejoró, aunque discrepan sobre sus causas.Both reports agree that access improved, although they disagree about its causes.
La entrevista aporta la perspectiva de los usuarios, mientras que el estudio cuantifica la tendencia.The interview provides users' perspective, while the study quantifies the trend.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Build a thematic structure

Source order follow karne ke bajaye themes ke mutabiq organise karein: consensus, divergence, evidence gaps aur implications.

En conjunto, las fuentes describen una mejora desigual entre regiones.Taken together, the sources describe an uneven improvement across regions.
Un primer tema común es la falta de criterios comparables.A first shared theme is the lack of comparable criteria.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Attribute claims precisely

Attribution se reader ko pata chalta hai kis source ne kya kaha. Segun, de acuerdo con, el estudio observa aur la entrevistada cuestiona ko accurately use karein.

Según el análisis longitudinal, el cambio fue gradual.According to the longitudinal analysis, the change was gradual.
La entrevistada cuestiona que esa mejora se haya distribuido por igual.The interviewee questions whether that improvement was distributed equally.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Handle contradiction without forcing agreement

Contradiction ko mistake assume na karein. Different samples, dates, definitions ya methods conflicting results explain kar sakte hain.

La encuesta registra una subida, mientras que los datos administrativos muestran estabilidad.The survey records an increase, whereas administrative data show stability.
La discrepancia podría deberse a que ambos estudios miden fenómenos distintos.The discrepancy may be due to the two studies measuring different phenomena.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Integrate spoken and written evidence

Interview, lecture aur report different evidence types provide karte hain. Spoken material mein stance, hesitation aur context bhi note karein.

La ponente matiza la conclusión al reconocer que la muestra es limitada.The speaker qualifies the conclusion by acknowledging that the sample is limited.
El documento técnico confirma la tendencia, pero no explica la experiencia cotidiana.The technical document confirms the trend but does not explain everyday experience.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Write a coherent synthesis

Synthesis mein shared claim, source-specific contribution, disagreement aur cautious conclusion combine karein. Attribution ko throughout maintain karein.

Las fuentes apuntan a una mejora real, aunque su alcance sigue siendo objeto de debate.The sources point to a real improvement, although its extent remains debated.
La evidencia disponible permite formular una conclusión provisional, no definitiva.The available evidence allows a provisional conclusion, not a definitive one.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Teen sources (ek interview, ek report aur ek analytical article) se 350–400 alfaaz ki synthesis likhein. Kam az kam do shared themes, ek contradiction, attribution aur evidence limitation include karein.
