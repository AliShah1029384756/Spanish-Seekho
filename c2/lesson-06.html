CEFR C2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

Reconstructing arguments and identifying hidden assumptions

Complex arguments ko claims, premises, warrants aur conclusions mein reconstruct karein; hidden assumptions aur reasoning gaps identify karein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Complex argument ki logical structure reconstruct karein.
  • Explicit premises aur unstated assumptions identify karein.
  • Evidence, causal claims aur conclusions ke beech reasoning gaps evaluate karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Map the argument

Argument ko main conclusion, supporting premises, evidence aur intermediate claims mein divide karein. Rhetorical confidence logical validity ka substitute nahi.

La conclusión depende de dos premisas que el autor apenas justifica.The conclusion depends on two premises that the author barely justifies.
El dato respalda la primera afirmación, pero no la conclusión general.The figure supports the first claim, but not the overall conclusion.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Identify warrants

Warrant woh bridge assumption hai jo evidence ko conclusion se connect karta hai. Aksar writer is bridge ko explicit nahi karta.

El argumento presupone que una mayor inversión producirá necesariamente mejores resultados.The argument assumes that greater investment will necessarily produce better results.
Esa inferencia solo sería válida si las condiciones fueran comparables.That inference would only be valid if the conditions were comparable.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Find hidden assumptions

Definite wording, category labels aur causal verbs assumptions hide kar sakte hain. Poochhein: alternative explanation kya ho sakti hai?

La propuesta da por sentado que todos los centros parten de la misma situación.The proposal takes for granted that all centres start from the same position.
El análisis excluye, sin justificarlo, otras variables relevantes.The analysis excludes other relevant variables without justifying the decision.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Distinguish correlation and causation

Temporal sequence ya correlation cause prove nahi karti. Confounders, selection effects aur reverse causality consider karein.

La correlación observada no permite concluir que una variable cause la otra.The observed correlation does not allow us to conclude that one variable causes the other.
También cabe la posibilidad de que la relación opere en sentido inverso.It is also possible that the relationship works in the opposite direction.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Test counterarguments

Strong evaluation best counterargument ko fairly reconstruct karti hai, phir evidence aur scope ke mutabiq response deti hai.

La objeción más sólida cuestiona la representatividad de la muestra.The strongest objection questions the representativeness of the sample.
La respuesta del autor aborda ese punto, aunque deja abierta la cuestión del sesgo de selección.The author's response addresses that point, although it leaves the issue of selection bias open.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Evaluate the inferential leap

Conclusion premises se follow karti hai ya extra assumption chahiye? C2 analysis mein logical gap ko precise language se explain karein.

De las premisas se desprende una posibilidad, no una necesidad.The premises support a possibility, not a necessity.
El razonamiento resulta coherente, siempre que se acepte la premisa inicial.The reasoning is coherent, provided that the initial premise is accepted.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek complex opinion article ka argument map banayein: main claim, teen premises, evidence, hidden warrant, counterargument aur final judgement. 300–350 alfaaz mein reasoning ka strongest aur weakest link explain karein.
