Beyond C2: expert practice and lifelong development plan

C2 is not an endpoint. Build a sustainable plan for specialist vocabulary, genre expertise, intercultural practice and continued critical engagement.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Apply advanced skills to an authentic communication task.
  • Evaluate evidence, audience needs and the limits of your own performance.
  • Plan next steps using observable evidence.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Choose a specialist direction

Select a domain that matters to your work or interests: research, law, technology, education, culture or public communication.

La especialización debe responder a objetivos reales y a contextos de uso concretos.Specialisation should respond to real objectives and concrete contexts of use.

PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Build a domain corpus

Collect authentic reports, lectures, interviews, debates and professional documents. Track terminology and collocations.

PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Maintain receptive expertise

Read widely and listen to varied speakers. Compare sources and revisit difficult material.

PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Develop productive practice

Write for real audiences, participate in discussions and seek feedback from competent speakers or domain experts.

La práctica mejora cuando existe un destinatario real y una finalidad comunicativa clara.Practice improves when there is a real recipient and a clear communicative purpose.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Track change

Set monthly outputs and quarterly reviews. Track precision, range and interactional flexibility.

No basta con acumular horas; es necesario observar cambios en el desempeño.Accumulating hours is not enough; changes in performance need to be observed.
No basta con acumular horas; es necesario observar cambios en el desempeño.Accumulating hours is not enough; changes in performance need to be observed.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Plan eight weeks

Weeks 1–2 build a corpus; 3–4 read/listen; 5–6 produce; 7 seek feedback and revise; 8 reflect and set targets.

El plan combina recepción, producción, interacción y revisión.The plan combines reception, production, interaction and review.
El plan combina recepción, producción, interacción y revisión.The plan combines reception, production, interaction and review.
PART 7 · CAPSTONE TASK

Put the learning together

Write an eight-week plan with one specialist domain, one authentic reading, one listening source, one written output, one spoken interaction and one feedback activity each week. Define measurable indicators and a review date.
