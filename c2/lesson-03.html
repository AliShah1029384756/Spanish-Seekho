Idiom, metaphor, humour and wordplay

Figurative language ko translate karne ke saath tone, cultural reference, social risk aur intended effect bhi interpret karein.

PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Idioms and fixed expressions

Idioms ka meaning individual words se predict nahi hota. Echar balones fuera responsibility avoid karna; estar en el ajo kisi matter mein involved hona.

Cuando le preguntaron por los plazos, echó balones fuera.When asked about the deadlines, he dodged the question.
Parece que todos estaban en el ajo menos nosotros.It seems everyone was in on it except us.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Metaphor and conceptual framing

Metaphor abstract idea ko concrete image se frame karti hai. Un callejón sin salida dead end; sembrar dudas doubt ko planted object banata hai.

La negociación ha entrado en un callejón sin salida.The negotiation has reached a dead end.
Sus declaraciones sembraron dudas sobre la versión oficial.His statements sowed doubts about the official account.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Irony and intended stance

Irony mein literal praise actual stance se opposite ho sakta hai. Voice, timing aur shared knowledge ke baghair sarcasm assume na karein.

¡Menuda puntualidad! Llegas media hora tarde.Some punctuality! You arrive half an hour late.
Claro, porque improvisar a última hora siempre sale fenomenal.Of course, because improvising at the last minute always works brilliantly.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Wordplay and double meaning

Juego de palabras sound, polysemy ya double reading par depend karta hai. Pun preserve na ho to function explain karein; fake equivalent invent na karein.

El banco no concede crédito, pero sí mucha letra pequeña.The bank does not grant trust, but certainly provides plenty of fine print.
No es lo mismo tener tiempo que dar tiempo al tiempo.Having time is not the same as giving time to time.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Humour, face and context

Humour relationship aur power dynamics se linked hai. Workplace ya unfamiliar audience mein teasing misfire kar sakti hai.

La broma funcionó entre amigos, pero habría resultado inoportuna en la reunión.The joke worked among friends but would have been out of place in the meeting.
Prefirió rebajar la tensión con una observación ligera, no con sarcasmo.She preferred to ease the tension with a light remark, not sarcasm.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Explaining figurative effect

Image kya foreground karti hai, kaun sa emotion create karti hai aur kya connotation chhorti hai? C2 learner metaphor explain aur evaluate kar sakta hai.

La metáfora presenta el cambio como una travesía incierta, no como una solución inmediata.The metaphor presents change as an uncertain journey, not an immediate solution.
El juego de palabras contrapone el sentido literal y el figurado de la expresión.The wordplay contrasts the literal and figurative meanings of the expression.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

3-minute commentary ka script likhein: 3 idioms, 2 metaphors aur 1 ironic line explain karein. Har item ka literal sense, intended meaning, tone aur audience suitability discuss karein.
