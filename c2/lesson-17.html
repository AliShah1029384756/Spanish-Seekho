CEFR C2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 17

Advanced sociolinguistic variation and identity

Spanish ko social, regional aur situational variation ke zariye analyse karein, avoiding stereotypes and treating speakers as individuals.

  • Language variation ko context, identity, audience aur interaction se relate karein.

  • Variation ko evidence aur context ke saath describe karein, stereotypes ke baghair.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Understand variation as systematic

Language variation random error nahi. Speakers region, age, community, setting aur purpose ke mutabiq choices karte hain.

La variación lingüística responde a factores sociales, geográficos y situacionales.Linguistic variation responds to social, geographical and situational factors.
Una misma persona puede alternar entre distintos registros según el contexto.The same person may shift between different registers depending on context.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Distinguish key concepts

Dialect regional/social variety hai; sociolect social group se associated patterns; idiolect individual usage; register situation-based choice.

El término 'idiolecto' se refiere a los rasgos propios del uso lingüístico de una persona.The term 'idiolect' refers to features characteristic of an individual's language use.
El registro depende de la situación comunicativa, no solo del tema tratado.Register depends on the communicative situation, not only on the topic being discussed.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Analyse identity and stance

Language choices belonging, solidarity, distance, authority ya resistance signal kar sakti hain, magar single feature se identity assume na karein.

La elección de determinadas formas puede expresar cercanía o marcar distancia social.The choice of certain forms can express closeness or mark social distance.
No conviene atribuir una identidad concreta a un hablante a partir de un único rasgo.It is unwise to attribute a particular identity to a speaker on the basis of a single feature.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Describe regional variation accurately

Spain mein pronunciation, vocabulary aur usage regional variation rakhte hain. Region ke andar bhi speakers differ karte hain.

El seseo está extendido en amplias zonas de España y de América, aunque su distribución es diversa.Seseo is widespread across large areas of Spain and the Americas, although its distribution is diverse.
La distribución de una forma no coincide necesariamente con fronteras administrativas.The distribution of a form does not necessarily coincide with administrative boundaries.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Avoid deficit judgements

Standard variety institutional contexts mein useful convention hai, linguistic superiority ka proof nahi. Non-standard variety ko deficient label na karein.

Una variedad estándar cumple funciones de referencia, pero no convierte a las demás en formas inferiores.A standard variety serves reference functions, but does not make other varieties inferior.
La adecuación de una forma depende del contexto y de las expectativas comunicativas.The appropriateness of a form depends on context and communicative expectations.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Use careful analytical language

Observed data, interpretation aur limitation separate rakhein. Frequency claim ko representative sample ke baghair universal na banayein.

En esta muestra se observa una preferencia, aunque no puede generalizarse a toda la comunidad.A preference is observed in this sample, although it cannot be generalised to the entire community.
Los resultados sugieren una asociación, no una relación causal demostrada.The results suggest an association, not a demonstrated causal relationship.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek Spanish-language interview ya media sample analyse karein. 300 alfaaz mein speaker context, register, three variation features, possible identity/stancetaking aur limits of inference describe karein.
