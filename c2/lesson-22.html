CEFR C2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 22

Producing extended, nuanced and audience-aware discourse

Extended C2 discourse mein complex ideas ko coherent architecture, controlled emphasis and audience-sensitive language se deliver karein.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Long-form discourse ko clear architecture aur purposeful progression dein.
  • Nuance, qualification aur emphasis ko coherent paragraphs mein manage karein.
  • Audience aur genre ke mutabiq tone, detail aur terminology adapt karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Architect the whole discourse

Extended text ka route map banayein: purpose, thesis, sections, evidence, counterposition and conclusion. Structure reader ke liye visible ho.

La exposición se organiza en torno a tres preguntas que conducen a una conclusión común.The presentation is organised around three questions that lead to a shared conclusion.
Cada sección cumple una función distinta dentro del argumento general.Each section serves a distinct function within the overall argument.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Control information density

Dense information ko manageable units mein divide karein. Key concept define karein, then build complexity stepwise.

Antes de introducir la excepción, conviene establecer la regla general.Before introducing the exception, it is useful to establish the general rule.
La explicación gana claridad cuando cada término técnico se define en su primera aparición.The explanation becomes clearer when each technical term is defined at first use.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Express degrees of certainty

Modal verbs, adverbs and evidential phrases se confidence calibrate karein. Strong claim ke liye strong support zaroori hai.

Los indicios apuntan a una explicación plausible, aunque no permiten descartarlo todo.The indications point to a plausible explanation, although they do not allow every alternative to be ruled out.
Cabe sostener esta hipótesis con cautela, a falta de datos concluyentes.This hypothesis can be maintained cautiously in the absence of conclusive data.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Manage emphasis and contrast

Fronting, parallelism, concession aur sentence rhythm focus direct karte hain. Emphasis ko argument ki hierarchy se match karein.

Lo decisivo no es la cantidad de información, sino la calidad de su interpretación.What matters is not the quantity of information, but the quality of its interpretation.
Por relevante que sea el resultado, no debe confundirse con una explicación causal.However relevant the result may be, it should not be confused with a causal explanation.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Adapt to audience and genre

Expert audience ko method detail chahiye; public audience ko definitions and implications. Genre conventions ko follow karein without flattening voice.

Para un público especializado, podemos precisar el alcance metodológico de la muestra.For a specialist audience, we can specify the methodological scope of the sample.
En una comunicación pública, interesa explicar primero qué consecuencias prácticas tiene el hallazgo.In public communication, it is useful to explain first what practical consequences the finding has.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Deliver a coherent ending

Conclusion key insights synthesize kare, implications bataye aur open questions separate kare. New evidence introduce na kare.

En conjunto, los resultados permiten responder a la pregunta inicial, aunque dejan abierta una línea de investigación.Taken together, the results allow us to answer the initial question, although they leave one line of research open.
La conclusión no cierra el debate; establece con mayor precisión qué sabemos y qué falta por comprobar.The conclusion does not close the debate; it establishes more precisely what we know and what remains to be tested.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

600-word long-form discourse likhein on a complex issue. Clear thesis, section architecture, 3 evidence-linked points, calibrated certainty, counterposition, audience-aware terminology aur synthesis conclusion include karein.
