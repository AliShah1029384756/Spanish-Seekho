CEFR C2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

Implication, inference, presupposition and allusion

Explicit meaning ke saath hidden assumptions, implied meanings aur cultural references ko context ke zariye interpret karein.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Explicit content ko inference aur presupposition se separate karein.
  • Particles, framing aur rhetorical questions ke interpretive effect ko identify karein.
  • Allusion aur indirect meaning samjhein, unsupported assumptions se bachein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Explicit and inferred meaning

Explicit content sentence directly kehta hai. Inference context aur wording se nikalti hai; plausible inference guaranteed fact nahi.

No descartamos revisar el calendario si cambian las circunstancias.We are not ruling out revising the timetable if circumstances change.
El director volvió a prometer transparencia.The director promised transparency again.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Presupposition

Presupposition background information ko accepted treat karti hai. Volver a, dejar de, seguir aur definite descriptions assumptions trigger kar sakte hain.

La comisión dejó de publicar los datos mensuales.The commission stopped publishing the monthly data.
¿Cuándo volverán a reunirse los representantes?When will the representatives meet again?
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Implicature and indirect replies

Ya veremos open possibility bhi ho sakta hai aur polite refusal bhi. Tone, relationship aur surrounding turns communicative force shape karte hain.

Ya veremos si encontramos una fecha que nos convenga a todos.We will see whether we can find a date that suits everyone.
No digo que la propuesta carezca de mérito.I am not saying that the proposal has no merit.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Framing and loaded questions

Question ka framing assumptions set kar sakta hai. Poochhein: hidden premise kya hai, kya foreground hua aur kya omit hua?

La empresa insiste en hablar de ajustes, no de recortes.The company insists on speaking of adjustments, not cuts.
¿Por qué se retrasó otra vez la publicación del informe?Why was the publication of the report delayed yet again?
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Allusion and shared knowledge

Allusion kisi text, event ya cultural reference ki taraf indirect ishara hai. Reference unfamiliar ho to extra resonance miss ho sakti hai.

La reunión terminó convertida en un auténtico laberinto kafkiano.The meeting ended up becoming a truly Kafkaesque labyrinth.
No necesitamos otro caballo de Troya en el debate.We do not need another Trojan horse in the debate.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Testing interpretations

Lexical trigger, surrounding turns, genre, relationship aur alternative readings check karein. Possible implication ko fact ki tarah present na karein.

Su respuesta deja entrever cierta reserva, aunque no constituye una negativa.Her response hints at reservations, although it is not a refusal.
El texto parece aludir a una crisis anterior, pero no la identifica expresamente.The text seems to allude to an earlier crisis, but does not identify it explicitly.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

300-word discourse analysis banayein. Chhe Spanish statements ke explicit meaning, possible presupposition/implicature aur at least one alternative interpretation identify karein.
