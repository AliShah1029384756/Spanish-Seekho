CEFR C2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 15

Negotiating disagreement and reconciling perspectives

Conflicting priorities ko clarify karein, shared interests identify karein aur workable agreements negotiate karein without pretending all differences disappear.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Positions ke peeche interests aur constraints identify karein.
  • Disagreement ko reframe karke options aur trade-offs explore karein.
  • Conditional proposals aur explicit agreements formulate karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Separate positions from interests

Position woh demand hai jo party state karti hai; interest underlying need ya concern. Interest identify karna new options create kar sakta hai.

La empresa pide más flexibilidad; el equipo busca previsibilidad en los horarios.The company asks for more flexibility; the team seeks predictability in schedules.
El desacuerdo parece centrarse en el plazo, pero también afecta a la distribución del riesgo.The disagreement seems to centre on the deadline, but also concerns the distribution of risk.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Find shared criteria

Shared criteria—cost, fairness, safety, feasibility—discussion ko personal preferences se objective comparison tak le jate hain.

Podríamos comparar las alternativas con los mismos criterios de coste y viabilidad.We could compare the alternatives using the same cost and feasibility criteria.
Ambas partes consideran prioritario mantener la calidad del servicio.Both sides consider maintaining service quality a priority.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Reframe the disagreement

False binary avoid karein. Ask if both goals can be met through staged implementation, pilot, review point ya alternative allocation.

Quizá no tengamos que elegir entre rapidez y control si establecemos fases diferenciadas.Perhaps we do not have to choose between speed and control if we establish separate phases.
La cuestión podría reformularse como una distribución gradual de responsabilidades.The issue could be reframed as a gradual distribution of responsibilities.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Make conditional proposals

Conditional proposal commitment aur safeguard combine karti hai. Conditions measurable aur agreed hon; vague assurances se bachein.

Estaríamos dispuestos a ampliar el plazo siempre que se revisen los hitos intermedios.We would be willing to extend the deadline provided that the interim milestones are reviewed.
Podemos aceptar la primera fase a cambio de una evaluación independiente.We can accept the first phase in exchange for an independent evaluation.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Record trade-offs and commitments

Agreement mein who, what, when, conditions aur review mechanism record karein. Concession ka cost aur expected benefit clear ho.

El acuerdo contempla una reducción inicial del alcance y una revisión en seis semanas.The agreement provides for an initial reduction in scope and a review in six weeks.
Cada parte asumirá una responsabilidad concreta y comunicará cualquier desviación.Each party will take on a specific responsibility and report any deviation.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Handle unresolved differences

Har negotiation full consensus par end nahi hoti. Record remaining disagreement, escalation path aur decision owner honestly.

No hemos resuelto la cuestión presupuestaria, pero sí hemos acordado cómo evaluarla.We have not resolved the budget issue, but we have agreed how to evaluate it.
La diferencia queda pendiente de decisión por parte del comité responsable.The difference remains pending a decision by the responsible committee.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Do parties ke darmiyan 350-word negotiation brief likhein. Dono ki positions aur interests map karein, shared criteria choose karein, two conditional options propose karein aur final commitments plus unresolved issues record karein.
