Comprehensive C2 review and gap repair

Audit advanced skills, identify recurring gaps and build a targeted repair cycle from evidence in your own work.

Learning outcomes

  • Review listening, reading, writing, speaking and mediation separately.
  • Diagnose recurring issues in precision, register, cohesion and pragmatic interpretation.
  • Build a measurable practice and revision cycle.

1. Audit skills separately

Review receptive, productive and interactive performance. Strength in one area does not prove equal control in every area.

Mi rendimiento es más consistente en la argumentación escrita que en la interacción espontánea.

My performance is more consistent in written argumentation than in spontaneous interaction.

2. Diagnose recurring gaps

Group issues by cause: lexical precision, collocation, syntax, register, cohesion, pragmatic inference or pronunciation.

Conviene distinguir entre un error ocasional y una dificultad sistemática.

It is useful to distinguish an occasional error from a systematic difficulty.

3. Repair complex comprehension

Revisit demanding audio and texts; separate explicit information, implication, stance, evidence and uncertainty.

Al volver a escuchar el fragmento, se aprecia una reserva que había pasado inadvertida.

On listening again, a reservation that had gone unnoticed becomes apparent.

4. Refine production and interaction

Revise an extended text and record an oral response. Improve structure, spontaneous repair and responses to challenge.

La segunda versión desarrolla mejor la objeción y delimita la conclusión con mayor precisión.

The second version develops the objection better and defines the conclusion more precisely.

5. Review mediation and pragmatics

Explain a specialist concept to a public audience and analyse an ambiguous exchange without stereotyping.

La explicación mantiene las limitaciones del estudio sin trasladar toda la terminología especializada.

The explanation preserves the study's limitations without transferring all the specialist terminology.

6. Build a repair cycle

Set a measurable target, choose practice sources, produce evidence, obtain feedback and repeat.

El progreso se valorará mediante muestras comparables y criterios explícitos.

Progress will be assessed through comparable samples and explicit criteria.

Capstone task

Prepare a C2 evidence audit: one complex listening task, one critical reading synthesis, one 700-word argument, one recorded oral defence and one mediation brief. Identify three recurring gaps and design a four-week repair cycle.

Checkpoint

This module is assessed through your evidence portfolio and reflection. Use the course progress tracker after reviewing all sections.
