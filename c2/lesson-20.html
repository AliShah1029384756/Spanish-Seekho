CEFR C2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 20

Adapting language across regions, communities and contexts

Language ko region, community, channel, audience and purpose ke mutabiq adapt karein while preserving clarity and respect.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Regional and community variation ke mutabiq lexical choices adapt karein.
  • Spoken, written and digital communication ke conventions manage karein.
  • Audience adaptation mein authenticity, clarity aur respect balance karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Choose region-aware vocabulary

Vocabulary regional meanings aur preferences rakh sakti hai. Audience location identify karein aur unclear terms ko explain karein.

Antes de distribuir el material, conviene comprobar si el vocabulario resulta natural para el público destinatario.Before distributing the material, it is worth checking whether the vocabulary sounds natural to the target audience.
Cuando el término varía según la región, podemos añadir una aclaración breve.When the term varies by region, we can add a brief clarification.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Adapt spoken and written channels

Spoken interaction mein turn-taking, repair aur prosody matter karte hain; formal writing mein structure, punctuation aur explicit links.

En una presentación oral, la información debe organizarse para facilitar la escucha.In an oral presentation, information should be organised to make listening easier.
En el informe escrito, conviene explicitar las relaciones que en la conversación aporta el contexto.In the written report, it is useful to make explicit the relationships that context supplies in conversation.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Adapt digital communication

Digital channel mein brevity, thread context, accessibility aur permanence consider karein. Emoji ya abbreviation audience aur purpose ke mutabiq.

Un mensaje breve no tiene por qué ser ambiguo si identifica claramente la acción solicitada.A brief message need not be ambiguous if it clearly identifies the requested action.
En un canal público, conviene evitar datos personales y referencias que solo entiende un grupo reducido.In a public channel, it is advisable to avoid personal data and references understood only by a small group.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Balance inclusion and naturalness

Inclusive wording choose karte waqt audience conventions, clarity aur institutional guidance consider karein. One formula har context mein fit nahi hoti.

La formulación debe ser inclusiva y, al mismo tiempo, comprensible para el público al que se dirige.The wording should be inclusive and at the same time understandable to the intended audience.
Podemos reformular el texto sin perder precisión ni recurrir a soluciones artificiales.We can rephrase the text without losing precision or resorting to artificial solutions.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Manage multilingual interaction

Code-switching, translation aur borrowed terms ko interactional purpose ke saath interpret karein. Har switch ko lack of competence na samjhein.

El cambio de lengua puede responder a una necesidad terminológica o a una estrategia de identidad.A language switch may respond to a terminological need or an identity strategy.
La traducción debe aclarar el concepto, no borrar la perspectiva de quien lo formula.Translation should clarify the concept, not erase the perspective of the person expressing it.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Review adaptation with the audience

Feedback se check karein ke message clear, respectful aur actionable hai. Adaptation ko stereotypes ke bajaye audience evidence se guide karein.

La versión final se revisó con hablantes del público destinatario para detectar ambigüedades.The final version was reviewed with speakers from the target audience to detect ambiguities.
La adaptación funciona si facilita la comprensión sin caricaturizar ninguna variedad.The adaptation works if it facilitates understanding without caricaturing any variety.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 180-word public information notice ko teen versions mein adapt karein: formal Spain-based institutional audience, general Spanish-speaking online audience aur short spoken announcement. Differences justify karein.
