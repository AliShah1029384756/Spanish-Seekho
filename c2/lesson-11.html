CEFR C2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

High-level professional and academic correspondence

Formal correspondence mein complex requests, disagreement, negotiation aur academic exchange ko precise, tactful aur audience-aware language se handle karein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Professional aur academic correspondence ko purpose aur relationship ke mutabiq structure karein.
  • Requests, disagreement aur follow-up ko tactfully but clearly express karein.
  • Register, attribution aur action points ko consistent rakhein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Structure the correspondence

Subject, opening, purpose, relevant context, requested action aur close ko clear order mein rakhein. Recipient ko action identify karna easy ho.

Me dirijo a usted para solicitar una aclaración sobre los criterios de evaluación.I am writing to request clarification regarding the assessment criteria.
Le agradecería que confirmara si la documentación adjunta es suficiente.I would be grateful if you could confirm whether the attached documentation is sufficient.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Make precise requests

Quisiera saber si, sería posible que aur le agradecería que formal requests ko soften karte hain, lekin action aur deadline clear rakhein.

¿Sería posible recibir una respuesta antes del viernes?Would it be possible to receive a response before Friday?
Le agradeceríamos que indicara qué versión del documento debe tomarse como referencia.We would appreciate it if you could indicate which version of the document should be used as the reference.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Disagree without obscuring the point

Tactful disagreement mein shared ground, specific concern aur reasoned alternative include karein. Over-softening se position unclear na ho.

Coincidimos en la necesidad de revisar el procedimiento, aunque discrepamos sobre el calendario propuesto.We agree on the need to review the procedure, although we disagree about the proposed timetable.
Respetuosamente, consideramos que esa interpretación no tiene en cuenta la última modificación.Respectfully, we consider that interpretation does not take the latest amendment into account.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Negotiate responsibilities and deadlines

Negotiation mein conditional proposals, feasible alternatives aur responsibilities specify karein. Vague commitment ko concrete next step banayein.

Podríamos asumir la primera fase, siempre que se confirme la disponibilidad de los datos.We could take on the first phase, provided that data availability is confirmed.
Si le parece, fijemos una fecha de revisión y asignemos las tareas pendientes.If you agree, let us set a review date and assign the outstanding tasks.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Handle follow-up and correction

Follow-up concise aur factual ho. Delay acknowledge karein, correction clearly state karein aur revised file/version identify karein.

Le escribo para dar seguimiento a la consulta que envié la semana pasada.I am writing to follow up on the enquiry I sent last week.
Adjunto la versión corregida; la anterior debe considerarse obsoleta.I attach the corrected version; the previous one should be considered obsolete.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Close with clarity and courtesy

Close recipient ke next action aur timeline ko recap kar sakta hai. Register formal rahe, magar unnecessarily archaic na ho.

Quedo a la espera de su confirmación para proceder con el siguiente paso.I look forward to your confirmation so that I can proceed with the next step.
Gracias de antemano por su tiempo y por las aclaraciones que pueda facilitarnos.Thank you in advance for your time and for any clarification you can provide.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 220–280 word formal email likhein: disputed assessment criterion par clarification maangein, apna reasoned disagreement explain karein, workable alternative propose karein aur clear response date request karein.
