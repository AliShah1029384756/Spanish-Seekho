CEFR C2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 10

Writing nuanced essays, reviews and critical commentary

Complex position ko coherent essay, review ya critical commentary mein develop karein, with a clear thesis, controlled evaluation and precise evidence.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Essay aur review ka structure purpose aur genre ke mutabiq organise karein.
  • Evaluation ko evidence, criteria aur qualified judgement ke saath support karein.
  • Complex ideas ko coherent paragraphs aur accurate transitions se develop karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Choose the right genre architecture

Essay argument ko develop karta hai; review work ko criteria ke mutabiq evaluate karta hai; commentary language, framing ya assumptions analyse kar sakti hai.

El ensayo examina las consecuencias a largo plazo de la reforma.The essay examines the long-term consequences of the reform.
La reseña valora tanto la solidez del argumento como la claridad de la exposición.The review assesses both the strength of the argument and the clarity of the presentation.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Write purposeful introductions

Introduction context establish kare, issue narrow kare aur thesis announce kare. Generic opening ya unsupported grand claim avoid karein.

Este análisis parte de una pregunta concreta: quién asume el coste de la transición.This analysis starts from a specific question: who bears the cost of the transition?
El propósito de este comentario es evaluar cómo se construye esa imagen.The purpose of this commentary is to assess how that image is constructed.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Develop analytical paragraphs

Paragraph ka central claim, evidence, interpretation aur link to thesis clear ho. Quote ko explain karein; quote khud analysis nahi.

El ejemplo resulta significativo porque revela una contradicción interna del argumento.The example is significant because it reveals an internal contradiction in the argument.
Este pasaje refuerza la tesis, aunque también introduce una reserva importante.This passage strengthens the thesis, although it also introduces an important qualification.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Evaluate with explicit criteria

Review mein criteria define karein: originality, coherence, evidence, usability, style. Personal preference ko reasoned evaluation se separate karein.

La obra destaca por su originalidad, pero pierde precisión en el desarrollo final.The work stands out for its originality but loses precision in its final development.
La valoración depende, en buena medida, del criterio que se priorice.The assessment depends, to a large extent, on which criterion is prioritised.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Manage counterpoints and transitions

Transitions logical relation show karein: contrast, qualification, consequence, concession. Paragraphs ko mechanical connectors se overload na karein.

Ahora bien, esa ventaja no elimina las dificultades de aplicación.That said, this advantage does not eliminate implementation difficulties.
A la luz de esta limitación, conviene reconsiderar la conclusión inicial.In light of this limitation, it is worth reconsidering the initial conclusion.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Conclude without repetition

Conclusion argument ko synthesize kare, implication bataye aur unresolved question acknowledge kare. Intro ko word-for-word repeat na karein.

En definitiva, el valor de la propuesta reside menos en su novedad que en su viabilidad.Ultimately, the proposal's value lies less in its novelty than in its feasibility.
Queda pendiente determinar si los resultados se mantienen en otros contextos.It remains to be determined whether the results hold in other contexts.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 500-word critical review ya essay likhein. Genre choose karein, explicit evaluation criteria use karein, 3 evidence-linked paragraphs develop karein aur conclusion mein ek unresolved issue acknowledge karein.
