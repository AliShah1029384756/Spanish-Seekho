CEFR C2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

Editing for precision, rhythm, coherence and audience

Advanced editing ko surface proofreading se aage le jayein: information flow, cohesion, rhythm, reference aur audience impact refine karein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Ambiguous reference, redundancy aur structural weakness identify karein.
  • Sentence rhythm aur information order ko purpose ke mutabiq refine karein.
  • Meaning, evidence aur authorial voice preserve karte hue text edit karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Edit at three levels

Macro editing structure aur argument; paragraph editing progression; sentence editing grammar, reference aur rhythm dekhti hai. Har level ko alag pass mein check karein.

Primero conviene revisar la estructura; después, la cohesión entre párrafos.It is best to review the structure first, then cohesion between paragraphs.
La corrección formal no compensa una argumentación mal organizada.Formal proofreading does not compensate for poorly organised reasoning.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Resolve ambiguous reference

Pronouns aur demonstratives ka antecedent clear ho. Este, aquello aur lo anterior ka reference reader ko guess na karwayein.

La comisión presentó dos alternativas, pero la segunda no resolvía el problema de fondo.The committee presented two alternatives, but the second did not solve the underlying problem.
Ese planteamiento resulta insuficiente porque no define a quién corresponde la decisión.That approach is insufficient because it does not define who is responsible for the decision.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Remove redundancy without losing nuance

Repeated ideas compress karein, lekin qualifications, contrast aur attribution preserve karein. Concise writing necessarily short sentences nahi hoti.

La medida es provisional y, por tanto, deberá revisarse más adelante.The measure is provisional and will therefore need to be reviewed later.
El informe ofrece una explicación parcial, no una respuesta completa.The report offers a partial explanation, not a complete answer.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Control rhythm and emphasis

Sentence length vary karein. Fronting, parallelism aur punctuation reader focus guide kar sakte hain; overly ornate syntax clarity reduce karti hai.

Una cosa conviene dejar clara: los resultados no son definitivos.One point should be made clear: the results are not definitive.
No es la rapidez lo que está en cuestión, sino la fiabilidad del proceso.What is at issue is not speed, but the reliability of the process.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Strengthen cohesion and progression

Reference, lexical chains aur transitions se paragraphs connect karein. Connectors ko logical relationship ke mutabiq select karein, decoration ke liye nahi.

Esta limitación condiciona la interpretación y explica la cautela de los autores.This limitation shapes the interpretation and explains the authors' caution.
A partir de ahí, el análisis pasa de describir el problema a valorar sus posibles soluciones.From that point, the analysis moves from describing the problem to assessing possible solutions.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Preserve voice and audience fit

Edit karte hue author ki stance aur intended audience preserve karein. Har text ko same formal style mein flatten na karein.

La versión revisada conserva el tono cercano, pero elimina las ambigüedades.The revised version keeps the approachable tone but removes ambiguities.
Para un público no especializado, conviene explicar el término antes de utilizarlo.For a non-specialist audience, the term should be explained before it is used.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 350-word draft ko edit karein. Pehle structure, phir paragraph cohesion, phir sentence-level precision review karein. 8 edits ka before/after log banayein aur har change ka effect explain karein.
