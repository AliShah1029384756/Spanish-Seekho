PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Understand the assessment design

Several tasks are used because C2 ability is multidimensional. One quiz cannot establish a complete proficiency profile.

La evaluación combina tareas distintas para obtener una visión más amplia del desempeño.The assessment combines different tasks to obtain a broader view of performance.

PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Listening and critical reading

Analyse a complex talk and long text. Record claims, evidence, stance, implication and uncertainty.

La lectura crítica exige valorar tanto el contenido como la forma en que se construye la evidencia.Critical reading evaluates both content and how evidence is constructed.
La lectura crítica exige valorar tanto el contenido como la forma en que se construye la evidencia.Critical reading evaluates both content and how evidence is constructed.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Extended written production

Write a structured response with a position, evidence, counterargument, qualification and conclusion.

El texto debe desarrollar una línea argumental coherente.The text should develop a coherent line of argument.

PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Spoken interaction

Present a position and answer challenging follow-up questions. Clarify, reformulate and concede where appropriate.

Una rectificación bien formulada puede reforzar la credibilidad del argumento.A well-formulated correction can strengthen the argument's credibility.
Una rectificación bien formulada puede reforzar la credibilidad del argumento.A well-formulated correction can strengthen the argument's credibility.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Mediation and pragmatics

Explain a specialist finding to a public audience and interpret an ambiguous exchange contextually.

La mediación debe conservar las reservas metodológicas en un lenguaje accesible.Mediation should preserve methodological reservations in accessible language.
La mediación debe conservar las reservas metodológicas en un lenguaje accesible.Mediation should preserve methodological reservations in accessible language.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Score transparently

Evaluate task completion, coherence, range, precision, interaction, mediation and register using concrete evidence.

La valoración debe apoyarse en muestras concretas y criterios previamente definidos.Assessment should be based on concrete samples and explicit criteria.
La valoración debe apoyarse en muestras concretas y criterios previamente definidos.Assessment should be based on concrete samples and explicit criteria.
PART 7 · CAPSTONE TASK

Put the learning together

Complete a self-assessment pack: listening/reading notes, 700-word argument, five-minute oral presentation plus Q&A, 250-word mediation brief and a rubric-based reflection. Keep the evidence and scoring rationale.
