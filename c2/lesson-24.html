CEFR C2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 24

C2 capstone: defending a complex position with precision

Final core capstone: research, synthesis, argument, mediation and spontaneous defence ko ek coherent C2-level performance mein combine karein.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Complex position ko multi-source evidence aur transparent reasoning se defend karein.
  • Counterarguments, uncertainty aur limitations ko precisely address karein.
  • Written, spoken and interactive outputs ko audience aur purpose ke mutabiq integrate karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Define the issue and thesis

Complex issue ko narrow karke defensible thesis formulate karein. Key terms define aur scope explicitly delimit karein.

La tesis que defenderé es que la reforma puede ser eficaz, siempre que se acompañe de mecanismos de evaluación independientes.The thesis I will defend is that the reform can be effective, provided it is accompanied by independent evaluation mechanisms.
El análisis se limita a los efectos observados durante el periodo estudiado.The analysis is limited to the effects observed during the period studied.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Build a multi-source evidence base

Research, source evaluation and synthesis ko combine karein. Source roles aur methods transparently explain karein.

La encuesta permite identificar tendencias, mientras que las entrevistas ayudan a comprender las experiencias subyacentes.The survey makes it possible to identify trends, while interviews help explain the underlying experiences.
Ninguna fuente basta por sí sola; la interpretación depende de la convergencia y de sus límites.No single source is sufficient on its own; the interpretation depends on convergence and its limits.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Defend the reasoning

Evidence se claim tak inferential bridge explain karein. Correlation, causation and alternative explanations separate rakhein.

La relación observada es compatible con la hipótesis, aunque no demuestra por sí misma una causalidad directa.The observed relationship is compatible with the hypothesis, although it does not by itself demonstrate direct causation.
La explicación alternativa merece consideración, pero encaja peor con el conjunto de los datos.The alternative explanation deserves consideration, but fits the overall data less well.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Address the strongest counterargument

Counterargument ko fairly present karein, its valid insight acknowledge karein aur explain karein why thesis remains qualifiedly stronger.

La objeción más relevante señala el coste de aplicación, una limitación que no debe minimizarse.The most relevant objection points to implementation cost, a limitation that should not be minimised.
Aun así, el coste puede gestionarse mediante una aplicación gradual y criterios de revisión explícitos.Even so, the cost can be managed through gradual implementation and explicit review criteria.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Mediate the position for different audiences

Specialist version method detail include kare; public version implications explain kare. Core claim aur caveat dono preserve karein.

Para el equipo técnico, la cuestión central es la validez del diseño y la calidad de los indicadores.For the technical team, the central issue is the validity of the design and the quality of the indicators.
Para la ciudadanía, importa explicar qué cambiará, quién se verá afectado y cómo se medirán los resultados.For the public, it matters to explain what will change, who will be affected and how results will be measured.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Deliver a defensible final judgement

Final judgement claim, evidence, limitation and next step connect kare. Overclaim nahi; reasoned confidence show karein.

En definitiva, la propuesta está justificada como intervención evaluable, no como solución universal.Ultimately, the proposal is justified as an evaluable intervention, not as a universal solution.
La solidez de esta conclusión dependerá de que los resultados se publiquen y puedan ser examinados independientemente.The strength of this conclusion will depend on results being published and open to independent scrutiny.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

C2 capstone portfolio: 700-word evidence-led position paper, 180-word public summary, 5-minute oral defence outline, strongest counterargument and response, source log, uncertainty statement and self-reflection. Treat this as learning evidence, not official CEFR certification.
