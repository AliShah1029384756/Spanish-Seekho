CEFR C2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 14

Mediating complex ideas across audiences and registers

Specialist knowledge ko non-specialists, decision-makers aur mixed audiences tak faithfully relay karein without oversimplifying or distorting.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Complex concepts ko audience ke prior knowledge ke mutabiq reframe karein.
  • Technical detail aur accessible explanation ke darmiyan balance rakhein.
  • Multiple speakers ki positions ko fairly relay aur compare karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Diagnose the audience

Audience ki prior knowledge, purpose aur decision needs assess karein. Same concept har group ko same detail mein explain karna zaroori nahi.

Para un público general, conviene empezar por la consecuencia práctica.For a general audience, it is best to start with the practical consequence.
El equipo técnico necesita conocer el margen de error y las condiciones de medición.The technical team needs to know the margin of error and the measurement conditions.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Explain without distortion

Analogy helpful hai magar limits state karein. Simplification essential conditions aur uncertainty remove nahi karni chahiye.

Podríamos compararlo con un filtro, aunque la analogía tiene sus límites.We could compare it to a filter, although the analogy has its limits.
Dicho de otro modo, el sistema prioriza ciertos datos, pero no decide por sí solo.In other words, the system prioritises certain data, but does not make decisions on its own.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Layer information

Pehle plain-language overview, phir mechanism, evidence aur caveats. Audience ko detail mein invite karein without overwhelming them.

En términos sencillos, el resultado depende de tres factores que explicaré por separado.In simple terms, the result depends on three factors that I will explain separately.
Si desean profundizar, podemos revisar después los supuestos del modelo.If you wish to go deeper, we can review the model's assumptions afterwards.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Relay positions fairly

Mediation mein speakers ki positions accurately attribute karein. Disagreement ko neutralise na karein aur apni interpretation ko source ke naam par na rakhein.

El equipo sostiene que el plazo es viable; los usuarios, en cambio, cuestionan su accesibilidad.The team maintains that the deadline is feasible; users, by contrast, question its accessibility.
Ambas partes coinciden en el objetivo, pero difieren en los medios para alcanzarlo.Both sides agree on the goal but differ on the means of achieving it.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Translate between registers

Specialist term ko preserve karein jab zaroori ho, saath plain-language equivalent dein. Public-facing wording technical meaning ko distort na kare.

La 'latencia', es decir, el retraso entre la solicitud y la respuesta, afecta a la experiencia.Latency, that is, the delay between the request and the response, affects the experience.
En el documento formal se mantiene el término técnico y se define en su primera aparición.The technical term is retained in the formal document and defined at first mention.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Check shared understanding

Mediation ke baad audience se paraphrase, questions ya decision summary ke zariye understanding confirm karein. Silence ko agreement na samjhein.

Para comprobar que me he explicado bien, ¿podrían resumir el cambio principal?To check that I have explained it clearly, could you summarise the main change?
Antes de cerrar, confirmemos qué decisión corresponde a cada equipo.Before closing, let us confirm which decision belongs to each team.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek specialist concept ko teen audiences ke liye explain karein: technical team, public audience aur senior decision-makers. Har version 120–150 words ho; key facts aur caveats consistent rakhein.
