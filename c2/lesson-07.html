CEFR C2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

Evaluating specialised and unfamiliar material

Unfamiliar specialist material ko strategic reading, terminology tracking aur evidence evaluation ke zariye samjhein — har detail translate karna zaroori nahi.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Specialised texts ka purpose, structure aur key claims rapidly identify karein.
  • Unknown terminology ko context, morphology aur reference se infer karein.
  • Evidence quality aur authorial limits ko assess karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Survey before reading deeply

Title, abstract, headings, figures aur conclusion scan karein. Pehle text ka map banayein, phir relevant sections detail se parhein.

El resumen delimita el alcance del estudio y anticipa sus principales limitaciones.The abstract defines the scope of the study and previews its main limitations.
La sección metodológica permite valorar cómo se obtuvieron los resultados.The methods section allows the reader to assess how the results were obtained.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Infer specialist terminology

Unknown term ko prefix, suffix, root aur surrounding explanation se infer karein. Guess ko provisional rakhein aur key term verify karein.

El término se define en el glosario antes de emplearse en el análisis.The term is defined in the glossary before being used in the analysis.
Por el contexto, la expresión parece referirse a una medida indirecta.From the context, the expression appears to refer to an indirect measure.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Track definitions and scope

Same word disciplines mein different meaning rakh sakta hai. Author ki operational definition aur text ke scope ko follow karein.

En este estudio, 'resiliencia' se limita a la capacidad de recuperación institucional.In this study, 'resilience' is limited to the capacity for institutional recovery.
La definición adoptada no debe extrapolarse a otros contextos sin cautela.The adopted definition should not be extended to other contexts without caution.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Evaluate methods and evidence

Sample, measurement, comparison group, uncertainty aur reproducibility inspect karein. Technical vocabulary strong methodology ki guarantee nahi.

La muestra es amplia, pero no representa necesariamente a toda la población.The sample is large, but does not necessarily represent the entire population.
Los autores reconocen que la medición indirecta introduce cierto margen de error.The authors acknowledge that indirect measurement introduces a margin of error.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Separate expertise from authority

Specialist expertise relevant hoti hai, lekin claim ko source, method aur evidence se evaluate karein. Expert status har conclusion ko unquestionable nahi banata.

La experiencia del equipo aporta contexto, aunque no sustituye la validación independiente.The team's experience provides context, although it does not replace independent validation.
La conclusión debe valorarse a la luz de los datos y del procedimiento empleado.The conclusion must be assessed in light of the data and the procedure used.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Read strategically under uncertainty

Jab full comprehension possible na ho, main question, central claim, key terms aur evidence limits extract karein. Irrelevant detail par time waste na karein.

Aunque desconozcamos algunos términos, podemos reconstruir la tesis principal.Even if we do not know some terms, we can reconstruct the main thesis.
La información disponible basta para identificar el argumento central, no todos sus matices.The available information is enough to identify the central argument, not all its nuances.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek unfamiliar specialist Spanish text choose karein. 250–300 alfaaz mein purpose, central claim, five key terms, method/evidence, one limitation aur confidence level report karein.
