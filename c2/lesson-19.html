CEFR C2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 19

Intercultural interpretation and critical perspectives

Intercultural communication ko critical lens se analyse karein: perspective, power, representation, assumptions and context.

C2 highly proficientRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Cultural claims ko situated perspectives aur evidence ke through evaluate karein.
  • Stereotypes, essentialism aur unequal power relations identify karein.
  • Intercultural misunderstanding ko multiple interpretations ke saath analyse karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Treat culture as dynamic

Culture fixed national personality nahi. Practices generation, region, profession, class, migration aur individual experience se vary karti hain.

Las prácticas culturales cambian y se reinterpretan en función de las circunstancias.Cultural practices change and are reinterpreted according to circumstances.
No existe una única forma de comunicarse que represente a toda una sociedad.There is no single way of communicating that represents an entire society.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Identify essentialism and stereotypes

Statements jo poori community ko same traits assign karein essentialist hain. Ask who is represented, who is excluded and what evidence supports claim.

La afirmación generaliza una experiencia particular a una comunidad muy diversa.The statement generalises one particular experience to a highly diverse community.
Conviene distinguir entre una tendencia documentada y un estereotipo repetido sin pruebas.It is important to distinguish a documented tendency from a stereotype repeated without evidence.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Analyse perspective and power

Who gets to define normal, professional ya appropriate? Institutional authority aur unequal access narratives shape kar sakte hain.

La norma presentada como neutral refleja también la perspectiva de la institución que la establece.The norm presented as neutral also reflects the perspective of the institution that establishes it.
El análisis debe preguntar quién puede intervenir y qué voces quedan fuera.The analysis should ask who can participate and which voices are left out.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Interpret misunderstandings relationally

Misunderstanding ko one party ki deficiency na banayein. Different assumptions, interactional norms, language proficiency and unequal power investigate karein.

El malentendido surgió de expectativas distintas, no necesariamente de una falta de cortesía.The misunderstanding arose from different expectations, not necessarily from a lack of courtesy.
Ambas partes interpretaron el silencio de manera diferente.Both parties interpreted the silence differently.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Avoid cultural determinism

Culture possible context provide karti hai, behaviour predict nahi karti. Individual agency aur situational factors include karein.

La referencia cultural ayuda a contextualizar la conducta, pero no la explica por completo.The cultural reference helps contextualise the behaviour but does not explain it completely.
No cabe deducir las intenciones individuales únicamente de la pertenencia grupal.Individual intentions cannot be inferred solely from group membership.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Develop reflexive interpretation

Apni assumptions aur standpoint ko acknowledge karein. Alternative readings compare karein aur evidence ke mutabiq conclusion qualify karein.

Mi interpretación parte de una perspectiva concreta y, por tanto, no agota las posibles lecturas.My interpretation starts from a particular perspective and therefore does not exhaust the possible readings.
Una explicación más sólida tendría que incorporar la experiencia de los participantes.A stronger explanation would need to incorporate the participants' experience.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek intercultural case study ka critical analysis likhein: competing interpretations, possible power imbalance, one stereotype/essentialist assumption, individual agency aur evidence limitations discuss karein.
