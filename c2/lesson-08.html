CEFR C2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

Producing concise, faithful and nuanced summaries

Long complex material ko concise summary mein compress karein — main argument, attribution, uncertainty aur meaningful disagreement preserve karte hue.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Central claims aur supporting details ko importance ke mutabiq rank karein.
  • Paraphrase karte waqt source meaning aur degree of certainty preserve karein.
  • Concise summaries likhein jo nuance aur attribution lose na karein.
PART 1 · C2 MASTERY

Find the central proposition

Summary ka start main proposition se ho, background detail se nahi. Supporting examples tab include karein jab claim samajhne ke liye zaroori hon.

El artículo sostiene que la reforma mejoró el acceso, pero no resolvió las desigualdades.The article argues that the reform improved access but did not resolve inequalities.
La autora distingue entre ampliar la cobertura y garantizar resultados equivalentes.The author distinguishes between expanding coverage and guaranteeing equivalent outcomes.
PART 2 · C2 MASTERY

Rank information by relevance

Main claim, key support, limitation aur consequence identify karein. Repetition, anecdotes aur minor examples compress karein.

El informe destaca tres factores y considera secundario el efecto del calendario.The report highlights three factors and treats the effect of the timetable as secondary.
Los datos regionales ilustran la tendencia, pero no alteran la conclusión principal.The regional data illustrate the trend but do not change the main conclusion.
PART 3 · C2 MASTERY

Paraphrase faithfully

Paraphrase lexical form change karta hai, meaning nahi. Scope, negation, attribution aur modality preserve karein.

El estudio no descarta una mejora, aunque la considera todavía incierta.The study does not rule out an improvement, although it still considers it uncertain.
Según los autores, la relación es probable, no concluyente.According to the authors, the relationship is probable, not conclusive.
PART 4 · C2 MASTERY

Preserve disagreement and uncertainty

Do sources disagree? Summary mein disagreement ko mention karein. Uncertainty ko confident declarative statement mein convert na karein.

Los expertos coinciden en el diagnóstico, pero discrepan sobre la respuesta más adecuada.The experts agree on the diagnosis but disagree about the most appropriate response.
La causa sigue sin determinarse, pese a las hipótesis planteadas.The cause remains undetermined despite the hypotheses proposed.
PART 5 · C2 MASTERY

Avoid distortion through compression

Short summary mein causal link invent na karein, qualifiers delete na karein aur source ki position ko apni opinion na banayein.

El texto sugiere una relación, pero no afirma que exista causalidad directa.The text suggests a relationship but does not claim that direct causation exists.
La propuesta podría ser viable si se cumplen las condiciones señaladas.The proposal could be viable if the stated conditions are met.
PART 6 · C2 MASTERY

Review the final summary

Check karein: source accurately represented? Main idea clear? Repetition removed? Nuance preserved? Summary original ke muqable mein concise hai?

En síntesis, la medida ofrece ventajas, aunque su eficacia depende de la aplicación.In summary, the measure offers advantages, although its effectiveness depends on implementation.
La síntesis conserva la tesis central y las reservas expresadas por el autor.The synthesis preserves the central thesis and the reservations expressed by the author.
PART 7 · INDEPENDENT PRODUCTION

Put the language to work

Ek 900-word source ko 130–160 words mein summarise karein. Central thesis, do supporting points, ek limitation aur source attribution preserve karein. Phir apni summary ko original se compare karke omissions justify karein.
