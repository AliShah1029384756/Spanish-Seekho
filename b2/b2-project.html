Practical project: deliver and defend a proposal

Professional ya academic setting mein aik realistic proposal tayyar karein, defend karein aur stakeholders ke liye clear summary dein.

PROJECT BRIEF

Deliver and defend a proposal

Aap aik educational, public ya workplace service ko improve karne ke liye proposal tayyar kar rahe hain. Aapko problem define karni hai, do options compare karne hain, evidence ko qualify karna hai, objections ka jawab dena hai aur implementation plan defend karna hai.

Your proposal should explain the learning need, compare two approaches and recommend a measurable pilot.

TASK 1 · DEFINE THE PROBLEM

Define the problem

Write 100–130 words. Explain who is affected, what currently happens and why change may be needed.

El problema principal consiste en…

TASK 2 · COMPARE TWO OPTIONS

Compare two options

Write 120–150 words. Compare two realistic options, benefits, costs or constraints. Do not invent real statistics; label assumptions.

Por una parte…; por otra…; una posible limitación…

TASK 3 · PRESENT YOUR EVIDENCE

Present your evidence

Write 100–130 words. Use two clearly labelled practice sources or observations, attribute each claim and explain limitations.

Según el primer documento…; el segundo sugiere…

TASK 4 · ANSWER A COUNTERARGUMENT

Answer a counterargument

Write 100–130 words. Present a fair objection, concede what is reasonable and respond with a qualified argument.

Es cierto que…; aun así…; convendría…

TASK 5 · WRITE THE FINAL PROPOSAL

Write the final proposal

Write 180–220 words. Include recommendation, pilot duration, responsibilities, success criteria, risks and review date.

Proponemos poner en marcha…; el equipo se encargará de…

TASK 6 · DEFEND AND MEDIATE

Defend and mediate

Prepare a 2–3 minute spoken defence. Then write a 70–90 word summary for a stakeholder who needs only the decision and next steps.

En resumen, recomendamos…; la decisión se revisará…

SAVE YOUR PROJECT

Review and mark your work

Drafts autosave on this device. Completion requires all six drafts to contain at least 45 words and all six self-checks to be ticked. The check confirms task completion, not Spanish accuracy.
