CEFR B2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 24

Integrated B2 communication: defend, synthesise and negotiate

Final core unit mein arguments, source synthesis, register aur negotiation ko ek realistic task mein combine karein — clear stance, fair evidence aur workable agreement ke saath.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Defend a nuanced position with reasons, evidence and qualified claims.
  • S synthesise contrasting information while attributing sources and limits.
  • Negotiate a practical outcome and adapt communication to the audience.
PART 1 · DEFINE THE ISSUE AND POSITION

Define the issue and position

Integrated communication mein pehle issue, audience aur purpose define karein. Main stance clear ho, lekin conditions aur limits bhi mention hon. Mi postura es…; sostengo que…; siempre que… useful structures hain.

Sostengo que la propuesta es viable, siempre que se revise el calendario.I maintain that the proposal is viable, provided the timetable is reviewed.
Mi postura no es rechazar el cambio, sino establecer unas condiciones realistas.My position is not to reject the change, but to establish realistic conditions.
PART 2 · BUILD A REASONED DEFENCE

Build a reasoned defence

Claim ko reason, example aur evidence se support karein. Dado que…; esto se debe a…; un ejemplo significativo es… Logical link banate hain. Unsupported certainty aur exaggerated claims avoid karein.

La medida merece apoyo, dado que los datos muestran una mejora sostenida.The measure deserves support, given that the data show a sustained improvement.
Un ejemplo significativo es la reducción de los tiempos de espera en dos centros.A significant example is the reduction in waiting times at two centres.
PART 3 · S SYNTHESISE CONTRASTING SOURCES

S synthesise contrasting sources

Sources compare karte waqt agreement, disagreement, method, scope aur date dekhein. Una fuente señala…, mientras que otra advierte… attribution deta hai. Contradiction ko hide na karein.

Una fuente destaca el ahorro económico, mientras que otra señala posibles costes sociales.One source highlights the financial savings, while another points to possible social costs.
Ambos informes coinciden en la tendencia, aunque utilizan periodos de referencia distintos.Both reports agree on the trend, although they use different reference periods.
PART 4 · QUALIFY CONCLUSIONS AND UNCERTAINTY

Qualify conclusions and uncertainty

Evidence limited ho to conclusion bhi limited rakhein: los datos sugieren…, no permiten afirmar…, es probable que… Certainty ko evidence ke mutabiq calibrate karein.

Los resultados sugieren una mejora, pero no permiten afirmar que la medida sea la única causa.The results suggest an improvement, but they do not allow us to claim that the measure is the only cause.
Es probable que el efecto varíe según el tamaño de cada organización.The effect is likely to vary according to the size of each organisation.
PART 5 · NEGOTIATE OPTIONS AND CONDITIONS

Negotiate options and conditions

Defend karna aur negotiate karna opposite nahi. Priorities identify karein, options compare karein aur conditional proposal dein. Si aceptamos…, a cambio…, podríamos acordar…

Si aceptamos una fase piloto, podríamos evaluar los resultados antes de ampliarla.If we accept a pilot phase, we could evaluate the results before expanding it.
A cambio de ampliar el plazo, el equipo se comprometería a presentar avances semanales.In exchange for extending the deadline, the team would commit to presenting weekly progress.
PART 6 · PRESENT A DECISION-READY OUTCOME

Present a decision-ready outcome

End par recommendation, conditions, responsibilities aur review criteria summarise karein. Audience ko next step samajh aana chahiye. En resumen…; proponemos…; la decisión debería revisarse…

En resumen, proponemos iniciar una prueba de tres meses con criterios de evaluación públicos.In summary, we propose starting a three-month trial with public evaluation criteria.
La decisión debería revisarse cuando dispongamos de los resultados del primer trimestre.The decision should be reviewed when we have the first-quarter results.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Prepare a 280–320-word briefing and a 2-minute spoken defence of a proposal to improve a public, educational or workplace service. Compare two short sources (you may create clearly labelled practice extracts), state a qualified position, address one counterargument, negotiate two conditions and finish with responsibilities plus review criteria. Attribute every source-based claim; do not invent real-world evidence.
