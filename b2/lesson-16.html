CEFR B2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 16

Workplace conflict, feedback and negotiation

Professional disagreement ko calmly handle karein, constructive feedback dein, interests identify karein aur workable agreements negotiate karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Describe a workplace problem without personal attacks or exaggeration.
  • Give specific, constructive feedback and respond to criticism professionally.
  • Negotiate priorities, conditions and compromises while recording clear agreements.
PART 1 · DESCRIBING A PROBLEM NEUTRALLY

Focus on facts and impact

Conflict ko blame se nahi, observable facts aur impact se explain karein. Se ha producido un retraso neutral hai; esto ha afectado a… impact; me preocupa que… concern introduce karta hai. Motive assume na karein.

Se ha producido un retraso en la entrega de los materiales.There has been a delay in delivering the materials.
Me preocupa que este cambio afecte al calendario de todo el equipo.I am concerned that this change may affect the whole team's schedule.
PART 2 · GIVING CONSTRUCTIVE FEEDBACK

Be specific and actionable

Feedback mein behaviour/example, impact aur next step include karein. He observado que…; cuando ocurre…, el resultado es…; sería útil que… Specific feedback personal label se zyada fair hota hai.

He observado que los informes llegan sin las cifras de referencia.I have noticed that the reports arrive without the reference figures.
Sería útil que incluyeras la fuente de cada dato en la próxima versión.It would be helpful if you included the source of each figure in the next version.
PART 3 · RECEIVING CRITICISM AND CLARIFYING

Listen before defending

Entiendo lo que dices, gracias por señalarlo aur ¿podrías darme un ejemplo concreto? constructive responses hain. Agar disagreement ho to Entiendo tu punto, aunque… use karein. Clarification defensiveness ko kam karti hai.

Gracias por comentarlo; revisaré el procedimiento y te enviaré una versión corregida.Thank you for mentioning it; I will review the procedure and send you a corrected version.
Entiendo tu punto, aunque creo que falta tener en cuenta la carga de trabajo actual.I understand your point, although I think the current workload also needs to be considered.
PART 4 · IDENTIFYING INTERESTS AND OPTIONS

Look beyond fixed positions

Position = jo banda maang raha hai; interest = us need ki wajah. ¿Qué es lo prioritario para cada parte? options brainstorm karein: redistribute tasks, extend deadline, reduce scope, add support. Options ko evaluate karne se pehle sun lein.

Para encontrar una solución, necesitamos entender qué plazo es realmente imprescindible.To find a solution, we need to understand which deadline is genuinely essential.
Podríamos repartir las tareas de otra manera y revisar la fecha de entrega.We could redistribute the tasks and review the delivery date.
PART 5 · NEGOTIATING A COMPROMISE

Make conditions explicit

Podríamos acordar… proposal; si aceptamos…, necesitaríamos… condition; a cambio de… exchange. Concession dono taraf realistic hon. Ambiguous promises ke bajaye measurable commitments rakhein.

Podríamos acordar una entrega parcial el miércoles y la versión completa el lunes.We could agree on a partial delivery on Wednesday and the complete version on Monday.
A cambio de ampliar el plazo, el equipo enviará una actualización diaria.In exchange for extending the deadline, the team will send a daily update.
PART 6 · CONFIRMING AND REVIEWING AGREEMENTS

Prevent the same conflict returning

Agreement mein who, what, when aur review date likhein. Para evitar malentendidos, confirmo que…; revisaremos el acuerdo el…; si surge algún problema, avisaremos con antelación. Written summary shared understanding banata hai.

Para evitar malentendidos, confirmo que yo revisaré los datos antes del jueves.To avoid misunderstandings, I confirm that I will review the figures before Thursday.
Revisaremos el acuerdo dentro de dos semanas para comprobar si funciona.We will review the agreement in two weeks to check whether it is working.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a 220–260-word dialogue or mediation summary for a workplace disagreement about workload or deadlines. Include each side's concern, neutral clarification, a constructive feedback statement, two possible solutions, a negotiated agreement, responsibilities and a review date.
