CEFR B2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 22

Idiomatic language, collocations and lexical patterns

Natural Spanish ke liye words ko isolated translate na karein: common word partnerships, verb-preposition patterns, idioms aur register ko context mein seekhein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Use frequent Spanish collocations accurately rather than translating word by word.
  • Recognise common verb–preposition and adjective–preposition patterns.
  • Interpret and use selected idioms with attention to register and context.
PART 1 · LEARN WORDS IN PARTNERSHIPS

Learn words in partnerships

Collocation woh words hain jo naturally saath aate hain. Tomar una decisión, prestar atención aur cometer un error ko chunks ki tarah yaad karein; literal word substitution unnatural ho sakti hai.

Tenemos que tomar una decisión antes de que termine la reunión.We have to make a decision before the meeting ends.
Presta atención a los detalles antes de firmar el contrato.Pay attention to the details before signing the contract.
PART 2 · VERB AND PREPOSITION PATTERNS

Verb and preposition patterns

Kuch verbs fixed preposition maangte hain: depender de, insistir en, contar con, contribuir a. Verb ke saath preposition aur example bhi learn karein; har English preposition ka direct equivalent assume na karein.

El resultado depende de varios factores que aún no conocemos.The result depends on several factors that we do not yet know.
La directora insistió en revisar los datos originales.The director insisted on reviewing the original data.
PART 3 · ADJECTIVE AND PREPOSITION PATTERNS

Adjective and preposition patterns

Adjectives bhi specific prepositions ke saath aate hain: consciente de, dispuesto/a a, satisfecho/a con, capaz de. Gender/number agreement aur following infinitive/noun structure par focus karein.

El equipo está dispuesto a modificar el calendario.The team is willing to modify the schedule.
No estoy satisfecho con la explicación que nos dieron.I am not satisfied with the explanation they gave us.
PART 4 · IDIOMS IN CONTEXT

Idioms in context

Idioms ka meaning words ke literal sum se different ho sakta hai. Echar una mano = help; estar al tanto de = keep informed; dar en el clavo = get it exactly right. Audience aur register check karein.

Gracias por echarme una mano con la presentación.Thanks for giving me a hand with the presentation.
Con esa observación has dado en el clavo.With that observation, you have hit the nail on the head.
PART 5 · NEAR-SYNONYMS AND LEXICAL PRECISION

Near-synonyms and lexical precision

Synonyms har context mein interchangeable nahi. Lograr, conseguir aur alcanzar overlap karte hain, lekin collocation aur nuance vary karti hai. Repetition avoid karne ke liye exact meaning pehle identify karein.

La investigación pretende alcanzar un objetivo concreto.The research aims to achieve a specific objective.
Por fin conseguimos resolver el problema técnico.At last we managed to solve the technical problem.
PART 6 · BUILD A PERSONAL LEXICAL NOTEBOOK

Build a personal lexical notebook

Phrase ko complete example, meaning, preposition, register aur apne sentence ke saath record karein. Corpus ya dictionary example se usage verify karein; one-off translation ko universal rule na banayein.

Me cuesta acostumbrarme al nuevo horario, pero poco a poco me adapto.I find it hard to get used to the new timetable, but I am gradually adapting.
La propuesta tiene en cuenta las necesidades de los usuarios.The proposal takes users' needs into account.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Create a lexical notebook with 12 useful chunks from study, work or daily life. For each, record the full Spanish pattern, English meaning, required preposition (if any), register and an original sentence. Then write a 180–220-word text using at least eight chunks naturally; underline them and explain two choices.
