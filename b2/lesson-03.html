CEFR B2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 03

Reconstructing events and conflicting accounts

Reports, evidence aur mukhtalif statements ko ehtiyat se present karein — fact, claim aur uncertainty ko alag rakhein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Attribute information to its source rather than presenting every claim as fact.
  • Use reporting expressions to show certainty and uncertainty.
  • Compare conflicting accounts in a neutral, evidence-aware way.
PART 1 · FACT, CLAIM OR INFERENCE?

Do not turn a report into a fact

B2 communication mein source clear rakhna zaroori hai. Según el informe = report ke mutabiq; la testigo afirma que = witness ka claim; los datos indican que = data ka signal. In phrases se speaker evidence aur interpretation ko separate karta hai.

La empresa sostiene que cumplió el contrato.The company maintains that it fulfilled the contract.
El portavoz reconoció que hubo un error.The spokesperson acknowledged that there had been an error.
La organización negó haber recibido la solicitud.The organisation denied having received the request.
PART 2 · REPORTING AND DISTANCE

Attribute the statement

Afirmar, sostener, señalar, reconocer, negar aur asegurar different shades rakhte hain. Afirmar/asegurar speaker ka confidence dikhate hain; admitir/reconocer kisi baat ko accept karna; negar explicitly reject karna. Neutral writing mein source ko name karein.

Al parecer, el tren salió por una vía distinta.Apparently, the train left on a different track.
Por lo visto, nadie había informado al equipo.It seems nobody had informed the team.
Según varios medios, el director habría dimitido.According to several media outlets, the director is said to have resigned.
PART 3 · UNCERTAINTY AND INFERENCE

al parecer, por lo visto, aparentemente

Al parecer aur por lo visto information ko provisional ya reported banate hain. Aparentemente appearance/inference show karta hai. Conditional simple/compound kabhi news reports mein unconfirmed information ke liye aata hai: El director habría dimitido. Iska matlab confirmed fact nahi; report ke mutabiq resignation hua ho sakta hai.

El primer testigo sitúa la llegada a las ocho, mientras que el segundo habla de las ocho y media.The first witness places the arrival at eight, whereas the second says half past eight.
La empresa atribuye el retraso a un fallo técnico; los usuarios, en cambio, señalan falta de información.The company attributes the delay to a technical fault; users, by contrast, point to a lack of information.
Por ahora, esa versión no se ha podido confirmar.For now, that account has not been possible to confirm.
PART 4 · COMPARE ACCOUNTS

Show agreement and contradiction fairly

Mientras que, en cambio, por su parte aur sin embargo accounts compare karte hain. Dono sources ki baat ko fair wording dein. Agar evidence incomplete ho to “no se ha podido confirmar” jaisi phrasing use karein.

Según el registro de acceso, la puerta se abrió a las 7:52.According to the access log, the door opened at 7:52.
El responsable sostiene que llegó diez minutos después.The person in charge maintains that he arrived ten minutes later.
La investigación continúa y todavía no hay una conclusión definitiva.The investigation continues and there is still no definitive conclusion.

PART 6 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Summarise a disagreement responsibly

Speaking: present two different accounts of a delayed event without choosing a side prematurely. Writing: 160–190 words. Include at least two named sources, one evidence-based statement, one uncertainty marker and a clear note about what is not yet confirmed.
