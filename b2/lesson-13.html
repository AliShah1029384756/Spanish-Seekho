CEFR B2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 13

Meetings, presentations and collaboration

Professional meetings mein agenda follow karein, apni baat structure karein, clarification maangein aur decisions ko accurately summarise karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Open and close a meeting professionally and refer to its agenda.
  • Present a proposal in a clear sequence and guide listeners through key points.
  • Clarify, interrupt politely, invite contributions and summarise agreed actions.
PART 1 · MEETING PURPOSE AND AGENDA

Set the purpose before discussion

Meeting language ko clear aur concise rakhein. Para empezar / en primer lugar opening aur sequencing dete hain; el objetivo de esta reunión es… purpose batata hai. Agenda item ko name kar ke discussion focused rakhein.

El objetivo de esta reunión es revisar los resultados del trimestre.The aim of this meeting is to review the quarterly results.
Si os parece, empezamos por el primer punto del orden del día.If that works for everyone, let us start with the first item on the agenda.
PART 2 · CONTRIBUTING AND TURN-TAKING

Add a point and enter the discussion

Aap kisi ki baat support, extend ya respectfully challenge kar sakte hain. Quisiera añadir… formal contribution hai; en cuanto a… topic shift karta hai. Spain mein team setting mein vosotros common hai, lekin formal external audience ke liye ustedes bhi samajhna zaroori hai.

Quisiera añadir un dato que puede cambiar nuestra valoración.I would like to add a piece of information that may change our assessment.
En cuanto al presupuesto, todavía necesitamos cifras más precisas.As for the budget, we still need more precise figures.
PART 3 · POLITE INTERRUPTION AND CLARIFICATION

Keep interaction cooperative

Interruption ko soften karne ke liye perdona que te interrumpa / si me permites. Agar baat clear na ho to ¿podrías concretar…? ya ¿te refieres a…? use karein. Sirf repeat karwana nahi, exact point clarify karein.

Perdona que te interrumpa; ¿podrías aclarar a qué plazo te refieres?Sorry to interrupt; could you clarify which deadline you mean?
Si te he entendido bien, propones aplazar la decisión hasta junio.If I have understood you correctly, you suggest postponing the decision until June.
PART 4 · STRUCTURING A PRESENTATION

Signpost the argument

Presentation ko introduction, main points, evidence aur conclusion mein divide karein. En primer lugar, por otra parte, a continuación aur para concluir listener ko roadmap dete hain. Har slide ko read karne ke bajaye key message explain karein.

Voy a dividir la presentación en tres partes: contexto, resultados y próximos pasos.I am going to divide the presentation into three parts: context, results and next steps.
Como podéis ver en esta gráfica, la participación ha aumentado de forma constante.As you can see in this chart, participation has increased steadily.
PART 5 · REACHING AND RECORDING AGREEMENTS

Turn discussion into action

Agreement ko vague na chhorein. Queda acordado que… decision record karta hai; se encargará de… responsibility; antes del viernes deadline. No se ha tomado una decisión means decision abhi nahi hui.

Queda acordado que el equipo enviará una versión revisada antes del viernes.It is agreed that the team will send a revised version before Friday.
Marta se encargará de contactar con los proveedores y yo prepararé el resumen.Marta will contact the suppliers and I will prepare the summary.
PART 6 · FOLLOW-UP AND PROFESSIONAL REGISTER

Close the loop

Meeting ke baad minutes/acta share karein. Formal register mein usted/ustedes, le agradecería aur quedo a su disposición useful hain. Internal teams mein natural tone ho sakti hai, magar clarity aur respect maintain karein.

Adjunto el acta de la reunión con los acuerdos y las tareas pendientes.I am attaching the meeting minutes with the agreements and outstanding tasks.
Les agradecería que confirmaran la recepción del documento.I would be grateful if you could confirm receipt of the document.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Prepare a 3–4 minute presentation for a team meeting about improving a service or project. State the objective, explain two options, recommend one, invite questions and finish with three action points. Then write a 100-word meeting summary naming responsibilities and deadlines.
