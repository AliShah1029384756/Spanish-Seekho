CEFR B2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 20

Summarising and comparing multiple sources

Multiple Spanish sources ko accurately summarise aur synthesise karein; shared findings, contradictions, source quality aur evidence ki limits ko coherent report mein combine karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Extract central ideas and relevant details from several sources.
  • Compare agreement, contradiction, scope and reliability across sources.
  • Produce an attributed synthesis without copying or distorting source meaning.
PART 1 · READING FOR THE CENTRAL MESSAGE

Find the argument, not every detail

Source read karte waqt central claim, purpose, audience aur supporting evidence identify karein. La idea principal es…; el texto se centra en…; el autor sostiene que… Main point ko examples aur minor details se separate karein.

El artículo se centra en los efectos de la vivienda turística sobre los barrios.The article focuses on the effects of tourist accommodation on neighbourhoods.
La idea principal es que el cambio requiere medidas coordinadas.The main idea is that change requires coordinated measures.
PART 2 · ACCURATE SUMMARISING

Compress without changing meaning

Summary apne words mein source ka core meaning preserve karta hai. El autor señala que…; el informe concluye que…; según el análisis… Attribution maintain karein; source ki uncertainty ko apni certainty mein convert na karein.

El informe señala que los resultados varían considerablemente entre regiones.The report points out that results vary considerably between regions.
La autora sostiene que la medida podría reducir los costes a medio plazo.The author argues that the measure could reduce costs in the medium term.
PART 3 · COMPARING AGREEMENT AND DIFFERENCE

Build a source map

Las fuentes coinciden en… agreement; discrepan en cuanto a… disagreement; mientras que… contrast. Compare topic, data, methods, date aur scope. Do sources ka same result independent evidence zaroori nahi agar dono same dataset cite karte hon.

Las tres fuentes coinciden en que el acceso a la vivienda es un problema relevante.All three sources agree that access to housing is a significant issue.
Discrepan, sin embargo, en cuanto a la eficacia de los controles de precios.However, they disagree about the effectiveness of price controls.
PART 4 · WEIGHING SOURCE QUALITY

Assess authority and limitations

Quién publica, cuándo, con qué método, sample aur purpose? Una fuente institucional, académica, journalistic ya opinion source ka role different ho sakta hai. Authority alone accuracy guarantee nahi karti; transparency aur evidence inspect karein.

El estudio ofrece datos detallados, aunque su muestra se limita a dos ciudades.The study provides detailed data, although its sample is limited to two cities.
La columna expresa una opinión fundamentada, pero no presenta datos originales.The opinion column expresses a reasoned view but does not present original data.
PART 5 · SYNTHESISING RATHER THAN LISTING

Create a combined interpretation

Synthesis sources ko paragraph by paragraph repeat nahi karti; themes ke around combine karti hai. En conjunto, las fuentes apuntan a…; tomadas en consideración, sugieren…; una posible explicación es… Apni inference ko sources ke direct claim se distinguish karein.

En conjunto, las fuentes apuntan a una relación entre el transporte y el acceso al empleo.Taken together, the sources point to a relationship between transport and access to employment.
Tomadas en consideración, sugieren que las soluciones deben adaptarse al contexto local.Considered together, they suggest that solutions should be adapted to the local context.
PART 6 · WRITING A TRACEABLE CONCLUSION

Show what is known and what remains open

Conclusion mein answer, evidence basis aur remaining uncertainty clear karein. La evidencia disponible permite afirmar que…; no obstante, todavía no está claro si…; haría falta… Attribution aur actual bibliography retain karein.

La evidencia disponible permite afirmar que la situación varía según la zona.The available evidence allows us to say that the situation varies by area.
No obstante, haría falta disponer de datos más recientes para evaluar el efecto a largo plazo.Nevertheless, more recent data would be needed to assess the long-term effect.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Select three credible sources in Spanish about one topic. Produce a 250–300-word synthesis with: a short framing question, each source's central contribution, two points of agreement, one disagreement or gap, a comment on source limitations, and a measured conclusion. Attribute every source and do not invent publication details.
