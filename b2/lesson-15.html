CEFR B2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 15

Academic discussion and source-based writing

Academic discussion mein claim, evidence aur interpretation ko distinguish karein; sources ko accurately paraphrase karein aur cautious conclusions likhein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Contribute to seminars by building on, questioning and qualifying ideas.
  • Summarise and paraphrase sources without changing their meaning.
  • Integrate evidence, attribution and cautious evaluation in structured writing.
PART 1 · ACADEMIC CLAIMS AND DISCUSSION

State a position precisely

Academic argument mein tema, pregunta de investigación, postura aur scope clear hon. Sostengo que… direct thesis hai; cabe preguntarse si… research question open karta hai. Generalisation ko evidence ke baghair universal fact na banayein.

En este trabajo sostengo que el acceso digital mejora la participación, aunque no garantiza la igualdad.In this paper, I argue that digital access improves participation, although it does not guarantee equality.
Cabe preguntarse si las medidas actuales responden a las necesidades de todos los grupos.It is worth asking whether current measures respond to the needs of all groups.
PART 2 · PARAPHRASING AND ATTRIBUTION

Represent sources fairly

Paraphrase ka matlab wording aur sentence structure apne alfaaz mein karna, magar source ka meaning preserve karna. Según X, el estudio señala que… attribution deta hai. Direct quote ho to exact wording aur required citation style follow karein.

Según el informe, la participación aumentó especialmente entre los estudiantes adultos.According to the report, participation increased particularly among adult students.
La autora señala que el acceso a internet no elimina por sí solo las desigualdades.The author points out that internet access alone does not eliminate inequalities.
PART 3 · COMPARING SOURCES

Notice agreement and tension

Mientras que contrast karta hai; ambas fuentes coinciden en… shared finding; sin embargo, difieren en… difference. Sources ki date, method, sample aur purpose compare karein — sirf conclusion compare na karein.

Ambos estudios coinciden en la importancia de la formación continua.Both studies agree on the importance of continuing education.
Mientras que el primer informe analiza datos nacionales, el segundo se centra en una sola región.Whereas the first report analyses national data, the second focuses on a single region.
PART 4 · QUALIFYING AND EXPRESSING UNCERTAINTY

Avoid overclaiming

Los resultados sugieren, es probable que, hasta cierto punto aur no se puede descartar calibrated language hain. Parece que + indicative commonly presents an impression; es posible que + subjunctive possibility ko frame karta hai.

Los resultados sugieren que la medida podría mejorar el acceso.The results suggest that the measure could improve access.
Es posible que otros factores hayan influido en las cifras.It is possible that other factors influenced the figures.
PART 5 · EVALUATING METHODS AND LIMITATIONS

Assess how knowledge was produced

Muestra, sesgo, datos incompletos, periodo de observación aur correlación/causalidad ke farq ko notice karein. Una limitación del estudio es…; los datos no permiten concluir que… responsible evaluation hai.

Una limitación del estudio es el reducido tamaño de la muestra.One limitation of the study is the small sample size.
Los datos muestran una asociación, pero no permiten concluir que exista una relación causal.The data show an association, but they do not allow us to conclude that a causal relationship exists.
PART 6 · SYNTHESIS AND CONCLUSION

Build a reasoned final position

Conclusion mein sources ko repeat na karein; unhein synthesize karein. En conjunto, la evidencia apunta a…; a la luz de estos datos…; se necesitan más estudios… measured close provide karte hain. References list mein sirf actual consulted sources rakhein.

En conjunto, la evidencia apunta a una mejora gradual, aunque desigual.Overall, the evidence points to gradual but uneven improvement.
A la luz de estos datos, conviene ampliar la investigación antes de adoptar una medida general.In light of these data, further research is advisable before adopting a general measure.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Choose a public issue you can research. Write a 250–300-word source-based discussion using at least two credible sources. Paraphrase each source, attribute claims clearly, compare the evidence, identify one limitation and reach a measured conclusion. Do not invent quotations or references.
