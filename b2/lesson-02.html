CEFR B2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 02

Complex past-time relationships

Past ke andar multiple time layers ko organise karein aur pehle, us waqt aur baad ke events ka relation clear rakhein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Sequence two or more past events accurately.
  • Contrast pluscuamperfecto, indefinido and imperfecto by meaning.
  • Use temporal clauses and markers to remove ambiguity.
PART 1 · BUILD A TIMELINE

One past reference point, several time layers

Jab story mein multiple past events hon, listener ko reference point chahiye. Indefinido main completed event batata hai; imperfecto ongoing/background action; pluscuamperfecto us reference point se pehle complete ho chuka event.

La empresa había cambiado de proveedor antes de nuestra visita.The company had changed supplier before our visit.
No habían reservado una sala, así que improvisamos.They had not booked a room, so we improvised.
Nunca había visto una situación semejante.I had never seen a situation like that.
PART 2 · PLUSCUAMPERFECTO IN CONTEXT

Earlier action, later discovery

Structure: había / habías / había / habíamos / habíais / habían + participio. Is tense ko har past sentence mein use nahi karna. Sirf jab past reference point se pehle ka relation relevant ho.

Antes de que llegara la directora, revisamos el informe.Before the director arrived, we reviewed the report.
Después de que terminó la entrevista, tomamos notas.After the interview ended, we took notes.
Al recibir la noticia, avisó a su familia.On receiving the news, she informed her family.
PART 3 · TEMPORAL CONNECTORS

Make the order explicit

Antes de que ke baad subjunctive aam tor par aata hai jab subordinate action abhi reference point par complete nahi hua tha: antes de que llegara. Después de que ke saath tense context par depend karta hai. Al + infinitive ek concise option hai jab subject same ho.

Cuando llamé, ya habían salido.When I called, they had already left.
A las diez todavía no habían respondido.At ten, they had not replied yet.
El problema aún seguía sin resolverse.The problem was still unresolved.
PART 4 · ALREADY, STILL AND NOT YET

ya, todavía and aún

Ya = already/by then; todavía no / aún no = not yet; todavía seguía = was still continuing. In markers ko tense ke saath combine karke expectation aur timing clear hoti hai.

La candidata había enviado la solicitud dos semanas antes.The candidate had sent the application two weeks earlier.
Cuando revisaron el expediente, descubrieron que faltaba un documento.When they reviewed the file, they discovered a document was missing.
Aún no habían confirmado la fecha cuando se publicó el anuncio.They had not yet confirmed the date when the announcement was published.
PART 5 · MODEL TIMELINE

An interrupted application process

Read the sequence carefully: application sent → interview scheduled → documents discovered missing → candidate contacted.

PART 6 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Explain a chain of events

Speaking: explain a real or invented situation with at least three time layers. Writing: 150–180 words using indefinido, imperfecto and pluscuamperfecto accurately. Underline or list the event that happened first, the reference event and the later consequence.
