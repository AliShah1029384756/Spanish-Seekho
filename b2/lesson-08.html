CEFR B2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 08

Counterarguments and balanced opinions

Opposing views ko fairly acknowledge karein, concession aur rebuttal use karein aur evidence-based balanced conclusion tak pohnchein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Present an opposing view accurately and fairly.
  • Use concession and rebuttal structures with correct mood and register.
  • Reach a balanced conclusion that reflects evidence and limitations.
PART 1 · UNDERSTAND THE OTHER SIDE

Represent the counterargument fairly

Counterargument sirf opposing view ka weak version nahi hota. Pehle doosri side ka strongest reasonable point state karein. “Quienes se oponen sostienen que…” source attribute karta hai; “su principal preocupación es…” concern identify karta hai.

Quienes se oponen a la medida señalan su posible coste económico.Those who oppose the measure point to its possible economic cost.
Su principal preocupación es que los pequeños comercios salgan perjudicados.Their main concern is that small businesses may be harmed.
PART 2 · CONCESSION

Acknowledge what is valid

Es cierto que…, es verdad que…, aunque… concession introduce karte hain. Si bien formal hai. A pesar de que + indicative jab fact accepted ho; subjunctive jab hypothetical/unknown concession ho. Mood meaning aur certainty ke mutabiq choose karein.

Es cierto que la transición requiere inversión, pero puede reducir gastos a largo plazo.It is true that the transition requires investment, but it may reduce costs in the long term.
Aunque la propuesta tiene inconvenientes, ofrece ventajas importantes.Although the proposal has drawbacks, it offers important advantages.
PART 3 · REBUTTAL

Respond with reasons, not dismissal

Sin embargo, no obstante, aun así aur con todo counterpoint ke baad response introduce karte hain. Rebuttal opposing concern ko evidence, alternative interpretation ya practical safeguard se address karta hai.

El coste inicial es elevado; aun así, podría reducirse mediante una implantación gradual.The initial cost is high; even so, it could be reduced through gradual implementation.
No obstante, esa objeción no tiene en cuenta los beneficios a largo plazo.Nevertheless, that objection does not take long-term benefits into account.
PART 4 · CONTRASTING STRUCTURES

Whereas, while and on the other hand

Mientras que / whereas direct contrast; por un lado… por otro lado… two dimensions; en cambio contrasts alternatives. “A diferencia de” compares differences. Ensure each side of comparison is grammatically parallel and logically fair.

Mientras que algunas personas priorizan el coste, otras valoran la accesibilidad.While some people prioritise cost, others value accessibility.
Por un lado, la medida exige recursos; por otro, puede mejorar la calidad del servicio.On the one hand, the measure requires resources; on the other, it may improve service quality.
PART 5 · BALANCED CONCLUSIONS

Conclude without pretending every issue is settled

En definitiva / en conjunto summary; teniendo en cuenta… weighs factors; la opción más razonable sería… proposes a qualified conclusion. Conclusion evidence se zyada strong nahi honi chahiye. Remaining uncertainty ko name karein.

En conjunto, parece razonable probar la medida y evaluar sus resultados.Overall, it seems reasonable to trial the measure and evaluate its results.
La decisión final debería basarse en datos más amplios y en la opinión de las personas afectadas.The final decision should be based on broader data and the views of the people affected.
PART 6 · WRITE A BALANCED ARGUMENT

Position → counterargument → concession → rebuttal → judgement

Ek strong balanced essay opposing position ko dismiss nahi karta. Speaking: 2–3 minute discussion. Writing: 190–220 words. At least one genuine concession, one evidence-aware rebuttal and a conclusion that identifies what further information would help.

Aunque existen razones válidas en ambas posturas, la decisión debe tener en cuenta el contexto concreto.Although there are valid reasons on both sides, the decision must take the specific context into account.
Por ahora, una prueba limitada sería más prudente que una aplicación general.For now, a limited trial would be more prudent than general implementation.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a balanced opinion (190–220 words) on whether public transport should be free. Present the strongest counterargument fairly, concede one valid point, respond with evidence or reasoning, and end with a qualified conclusion plus one unresolved question.

Self-review

Check that your position is clear, examples are relevant, connectors express the intended relationship, and certainty matches the evidence. Record yourself once, then repeat with clearer structure and more precise language.
