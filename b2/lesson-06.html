CEFR B2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 06

Debate, agreement and disagreement

Debate mein respectfully agree/disagree karein, doosre ki baat reformulate karein aur register ke mutabiq apna response adjust karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Express degrees of agreement and disagreement politely.
  • Reformulate another speaker’s point before responding.
  • Manage interruption, clarification and turn-taking in discussion.
PART 1 · DEGREES OF AGREEMENT

Agreement is not simply yes or no

Estoy de acuerdo / totalmente de acuerdo strong agreement hai. Estoy de acuerdo en parte partial agreement. Comparto esa idea lekin nuance add karta hai. No estoy del todo convencido soft disagreement hai. Register aur tone meaning ko affect karte hain.

Comparto la idea general, aunque matizaría un aspecto.I share the general idea, although I would qualify one aspect.
Estoy totalmente de acuerdo con esa conclusión.I completely agree with that conclusion.
PART 2 · POLITE DISAGREEMENT

Challenge the idea, not the person

No lo veo exactamente así, desde mi punto de vista aur entiendo tu argumento, pero… disagreement ko respectful banate hain. Formal meeting mein “No comparto del todo esa interpretación” natural hai. Personal attack ya “estás equivocado” se discussion defensive ho sakti hai.

Entiendo tu postura, pero no comparto del todo la conclusión.I understand your position, but I do not entirely share the conclusion.
Desde mi punto de vista, habría que tener en cuenta otro factor.From my point of view, another factor should be taken into account.
PART 3 · REFORMULATING

Show that you understood the other speaker

Si no te he entendido mal… / Es decir, lo que propones es… / Entonces, tu principal preocupación es… paraphrase aur confirmation ke liye hain. Yeh active listening hai, agreement ka signal zaroori nahi.

Si no te he entendido mal, propones aplazar la decisión.If I have understood you correctly, you propose postponing the decision.
¿Quieres decir que el coste es el principal obstáculo?Do you mean that cost is the main obstacle?
PART 4 · TURN-TAKING AND INTERRUPTION

Enter the discussion naturally

Quería añadir algo, si me permitís… polite entry hai. Antes de continuar, ¿puedo aclarar un punto? clarification request hai. Déjame terminar informal hai; formal setting mein “Permítame terminar” use karein. Spain mein vosotros informal plural aur ustedes formal/plural in many contexts; region aur institution ke norms vary kar sakte hain.

Si me permitís, quisiera añadir un matiz.If you allow me, I would like to add a qualification.
Antes de seguir, ¿podrías aclarar a qué te refieres?Before we continue, could you clarify what you mean?
PART 5 · NUANCE AND QUALIFICATION

Avoid false certainty

Hasta cierto punto, en líneas generales, en principio aur depende del contexto statements ko qualify karte hain. Puede que + subjunctive possibility; no necesariamente rejects an overgeneralisation. Debate mein nuance weakness nahi, precision hai.

En líneas generales, la medida parece útil, pero depende de cómo se aplique.Broadly speaking, the measure seems useful, but it depends on how it is implemented.
Eso no significa necesariamente que la propuesta vaya a funcionar en todos los casos.That does not necessarily mean the proposal will work in every case.
PART 6 · A CONSTRUCTIVE DEBATE

Listen → reformulate → respond → move forward

2–3 minute role-play karein: ek participant proposal support karta hai, doosra concerns raise karta hai. Pehle opposing point accurately reformulate karein, phir reasoned response dein aur end par shared ground identify karein. Writing: 160–190 words ka balanced discussion summary.

Aunque partimos de posiciones distintas, coincidimos en que hace falta más información.Although we start from different positions, we agree that more information is needed.
Podríamos buscar una solución que tenga en cuenta ambas preocupaciones.We could look for a solution that takes both concerns into account.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Hold or write a structured discussion about whether schools should limit mobile-phone use. Include one strong agreement, one partial agreement, two polite disagreements, one reformulation and a shared-ground conclusion. Writing: 160–190 words.

Self-review

Check that your position is clear, examples are relevant, connectors express the intended relationship, and certainty matches the evidence. Record yourself once, then repeat with clearer structure and more precise language.
