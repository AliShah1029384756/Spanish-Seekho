CEFR B2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 05

Building strong arguments

Apni position ko clear claim, relevant reasons aur credible examples ke saath defend karein — bina baat ko overstate kiye.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • State a precise position and distinguish it from a topic.
  • Develop reasons with relevant examples or evidence.
  • Connect ideas using contrast, cause, consequence and concession.
PART 1 · FROM TOPIC TO CLAIM

Make your position specific

Sirf topic repeat karna argument nahi hota. Claim woh clear position hai jise aap defend karte hain. “La educación en línea” topic hai; “La educación en línea debería complementar, no sustituir, la enseñanza presencial” ek arguable claim hai.

La educación en línea debería complementar la enseñanza presencial.Online education should complement face-to-face teaching.
No basta con ofrecer más cursos; también hay que garantizar su calidad.Offering more courses is not enough; their quality must also be guaranteed.
PART 2 · REASONS AND SUPPORT

Explain why your claim makes sense

Har claim ke saath relevant reason dein. Porque, ya que aur dado que reason introduce karte hain. Puesto que zyada formal register mein common hai. Reason ko evidence samajh kar present na karein jab tak support available na ho.

La medida es necesaria porque reduce los tiempos de espera.The measure is necessary because it reduces waiting times.
Dado que los datos son limitados, conviene evitar conclusiones definitivas.Given that the data is limited, definitive conclusions should be avoided.
PART 3 · EVIDENCE AND EXAMPLES

Support, do not exaggerate

Por ejemplo, en particular aur como muestra… example introduce karte hain. Según los datos… source-based support deta hai. Evidence ki strength ke mutabiq verb choose karein: sugiere, indica, apunta a; demuestra tab jab evidence waqai decisive ho.

Los resultados apuntan a una mejora, aunque todavía hacen falta más datos.The results point to an improvement, although more data is still needed.
Por ejemplo, el nuevo horario ha reducido las ausencias en algunos grupos.For example, the new timetable has reduced absences in some groups.
PART 4 · CONNECTORS FOR LOGIC

Make the reasoning easy to follow

Por tanto / por consiguiente consequence; además adds a point; sin embargo contrasts; de hecho strengthens with a fact. Connectors ka kaam decoration nahi — reader ko argument ka logical relation samjhana hai.

El coste inicial es elevado; sin embargo, el ahorro a largo plazo puede compensarlo.The initial cost is high; however, long-term savings may offset it.
Además, la propuesta permitiría atender a más personas.Furthermore, the proposal would make it possible to serve more people.
PART 5 · CONCESSION AND QUALIFICATION

Recognise limits without abandoning your view

Es cierto que…, aunque… ek concern acknowledge karta hai. Si bien formal concession hai. Aun así / con todo counterpoint ke baad apni reasoning continue karte hain. Absolute claims (siempre, nunca, todos) se bachein jab evidence limited ho.

Es cierto que la inversión es considerable, pero los beneficios podrían ser duraderos.It is true that the investment is considerable, but the benefits could be lasting.
Si bien la propuesta tiene límites, merece la pena estudiarla.Although the proposal has limitations, it is worth examining.
PART 6 · BUILD A COHERENT CASE

Claim → reason → evidence → implication

Ek strong paragraph mein position, reason, example/evidence aur implication ka flow hota hai. Speaking task: 2 minutes. Writing task: 170–200 words. Har example ka source ya status clear rakhein; invented statistics ko real data ki tarah present na karein.

En conjunto, la evidencia disponible respalda una aplicación gradual de la medida.Overall, the available evidence supports a gradual implementation of the measure.
Por estas razones, considero que la propuesta merece una prueba piloto.For these reasons, I believe the proposal deserves a pilot trial.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Choose a practical issue (public transport, education, digital services or workplace schedules). Present a clear claim, two reasons, one concrete example and one limitation. Write 170–200 words and use at least five connectors without repeating the same one.

Self-review

Check that your position is clear, examples are relevant, connectors express the intended relationship, and certainty matches the evidence. Record yourself once, then repeat with clearer structure and more precise language.
