CEFR B2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 17

Understanding news, interviews and public debate

Spanish news ko critically samjhein: headline, reported facts, interview questions, attributed claims aur public debate ke different viewpoints identify karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Distinguish a headline, verified information, attributed claims and commentary.
  • Follow interviews by identifying questions, responses, stance and implied assumptions.
  • Summarise public debate fairly while separating evidence from opinion.
PART 1 · HEADLINES AND NEWS FRAMING

Notice what the headline emphasises

Headline short hoti hai aur aksar strongest angle choose karti hai. El titular, la noticia, la entradilla aur el reportaje alag cheezein hain. Headline ka verb aur omitted context dekhein; headline akeli full story nahi hoti.

El titular destaca el impacto económico de la nueva medida.The headline highlights the economic impact of the new measure.
La noticia explica qué ha ocurrido, cuándo y quiénes están implicados.The news report explains what happened, when and who is involved.
PART 2 · ATTRIBUTING INFORMATION

Separate facts from claims

Según fuentes oficiales / según el portavoz attribution dete hain. El ministerio ha confirmado… verified institutional statement ho sakta hai; un vecino afirma que… attributed claim hai. Allegation ko fact ke taur par repeat na karein.

Según el comunicado, las obras comenzarán el próximo lunes.According to the statement, construction will begin next Monday.
Una asociación vecinal afirma que no ha sido consultada.A neighbourhood association claims that it has not been consulted.
PART 3 · FOLLOWING INTERVIEWS

Track questions and responses

Interview mein entrevistador ka sawal, entrevistado ka jawab, follow-up aur interruption identify karein. ¿A qué atribuye…? cause poochta hai; ¿qué medidas propone? response/solution. Speaker ka stance sirf words nahi, qualifications se bhi samjhein.

¿A qué atribuye el aumento del coste de la vivienda?What do you attribute the rise in housing costs to?
La portavoz reconoce el problema, pero cuestiona la interpretación de las cifras.The spokesperson acknowledges the problem but questions the interpretation of the figures.
PART 4 · PUBLIC DEBATE AND POSITIONS

Map the disagreement fairly

Debate mein postura, supporting reason, evidence aur rebuttal alag note karein. Quienes defienden la propuesta sostienen que…; sus detractores advierten de que… Dono sides ko equal credibility dena zaroori nahi; evidence evaluate karein.

Quienes apoyan la reforma sostienen que mejorará el acceso al servicio.Those who support the reform argue that it will improve access to the service.
Sus detractores advierten de que podría aumentar las desigualdades territoriales.Its critics warn that it could increase regional inequalities.
PART 5 · UNCERTAINTY AND DEVELOPING STORIES

Avoid premature conclusions

Al parecer, por el momento, todavía no se ha confirmado aur la investigación sigue abierta uncertainty dikhate hain. Breaking story mein facts update ho sakte hain; publication date aur later correction check karein.

Por el momento, no se ha confirmado la causa del incidente.For the time being, the cause of the incident has not been confirmed.
La investigación sigue abierta y se esperan nuevos datos esta semana.The investigation is ongoing and new information is expected this week.
PART 6 · NEUTRAL NEWS SUMMARY

Synthesize without sensationalism

Summary mein main event, relevant context, attributed viewpoints aur unknowns include karein. El reportaje aborda…; según los datos disponibles…; aún queda por aclarar… sensational language aur unsupported conclusions avoid karein.

El reportaje aborda las consecuencias sociales de la reforma y recoge opiniones contrapuestas.The report examines the social consequences of the reform and includes opposing views.
Aún queda por aclarar cómo se financiará la medida.It remains to be clarified how the measure will be funded.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Choose a current public issue from two credible Spanish-language news reports. Write a 180–220-word neutral briefing: headline/context, what is confirmed, what different speakers claim, one unanswered question and a balanced summary. Do not present an allegation as a proven fact.
