CEFR B2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 04

Personal, historical and professional narratives

Personal experience, historical events aur professional case studies ko coherent, nuanced aur audience ke mutabiq present karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Organise a longer narrative around a clear theme and chronology.
  • Use passive and impersonal structures when the actor is unknown or less important.
  • Adapt tone and detail for personal, historical and professional contexts.
PART 1 · CHOOSE THE NARRATIVE FRAME

Personal, historical or professional?

Personal narrative mein reflection aur individual perspective central hota hai. Historical narrative chronology, context aur evidence ko balance karti hai. Professional case study problem, decision, action aur outcome ko clear rakhti hai. Audience decide karti hai kitni detail aur kitna formal register chahiye.

El edificio fue diseñado por un estudio local.The building was designed by a local studio.
En 2021 se inauguró una nueva biblioteca.A new library was opened in 2021.
Durante la revisión se detectaron varios errores.Several errors were detected during the review.
PART 2 · PASSIVE AND IMPERSONAL SE

Put the process or result in focus

Ser + participio passive voice mein action/result focus karta hai: El proyecto fue aprobado. Se + third-person verb process ya general actor ko foreground karta hai: Se inauguró el centro; Se revisaron los documentos. Agreement yaad rakhein: se revisaron los documentos (plural).

A raíz de la crisis, se revisaron los procedimientos.As a result of the crisis, the procedures were reviewed.
En paralelo, el equipo preparó una nueva propuesta.At the same time, the team prepared a new proposal.
Años más tarde, la iniciativa se convirtió en un servicio estable.Years later, the initiative became an established service.
PART 3 · COHESION OVER LONGER TEXTS

Link paragraphs, not only sentences

A partir de ahí, posteriormente, años más tarde, en paralelo, a raíz de, como consecuencia aur finalmente timeline aur logic ko guide karte hain. Har sentence mein connector na lagayein; transitions sirf jab reader ko relation samajhne mein madad dein.

El cambio redujo los tiempos de espera, aunque no resolvió todos los problemas.The change reduced waiting times, although it did not solve every problem.
En retrospectiva, la decisión fue acertada, pero implicó riesgos.In retrospect, the decision was right, but it involved risks.
Los resultados sugieren una mejora; no obstante, hacen falta más datos.The results suggest an improvement; nevertheless, more data are needed.
PART 4 · DETAIL AND INTERPRETATION

Separate what happened from what it meant

Narrative mein event aur evaluation ko alag rakhna clarity deta hai. “El cambio tuvo dos consecuencias…” facts organise karta hai; “En retrospectiva…” reflection deta hai. Historical/professional claims ko available evidence se zyada strong na banayein.

Se creó un formulario común y se asignaron tareas concretas.A shared form was created and specific tasks were assigned.
Seis meses después, el tiempo medio de respuesta se había reducido.Six months later, the average response time had fallen.
La organización decidió seguir midiendo los resultados antes de ampliarlo.The organisation decided to keep measuring results before expanding it.
PART 5 · MODEL CASE STUDY

From problem to outcome

Read the sequence: context → issue → response → measured outcome → cautious interpretation.

Production challenge

Speaking: give a 2–3 minute case study with context, problem, action, outcome and reflection.

PART 6 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Present a case with a balanced conclusion

Speaking: 2–3 minute case study with context, problem, action, outcome and reflection. Writing: 180–210 words. Use two different narrative connectors, one passive or impersonal se construction, and one balanced evaluation.
