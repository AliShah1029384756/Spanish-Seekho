CEFR B2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 21

Register, tone, politeness and pragmatic choices

Formal, neutral aur informal Spanish ke darmiyan context ke mutabiq switch karein; requests, disagreement aur social distance ko natural tone dein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Choose an appropriate register for professional, public and familiar situations.
  • Soften requests, disagreement and refusals without becoming vague.
  • Recognise how tone, context and relationship affect pragmatic meaning.
PART 1 · REGISTER FOLLOWS CONTEXT

Register follows context

Register sirf formal words ka naam nahi; audience, relationship, purpose aur setting mil kar choice banate hain. Usted professional ya unfamiliar context mein useful hai; tú familiar relationships mein common hai. Spain mein regional aur workplace variation hoti hai.

En una reunión formal, conviene dirigirse al equipo con un tono profesional.In a formal meeting, it is advisable to address the team in a professional tone.
Con personas de confianza, solemos utilizar un registro más cercano.With people we trust, we usually use a more familiar register.
PART 2 · MAKING REQUESTS TACTFULLY

Making requests tactfully

Direct command kabhi zaroori ho sakta hai, lekin polite request mein ¿Podría…?, ¿Te importaría…? aur Cuando tengas un momento… useful hain. Verb form aur context dono politeness create karte hain.

¿Podría enviarme el documento antes del viernes, por favor?Could you send me the document before Friday, please?
Cuando tengas un momento, ¿me echas una mano con esta parte?When you have a moment, could you give me a hand with this part?
PART 3 · SOFTENING OPINIONS AND DISAGREEMENT

Softening opinions and disagreement

B2 par disagreement ko blunt yes/no ke bajaye qualify karein: hasta cierto punto, no estoy del todo convencido/a, quizá convendría. Softener ka matlab weak argument nahi; tone respectful rehti hai.

Entiendo la propuesta, aunque no estoy del todo convencida de que sea viable.I understand the proposal, although I am not entirely convinced it is viable.
Hasta cierto punto comparto tu preocupación, pero matizaría la conclusión.To some extent I share your concern, but I would qualify the conclusion.
PART 4 · REFUSING AND SETTING BOUNDARIES

Refusing and setting boundaries

Polite refusal mein appreciation, clear limit aur alternative de sakte hain. False promise na karein. No me va a ser posible… neutral hai; prefiero no comprometerme… commitment ko honestly limit karta hai.

Te agradezco la invitación, pero esta vez no me va a ser posible asistir.I appreciate the invitation, but I will not be able to attend this time.
No quisiera comprometerme a una fecha que quizá no pueda cumplir.I would not want to commit to a date that I may not be able to meet.
PART 5 · PRAGMATIC MEANING AND INDIRECTNESS

Pragmatic meaning and indirectness

Literal translation har dafa social meaning nahi batati. Ya lo miraré context mein acknowledgement ya polite postponement ho sakta hai; ¿No te importaría…? negative form ke bawajood request hai. Context aur intonation dekhein.

¿No te importaría bajar un poco la música?Would you mind turning the music down a little?
Ya lo comentaré con el equipo y te digo algo mañana.I will discuss it with the team and let you know tomorrow.
PART 6 · ADAPTING THE SAME MESSAGE

Adapting the same message

Ek hi message ko audience ke mutabiq rewrite karein: colleague, manager, friend. Meaning aur boundaries same reh sakti hain, lekin greeting, pronouns, mitigation aur closing change hotay hain.

Le agradecería que confirmara la recepción del informe.I would be grateful if you could confirm receipt of the report.
¿Me confirmas que te ha llegado el informe?Can you confirm that you have received the report?
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write three versions of the same 160–200-word message: a formal email to an organisation, a neutral message to a colleague and an informal message to a friend. Keep the request and deadline consistent, but adapt pronouns, opening, mitigation and closing. Explain briefly why each register fits its audience.
