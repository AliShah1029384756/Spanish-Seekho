CEFR B2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 09

Subjunctive in complex opinions, evaluation and influence

Opinion, evaluation aur influence ko nuanced tareeqe se express karein. Samjhein ke kab subjunctive judgement ya request show karta hai aur kab indicative factual assertion deta hai.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Use subjunctive after evaluative and impersonal expressions.
  • Express requests and influence when another person performs the action.
  • Distinguish judgement from factual assertion.
PART 1 · EVALUATION

Impersonal judgement

Es importante que, es fundamental que aur conviene que kisi action ko evaluate karte hain; subordinate clause mein subjunctive use hota hai. Focus action par judgement hai, fact prove karna nahi.

Es fundamental que el equipo revise los datos antes de publicar el informe.It is essential that the team review the data before publishing the report.
Conviene que la propuesta incluya un calendario realista.It is advisable for the proposal to include a realistic timetable.
PART 2 · OPINIONS AND REACTIONS

Evaluate an action or situation

Me parece bien/mal que aur resulta sorprendente que evaluation show karte hain. Speaker action ko judge karta hai, is liye subjunctive natural hai.

Me parece positivo que la empresa ofrezca formación continua.I think it is positive that the company offers continuous training.
Resulta preocupante que algunos barrios pierdan servicios básicos.It is worrying that some neighbourhoods are losing basic services.
PART 3 · INFLUENCE

Requests and recommendations

Pedir, recomendar, exigir, permitir aur aconsejar + que ke baad subjunctive aata hai jab doosra subject action kare. Same subject ho to infinitive aksar use hota hai.

La dirección recomienda que todos participen en la encuesta.Management recommends that everyone take part in the survey.
Nos han pedido que entreguemos la documentación antes del viernes.They have asked us to submit the documentation before Friday.
PART 4 · PURPOSE

Para que + subjunctive

Para que purpose batata hai jab subordinate clause ka subject different ho. Same subject ho to para + infinitive use karein.

Te envío el resumen para que tengas toda la información.I am sending you the summary so that you have all the information.
Hemos simplificado el formulario para que cualquier persona pueda utilizarlo.We have simplified the form so that anyone can use it.
PART 5 · DOUBT AND DENIAL

Uncertainty and rejected claims

Dudar que aur no creer que uncertainty ya rejection show karte hain aur aam tor par subjunctive lete hain. Creer que affirmative mein usually indicative hota hai.

No creo que esta solución resuelva el problema por sí sola.I do not think this solution will solve the problem on its own.
Dudo que lleguemos a un acuerdo sin revisar las condiciones.I doubt we will reach an agreement without reviewing the conditions.
PART 6 · FACT OR EVALUATION

Choose mood by meaning

Es evidente que + indicative usually asserted fact; es importante que + subjunctive evaluation/need. Mood ko trigger list se nahi, speaker stance se choose karein.

Es evidente que el presupuesto ha aumentado este año.It is clear that the budget has increased this year.
Es importante que expliquemos cómo se utilizarán los fondos.It is important that we explain how the funds will be used.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write 180–220 words recommending a change to a school, workplace or local service. Include two evaluations with subjunctive, one request, one para que clause and one factual statement with indicative. Record a two-minute spoken version.
