CEFR B2 · UNIT 04 · LESSON 14

Reports, proposals and formal correspondence

Reports aur proposals ko logically organise karein, evidence aur recommendation ko separate rakhein, aur formal emails/letters mein precise professional Spanish use karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Organise a report with purpose, findings, interpretation and conclusion.
  • Present proposals with feasibility, benefits, limitations and recommendations.
  • Write formal requests, follow-ups and replies using an appropriate register.
PART 1 · REPORT STRUCTURE

Give the reader a route

Informe mein title, objective, scope, findings, analysis aur conclusions ko distinct rakhein. El presente informe analiza… formal opening hai. Se han observado… findings introduce karta hai; conclusion evidence se logically nikalna chahiye.

El presente informe analiza el uso de la biblioteca durante el último curso.This report analyses library use during the last academic year.
A continuación, se presentan los principales resultados de la encuesta.The main survey findings are presented below.
PART 2 · EVIDENCE AND INTERPRETATION

Separate data from conclusions

Según los datos, de acuerdo con la encuesta aur los resultados indican source-based claims hain. Esto demuestra tabhi likhein jab evidence strong ho; otherwise sugiere/apunta a/parece indicar uncertainty ko calibrate karte hain. Sample size aur limits mention karein.

Según los datos recogidos, el tiempo de espera se ha reducido un 18 %.According to the data collected, waiting time has fallen by 18%.
La muestra es limitada, por lo que los resultados deben interpretarse con cautela.The sample is limited, so the results should be interpreted with caution.
PART 3 · PROPOSAL AND FEASIBILITY

Explain what can realistically happen

Propuesta mein problem, proposed action, resources, timeline, risks aur expected benefit hon. Conviene + infinitive recommendation deta hai; sería conveniente + infinitive softer suggestion. Cost aur staffing constraints hide na karein.

Se propone ampliar el horario dos tardes por semana durante un periodo de prueba.It is proposed that opening hours be extended on two afternoons a week during a trial period.
Para llevarlo a cabo, sería necesario formar a dos personas del equipo.To implement it, it would be necessary to train two team members.
PART 4 · FORMAL EMAIL OPENINGS AND REQUESTS

Address the reader appropriately

Formal correspondence mein Estimado señor / Estimada señora ya Estimados señores use ho sakta hai. Me pongo en contacto con ustedes para… purpose batata hai. Le agradecería que + subjunctive polite request structure hai.

Me pongo en contacto con ustedes para solicitar información sobre el procedimiento.I am contacting you to request information about the procedure.
Le agradecería que me enviara la documentación antes del día 15.I would be grateful if you could send me the documentation before the 15th.
PART 5 · REFERENCING ATTACHMENTS AND FOLLOW-UPS

Make the action explicit

Adjunto / Le remito / En relación con… documents refer karte hain. Agradecería confirmación receipt request karta hai. Follow-up mein previous date ya subject mention karein, magar accusatory tone avoid karein.

Le remito adjunta la propuesta revisada para su valoración.I am sending you the revised proposal attached for your consideration.
En relación con mi correo del lunes, quisiera saber si han podido revisar la solicitud.Regarding my email on Monday, I would like to know whether you have been able to review the request.
PART 6 · FORMAL CLOSINGS AND STYLE

Polish the final message

Atentamente, Un cordial saludo aur Quedo a su disposición common closings hain. Usted/ustedes register consistent rakhein; contractions, slang aur emotional exaggeration avoid karein. Subject line specific ho aur paragraph short rakhein.

Quedo a su disposición para ampliar cualquier información que consideren necesaria.I remain available to provide any further information you may need.
Agradeciendo de antemano su atención, les saluda atentamente.Thanking you in advance for your attention, yours sincerely.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a 220–260-word proposal for a realistic improvement at a school, workplace or community service. Include an executive summary, current problem, two evidence-based findings, costs or constraints, a recommendation and next steps. Add a short formal email introducing the proposal.
