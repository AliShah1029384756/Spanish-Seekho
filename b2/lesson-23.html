CEFR B2 · UNIT 06 · LESSON 23

Managing misunderstandings and difficult conversations

Communication breakdown ko repair karein: clarification maangein, apni baat rephrase karein, assumptions check karein aur emotionally difficult conversation ko respectful structure dein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Identify and clarify ambiguity without blaming the other speaker.
  • Repair communication through reformulation, checking and confirmation.
  • Manage disagreement or sensitive topics with calm, precise language.
PART 1 · NOTICE AND NAME THE MISUNDERSTANDING

Notice and name the misunderstanding

Pehle identify karein ke issue vocabulary, reference, timing ya assumption ka hai. Creo que ha habido un malentendido neutral hai; No era eso lo que quería decir apni intention clarify karta hai.

Creo que ha habido un malentendido sobre la fecha de entrega.I think there has been a misunderstanding about the delivery date.
No era eso lo que quería decir; me refería al presupuesto, no al plazo.That is not what I meant; I was referring to the budget, not the deadline.
PART 2 · ASK FOR CLARIFICATION

Ask for clarification

Open questions aur specific checks use karein: ¿A qué te refieres exactamente?, ¿Podrías concretar…? Guess karne ke bajaye missing information identify karein.

¿Podrías concretar qué parte del informe habría que modificar?Could you specify which part of the report needs to be changed?
Cuando dices «urgente», ¿te refieres a hoy o a esta misma mañana?When you say 'urgent', do you mean today or this very morning?
PART 3 · REFORMULATE AND CHECK UNDERSTANDING

Reformulate and check understanding

Paraphrase speaker ki baat ko apne alfaaz mein repeat karta hai. Si no te he entendido mal… aur Entonces, lo que propones es… confirmation invite karte hain; speaker ko correct karne ka chance dein.

Si no te he entendido mal, prefieres aplazar la decisión hasta tener más datos.If I have understood you correctly, you would prefer to postpone the decision until we have more data.
Entonces, lo que propones es reducir el alcance, pero mantener la fecha.So what you are proposing is to reduce the scope but keep the date.
PART 4 · HANDLE EMOTIONALLY CHARGED MOMENTS

Handle emotionally charged moments

Tone slow rakhein, facts aur impact separate karein, aur pause lein agar zaroori ho. Entiendo que esto te preocupe acknowledgement hai; No quiero que la conversación se convierta en… boundary set karta hai.

Entiendo que la situación te haya frustrado; vamos a revisar qué ha ocurrido.I understand that the situation has frustrated you; let us review what happened.
Prefiero que hablemos de los hechos sin atribuir intenciones.I would prefer us to discuss the facts without attributing intentions.
PART 5 · CORRECT MISINFORMATION RESPECTFULLY

Correct misinformation respectfully

Agar fact wrong ho to source ya evidence ke saath correct karein. Puede que haya una confusión con… gentle; Según el documento que recibimos… attribution deta hai. Public embarrassment avoid karein.

Puede que haya una confusión con la versión del documento que se aprobó.There may be some confusion about the version of the document that was approved.
Según el correo del lunes, el plazo acordado era el día quince.According to Monday's email, the agreed deadline was the fifteenth.
PART 6 · CLOSE WITH AN AGREED NEXT STEP

Close with an agreed next step

Difficult conversation tab productive hoti hai jab next action clear ho: confirm who does what, deadline aur follow-up. Para asegurarme de que estamos de acuerdo… summary introduce karta hai.

Para asegurarme de que estamos de acuerdo, enviaré un resumen con los tres puntos acordados.To make sure we agree, I will send a summary with the three points we agreed on.
Si surge alguna duda, la aclaramos antes de dar el siguiente paso.If any question comes up, we will clarify it before taking the next step.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a 220–260-word dialogue about a misunderstanding over a shared task. Include an open clarification question, one paraphrase check, an acknowledgement of emotion, a respectful correction supported by a source, and a final agreement with responsibilities and a date. Then record a 90-second spoken version.
