PART 2 · READING

Evaluate a short report

Una asociación local ha comparado dos formas de organizar sus talleres. El formato presencial facilita el intercambio espontáneo, mientras que la modalidad híbrida permite participar a personas que viven lejos. La encuesta interna indica una preferencia por el formato híbrido, aunque la muestra incluye principalmente a participantes habituales. El informe recomienda mantener ambas opciones durante un periodo de prueba y recoger opiniones de nuevos usuarios antes de tomar una decisión definitiva.

PART 3 · WRITING

Write a decision brief

Write 180–220 words. Summarise the proposal, assess one limitation, state your recommendation and define a review condition.

PART 4 · SPEAKING AND MEDIATION

Defend and explain your decision

Speak for 2–3 minutes: explain the issue, compare options, respond to one objection and state what should happen next. Then write a 70–90 word summary for someone who has not read the full report.

PART 5 · ONLINE INTERACTION

Take part in an asynchronous decision thread

Scenario: A community team is deciding whether to keep its workshops in person or offer a hybrid format. Imagine you are contributing to a shared online discussion. Write three connected turns in Spanish. Keep a respectful tone, respond to the actual point made by another participant and make your final recommendation clear. Drafts save on this device.

Useful phrases: «Entiendo tu punto de vista…», «¿Podrías aclarar…?», «Estoy de acuerdo en parte, aunque…», «A la luz de lo comentado…».

PART 6 · SPEAKING AND PRONUNCIATION RUBRIC

Review intelligibility, not accent imitation

Read your decision brief aloud for 2–3 minutes. If possible, record yourself locally or ask a teacher/partner to listen. This page does not record or automatically score your voice. The goal is clear, comfortable communication—not copying a particular regional accent.

If a teacher or partner gives feedback, note one concrete example. A self-rating is a reflection tool, not an external pronunciation grade.
