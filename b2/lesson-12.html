CEFR B2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 12

Complex relative, subordinate and concessive clauses

Longer sentences ko clear aur controlled banayein. Relative clauses, concession aur cause/result/purpose connectors se ideas ka relation precisely show karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Combine defining and non-defining relative clauses accurately.
  • Use cuyo, lo cual and preposition + relative pronoun.
  • Control concessive, causal, result and purpose clauses.
PART 1 · RELATIVE CLAUSES

Defining and non-defining information

Defining clause noun identify karta hai; commas nahi. Non-defining extra information deta hai aur commas se separate hota hai. Punctuation meaning ko change kar sakti hai.

Los documentos que faltan están en recepción.The documents that are missing are at reception.
La directora, que lleva diez años en el centro, presentó el proyecto.The director, who has worked at the centre for ten years, presented the project.
PART 2 · PREPOSITIONS

Preposition + relative pronoun

Preposition ke baad el que/la que/los que aur quien/quienes use ho sakte hain. Donde place refer karta hai. Formal writing mein yeh structures clarity dete hain.

La persona con la que hablé conoce bien el procedimiento.The person I spoke with knows the procedure well.
Este es el edificio en el que se celebrará la conferencia.This is the building in which the conference will be held.
PART 3 · CUYO AND LO CUAL

Possession and reference to a whole idea

Cuyo/cuya/cuyos/cuyas possession show karta hai aur following noun ke gender/number se agree karta hai. Lo cual previous clause ya idea ko formal taur par refer karta hai.

Conocimos a una investigadora cuyo trabajo ha tenido mucha repercusión.We met a researcher whose work has had a major impact.
Se canceló el servicio, lo cual provocó numerosas quejas.The service was cancelled, which caused numerous complaints.
PART 4 · CONCESSION

A pesar de que and pese a que

Known fact ke saath indicative; hypothetical concession ke saath subjunctive. Aunque bhi speaker ke viewpoint ke mutabiq mood change karta hai.

A pesar de que llovía, el acto se celebró al aire libre.Although it was raining, the event took place outdoors.
Pese a que haya dificultades, mantendremos el servicio.Even if there are difficulties, we will maintain the service.
PART 5 · CAUSE AND RESULT

Explain reasons and consequences

Dado que, puesto que aur ya que cause introduce karte hain. Por lo tanto/por lo que result show karte hain; de ahí que + subjunctive use hota hai.

Dado que faltan datos, aplazaremos la decisión.Since information is missing, we will postpone the decision.
La demanda ha aumentado, de ahí que sea necesario ampliar el horario.Demand has increased, hence the need to extend opening hours.
PART 6 · PURPOSE AND COHESION

Build readable complex sentences

Para que + subjunctive purpose express karta hai. Relative clause ko correct noun se attach karein, connector ka exact meaning choose karein aur unnecessary long chains avoid karein.

La propuesta incluye ejemplos para que el equipo pueda aplicarla.The proposal includes examples so that the team can implement it.
No se publicaron los resultados, por lo que todavía no podemos valorar el impacto.The results were not published, so we cannot yet assess the impact.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a 190–220-word formal paragraph about a local service or public project. Include a non-defining relative, preposition + relative pronoun, cuyo or lo cual, a concessive clause, a cause/result connector and a purpose clause. Check punctuation and clause attachment.
