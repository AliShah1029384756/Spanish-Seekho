CEFR B2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 11

Conditional structures, hypotheses and counterfactuals

Real possibilities se imagined situations aur past regrets tak. Si-clauses, mixed conditionals aur polite distance ko accurately use karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Build real, hypothetical and past counterfactual si-clauses.
  • Use mixed conditionals and conditional forms for polite advice.
  • Recognise conditional forms used for conjecture and reported future.
PART 1 · OPEN CONDITIONS

Real possibilities

Si + present indicative ke saath present, future ya imperative result aa sakta hai. Standard open condition mein si ke baad future simple nahi aata.

Si terminamos hoy, podremos enviar el informe mañana.If we finish today, we will be able to send the report tomorrow.
Si tienes alguna duda, ponte en contacto con el equipo.If you have any questions, contact the team.
PART 2 · HYPOTHETICAL PRESENT

Imagine a different situation

Si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional simple imagined ya unlikely present/future situation banata hai. Condition ko reality se distance par rakhta hai.

Si tuviera más tiempo, estudiaría otro idioma.If I had more time, I would study another language.
Si viviéramos más cerca, nos veríamos con más frecuencia.If we lived closer, we would see each other more often.
PART 3 · PAST COUNTERFACTUALS

What would have happened?

Si + hubiera/hubiese + participle, conditional perfect result ke saath past ka unreal alternative batata hai. Dono clauses past reality ke opposite scenario imagine karte hain.

Si hubiéramos reservado antes, habríamos conseguido mejores asientos.If we had booked earlier, we would have got better seats.
Si me lo hubieras dicho, te habría ayudado.If you had told me, I would have helped you.
PART 4 · MIXED CONDITIONALS

Past condition, present result

Past unreal condition ka present result ho sakta hai: si + pluperfect subjunctive, conditional simple. Present trait ka past result bhi context ke saath mumkin hai.

Si hubiera aceptado aquel trabajo, ahora viviría en Valencia.If I had accepted that job, I would be living in Valencia now.
Si fuera más organizado, no habría perdido aquella oportunidad.If I were more organised, I would not have missed that opportunity.
PART 5 · POLITENESS AND REPORTED FUTURE

Soften advice and report expectations

Deberías, podrías aur sería conveniente soften advice. Conditional past reporting point se future ko bhi show karta hai.

Yo que tú, pediría una segunda opinión.If I were you, I would ask for a second opinion.
Nos dijeron que el paquete llegaría al día siguiente.They told us the parcel would arrive the following day.
PART 6 · CONJECTURE

Probability and estimates

Conditional simple estimate ya conjecture express kar sakta hai, jaise past time ya quantity ka andaza. Is use ko si-clause se confuse na karein.

Serían las diez cuando terminó la reunión.It must have been around ten when the meeting ended.
Habría unas treinta personas en la sala.There must have been about thirty people in the room.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write three connected scenarios: a real condition, a hypothetical present/future and a past counterfactual. Add one mixed conditional and two polite recommendations. Explain the time relationship in each si-clause.
