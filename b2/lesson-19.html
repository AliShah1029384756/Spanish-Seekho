CEFR B2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 19

Culture, identity and intercultural perspectives

Culture aur identity ko stereotypes ke baghair discuss karein; lived experience, context, belonging, cultural change aur intercultural misunderstandings ko nuanced language dein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Discuss identity and cultural practices without treating groups as uniform.
  • Compare perspectives while recognising context and internal diversity.
  • Explain intercultural misunderstandings and propose respectful ways forward.
PART 1 · CULTURE IS NOT A SINGLE STORY

Avoid broad generalisations

Cultura static ya uniform nahi hoti. En algunos contextos, muchas personas… carefully qualified claim hai; no todo el mundo… exceptions acknowledge karta hai. Region, generation, class, language, family aur individual experience matter karte hain.

Las prácticas sociales varían según la región y la generación.Social practices vary according to region and generation.
No todas las personas de una misma comunidad comparten las mismas costumbres.Not everyone in the same community shares the same customs.
PART 2 · IDENTITY AND BELONGING

Describe identity as layered

Identidad personal, lingüística, cultural aur colectiva multiple layers hain. Sentirse parte de… belonging; identificarse con… affinity; estar influido por… influence. Identity ko fixed label ya single characteristic tak reduce na karein.

Muchas personas combinan distintas referencias culturales en su vida cotidiana.Many people combine different cultural reference points in everyday life.
El idioma puede influir en el sentimiento de pertenencia, aunque no lo determina por completo.Language can influence a sense of belonging, although it does not determine it completely.
PART 3 · COMPARING CULTURAL PRACTICES

Compare context, not superiority

A diferencia de… contrast; mientras que… parallel contrast; en ambos contextos… similarity. Compare karte waqt setting, purpose aur participants explain karein. “Better/worse” judgments ko evidence aur criteria ke baghair avoid karein.

En ambos contextos, las comidas familiares pueden cumplir una función social importante.In both contexts, family meals can play an important social role.
A diferencia de una celebración formal, una reunión cotidiana suele ser más espontánea.Unlike a formal celebration, an everyday gathering is usually more spontaneous.
PART 4 · CULTURAL CHANGE AND CONTINUITY

Recognise adaptation over time

Las costumbres han cambiado / siguen cambiando; se mantienen ciertos elementos; se han adaptado a… continuity aur change dono show karte hain. Migration, media, economy aur generations practices ko reshape kar sakte hain.

Algunas tradiciones se han adaptado a las nuevas formas de vida urbana.Some traditions have adapted to new forms of urban life.
Aunque cambian las celebraciones, ciertos elementos siguen teniendo un valor simbólico.Although celebrations change, certain elements continue to have symbolic value.
PART 5 · INTERCULTURAL MISUNDERSTANDINGS

Interpret before judging

Misunderstanding communication style, humour, silence, directness, time expectations ya non-verbal cues se aa sakti hai. Puede interpretarse como…; quizá no pretendía…; conviene preguntar antes de sacar conclusiones. Intent aur impact different ho sakte hain.

Un comentario directo puede interpretarse como una crítica, aunque esa no fuera la intención.A direct comment may be interpreted as criticism, even if that was not the intention.
Antes de sacar conclusiones, conviene preguntar qué quería expresar la otra persona.Before drawing conclusions, it is advisable to ask what the other person meant to express.
PART 6 · BUILDING INTERCULTURAL UNDERSTANDING

Use curiosity and mutual adjustment

Escucha activa, preguntas abiertas, reformulación aur willingness to adapt useful strategies hain. No doy por hecho que…; ¿cómo se suele hacer en tu contexto?; si te he entendido bien… Respectful curiosity ko exoticising ya intrusive questioning se distinguish karein.

No doy por hecho que todas las familias celebren esta fecha de la misma manera.I do not assume that all families celebrate this date in the same way.
¿Cómo se suele organizar esta actividad en tu contexto?How is this activity usually organised in your context?
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write a 220–260-word reflective comparison of two cultural settings you know. Discuss one shared practice and one difference, explain how context shapes them, include a personal observation without generalising, and finish with two practical suggestions for respectful intercultural communication.
