CEFR B2 · UNIT 02 · LESSON 07

Persuasion, claims and evidence

Audience aur purpose ke mutabiq persuasive language choose karein, claims ko evidence se support karein aur certainty ko accurately calibrate karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Adapt persuasive language to audience and purpose.
  • Use modal expressions to calibrate certainty and recommendation.
  • Distinguish evidence, interpretation and unsupported generalisation.
PART 1 · KNOW YOUR AUDIENCE

Persuasion begins with relevance

Audience ko samjhe baghair persuasive language effective nahi hoti. Public audience ke liye accessible wording; professional proposal mein concise evidence; formal request mein courteous register. Aapka aim inform, recommend, invite ya convince ho sakta hai.

Esta propuesta responde a una necesidad concreta del barrio.This proposal responds to a specific need in the neighbourhood.
Para el equipo, la principal ventaja sería reducir tareas repetitivas.For the team, the main advantage would be reducing repetitive tasks.
PART 2 · RECOMMENDATIONS AND MODALITY

Choose the strength of your recommendation

Conviene + infinitive, sería recomendable que + subjunctive, deberíamos + infinitive aur sería preferible que + subjunctive recommendation ki different strength dikhate hain. Zaroorat se zyada forceful tone resistance paida kar sakti hai.

Sería recomendable que el ayuntamiento ampliara el horario.It would be advisable for the council to extend the opening hours.
Conviene evaluar los resultados antes de ampliar el programa.It is advisable to evaluate the results before expanding the programme.
PART 3 · CALIBRATING CERTAINTY

Make your confidence match the evidence

Es probable que + subjunctive; es posible que + subjunctive; todo indica que + indicative; los datos sugieren que… Evidence ki strength ko reflect karte hain. “Está demostrado” tabhi use karein jab reliable evidence waqai establish karta ho.

Es posible que la medida mejore el acceso, aunque todavía no hay datos suficientes.It is possible that the measure improves access, although there is not enough data yet.
Todo indica que la demanda seguirá creciendo.Everything indicates that demand will continue to grow.
PART 4 · PERSUASIVE STRUCTURE

Problem → impact → proposal → benefit

Problem ko specific banayein, impact explain karein, practical proposal dein aur expected benefit batayein. Agar possible ho to cost, timeline aur limitation bhi mention karein. Persuasion ka matlab facts ko hide karna nahi.

La falta de transporte nocturno dificulta los desplazamientos; proponemos ampliar el servicio los fines de semana.The lack of night transport makes travel difficult; we propose extending the service at weekends.
La medida permitiría mejorar la seguridad y facilitar el acceso al trabajo.The measure would improve safety and make access to work easier.
PART 5 · RHETORICAL EMPHASIS

Emphasise without manipulating

Lo fundamental es…, lo que está en juego es… aur no se trata solo de…, sino de… focus create karte hain. Rhetorical question kabhi useful hota hai, lekin repeated dramatic claims ya false dilemmas se bachein.

No se trata solo de ahorrar tiempo, sino de ofrecer un servicio más fiable.It is not just about saving time, but about offering a more reliable service.
Lo fundamental es que la solución sea accesible para todos.The key point is that the solution should be accessible to everyone.
PART 6 · A CREDIBLE PERSUASIVE PITCH

Persuasive, specific and transparent

90-second pitch prepare karein: audience problem, evidence, proposal, benefit, limitation and next step. Writing: 180–210 words. Agar data hypothetical hai to clearly hypothetical label karein; fabricated source, quote ya statistic use na karein.

La propuesta merece una prueba piloto antes de tomar una decisión definitiva.The proposal deserves a pilot trial before a final decision is made.
Si los resultados son positivos, podríamos estudiar una ampliación gradual.If the results are positive, we could consider a gradual expansion.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Create a persuasive 90-second pitch for a realistic improvement at a school, workplace or local service. Include the problem, one supportable piece of evidence, a recommendation, calibrated certainty, one limitation and a practical next step. Write 180–210 words.

Self-review

Check that your position is clear, examples are relevant, connectors express the intended relationship, and certainty matches the evidence. Record yourself once, then repeat with clearer structure and more precise language.
