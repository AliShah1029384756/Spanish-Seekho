Upper-intermediate Spanish with nuance and confidence.

B2 par aap complex ideas samajhne, detailed viewpoints defend karne aur spontaneous interaction mein zyada fluency develop karte hain. Yeh pathway abhi develop ho raha hai; neeche pehle chhe units ke available lessons listed hain.

Is unit mein narrative perspective, past-time layers, conflicting accounts aur personal/historical/professional narratives par kaam hoga. B1 grammar ko sirf repeat nahi karna — ab usay precise meaning aur emphasis ke liye use karna hai.
