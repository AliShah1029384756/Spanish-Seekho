CEFR B2 · UNIT 05 · LESSON 18

Social change, technology and ethical questions

Technology aur social change par nuanced discussion karein; benefits, risks, stakeholders, conditions aur ethical trade-offs ko precise Spanish mein explain karein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Describe social and technological change with cause-and-effect language.
  • Evaluate benefits, risks and ethical trade-offs from multiple perspectives.
  • Qualify recommendations and defend a reasoned position.
PART 1 · DESCRIBING SOCIAL CHANGE

Explain trends and drivers

Se ha producido un cambio… trend ko frame karta hai; cada vez más… growing pattern; a raíz de… trigger/cause. Change ko single cause se explain na karein jab multiple social, economic aur institutional factors hon.

En los últimos años se ha producido un cambio notable en los hábitos de consumo.In recent years there has been a notable change in consumer habits.
Cada vez más empresas incorporan herramientas digitales a sus procesos.More and more companies are incorporating digital tools into their processes.
PART 2 · TECHNOLOGY: BENEFITS AND LIMITS

Evaluate what a tool can and cannot do

Puede facilitar, permite automatizar aur contribuye a… benefits batate hain. Sin embargo, depende de… limitations/conditions. Technology ko solution kehne se pehle access, training, reliability aur unintended effects assess karein.

La inteligencia artificial puede facilitar tareas repetitivas y liberar tiempo para otras actividades.Artificial intelligence can make repetitive tasks easier and free up time for other activities.
Su utilidad depende de la calidad de los datos y de la supervisión humana.Its usefulness depends on data quality and human oversight.
PART 3 · ETHICAL PRINCIPLES AND STAKEHOLDERS

Ask who benefits and who bears the risk

Privacidad, transparencia, responsabilidad, equidad aur consentimiento key ethical concepts hain. ¿Quién se beneficia? ¿Quién asume el riesgo? Un grupo ka benefit dusre group ke cost par to nahi? Stakeholders ko name karein.

La transparencia es esencial cuando una decisión automatizada afecta a las personas.Transparency is essential when an automated decision affects people.
También hay que tener en cuenta a quienes no disponen de acceso digital.We must also take into account those who do not have digital access.
PART 4 · CAUSE, CONSEQUENCE AND TRADE-OFF

Build a balanced analysis

Esto puede dar lugar a… consequence; de ahí que + subjunctive result/consequence; a costa de… trade-off. Correlation ko causation na samjhein aur short-term vs long-term effects alag karein.

La automatización puede aumentar la productividad, aunque también puede transformar determinados empleos.Automation can increase productivity, although it can also transform certain jobs.
De ahí que sea necesario invertir en formación y reciclaje profesional.That is why it is necessary to invest in training and professional reskilling.
PART 5 · QUALIFYING AN ETHICAL POSITION

Make conditions and limits explicit

No se trata de… sino de… false opposition ko refine karta hai. Siempre que + subjunctive condition; en la medida en que… degree/condition. Absolute statements ki bajaye safeguards aur exceptions define karein.

No se trata de prohibir la tecnología, sino de establecer límites transparentes.The issue is not to ban technology, but to establish transparent limits.
Su uso puede ser beneficioso siempre que exista supervisión y protección de datos.Its use can be beneficial provided that oversight and data protection are in place.
PART 6 · DEFENDING A RESPONSIBLE RECOMMENDATION

Connect principles to action

Recommendation mein target, safeguards, accountability aur review include karein. Conviene establecer…; sería razonable exigir…; la medida debería revisarse… Position strong ho sakti hai, magar reasons aur limits clear rakhein.

Sería razonable exigir una evaluación independiente de los sistemas de alto riesgo.It would be reasonable to require an independent assessment of high-risk systems.
La regulación debería revisarse a medida que evolucione la tecnología.Regulation should be reviewed as the technology evolves.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Prepare a 3-minute spoken argument and a 200-word written response to this question: ¿Debería regularse más el uso de la inteligencia artificial en la educación? Include two benefits, two risks, affected groups, one counterargument and a conditional recommendation. Use evidence where available and distinguish your opinion from established facts.
