CEFR B2 · UNIT 03 · LESSON 10

Indicative and subjunctive: contrasts and shifts in meaning

Same connector ke sath mood badalne se meaning kaise shift hota hai? Facts, possibilities, future time aur unknown referents mein speaker ka viewpoint samjhein.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Choose mood after aunque according to fact or hypothesis.
  • Use subjunctive with future time clauses.
  • Distinguish known referents from non-specific ones in relative clauses.
PART 1 · AUNQUE

Accepted fact or hypothesis

Aunque + indicative jab information fact ya accepted reality ho. Aunque + subjunctive jab hypothetical, unknown ya argument ke liye irrelevant ho. Dono forms grammatically correct ho sakti hain, meaning different hota hai.

Aunque el plazo es corto, podemos terminar el proyecto.Although the deadline is short, we can finish the project.
Aunque el plazo sea corto, intentaremos terminarlo.Even if the deadline is short, we will try to finish it.
PART 2 · FUTURE TIME

Cuando, en cuanto and hasta que

Cuando, en cuanto, hasta que aur tan pronto como future event ke liye subjunctive lete hain. Past ya habitual reference mein indicative use hota hai.

Cuando termine la reunión, te llamaré.When the meeting finishes, I will call you.
Cuando termina la reunión, suele enviar un resumen.When the meeting finishes, she usually sends a summary.
PART 3 · RELATIVE CLAUSES

Known and non-specific people

Known/existing person ko describe karte hue indicative. Unknown ya not-yet-found person ke liye subjunctive use hota hai; speaker kisi quality ki requirement batata hai.

Conozco a una técnica que habla japonés.I know a technician who speaks Japanese.
Buscamos a alguien que pueda empezar en enero.We are looking for someone who can start in January.
PART 4 · CONCESSION

Por mucho que and por más que

Yeh phrases aksar subjunctive ke saath non-specific resistance ya hypothetical effort dikhati hain. Known repeated fact ke saath indicative bhi mumkin hai; context decisive hai.

Por mucho que insistas, no cambiaré la decisión.No matter how much you insist, I will not change the decision.
Por más que lo intento, no consigo abrir el archivo.No matter how hard I try, I cannot open the file.
PART 5 · COMO SI

Unreal comparisons

Como si ke baad imperfect subjunctive ya pluperfect subjunctive aata hai. Present unreal comparison: conociera; past unreal comparison: hubiera conocido.

Habla como si conociera personalmente a todos.He speaks as if he knew everyone personally.
Reaccionó como si hubiera recibido una noticia terrible.He reacted as if he had received terrible news.
PART 6 · VIEWPOINT

Explain why the mood changes

Mood choose karne se pehle poochein: accepted fact hai, future event hai, hypothetical possibility hai ya non-specific target? Sirf connector yaad karna kaafi nahi.

Aunque sabemos que hay dificultades, seguiremos adelante.Although we know there are difficulties, we will carry on.
Aunque haya dificultades, seguiremos adelante.Even if there are difficulties, we will carry on.
PART 7 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Put the language to work

Write about a plan depending on future events. Include cuando + subjunctive, factual and hypothetical aunque clauses, one non-specific relative clause and como si. Explain each mood choice.
