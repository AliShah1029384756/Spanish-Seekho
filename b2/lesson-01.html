CEFR B2 · UNIT 01 · LESSON 01

Managing narrative perspective

Ek hi waqia ko mukhtalif angles se bayan karein: background, foreground, hindsight aur emphasis.

B2 upper-intermediateRoman Urdu supportSpain Spanish
LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end, you can…

  • Choose a narrative viewpoint and keep it clear.
  • Use tense and aspect to distinguish scene-setting from foreground events.
  • Shift focus deliberately without confusing the timeline.
PART 1 · NARRATIVE LENS

Who is telling the story — and from where?

B2 par sirf yeh nahi dekhna ke event kab hua; yeh bhi dekhna hai ke speaker kis angle se usay present kar raha hai. First-person account personal experience dikhata hai; third-person account distance ya broader view de sakta hai. Narrator baad mein samajh aane wali baat ko hindsight ke saath bhi explain kar sakta hai.

La sala estaba vacía cuando llegó el equipo.The room was empty when the team arrived.
El portavoz anunció la decisión y salió de la sala.The spokesperson announced the decision and left the room.
Durante meses preparaban el proyecto hasta que recibieron financiación.For months they were preparing the project until they received funding.
PART 2 · FOREGROUND AND BACKGROUND

Control what the listener notices

Imperfecto aksar scene, state ya ongoing process ko frame karta hai; indefinido foreground mein completed event ko aage barhata hai. Lekin B2 par yeh sirf formula nahi: narrator kisi completed action ko background detail bana sakta hai, ya kisi state ko event ki tarah frame kar sakta hai, depending on meaning.

Cuando empezó la sesión, ya habían revisado los datos.When the session began, they had already reviewed the data.
No había recibido el mensaje y por eso llegó tarde.She had not received the message and that is why she arrived late.
Con el tiempo comprendimos lo que había ocurrido.With time we understood what had happened.
PART 3 · LOOKING BACK

Pluscuamperfecto and retrospective explanation

Pluscuamperfecto (había + participio) narrator ko past ke andar ek aur pehle ke waqt par le jata hai. Is se listener ko samajh aata hai ke kisi event se pehle kya ho chuka tha. Hindsight phrases jaise ahora sé, con el tiempo comprendí aur visto en retrospectiva interpretation add karte hain.

Lo que más me llamó la atención fue su tranquilidad.What struck me most was his calmness.
Hasta entonces, nadie había planteado esa posibilidad.Until then, nobody had raised that possibility.
A partir de ese día, cambiamos el procedimiento.From that day onwards, we changed the procedure.
PART 4 · EMPHASIS AND REFRAMING

Move the focus without changing the facts

Word order, time expressions aur framing phrases se emphasis change hota hai. “Lo que más me sorprendió fue…” listener ko key point par focus karwata hai. “Hasta entonces” aur “a partir de ese momento” timeline ko clear karte hain.

El equipo ya había detectado el riesgo antes del lanzamiento.The team had already detected the risk before the launch.
La dirección aplazó el proyecto para evitar más errores.Management postponed the project to avoid further errors.
PART 5 · MODEL ACCOUNT

A decision reconsidered

Is short account mein notice karein: scene-setting, earlier event, main event aur later reflection alag roles play karte hain.

Production challenge

Speaking: 2-minute account of a decision or unexpected event. Pehle us waqt ke apne perspective se batayein; phir hindsight se explain karein ke ab kya samajh aata hai.

PART 6 · SPEAKING AND WRITING

Tell the same event from two perspectives

Speaking: 2-minute account of a decision or unexpected event. Pehle us waqt ke apne perspective se batayein; phir hindsight se explain karein ke ab kya samajh aata hai. Writing: 160–190 words. Include scene-setting, at least two earlier-than-past references, and one sentence that explicitly reframes the event.
